\begin{abstract}
How much autonomy does an agent retain under influence? We present a
white-box-first audit schema whose primary output is an instantiation-relative
channel profile, provenance record, and evidence grade, not a context-free
score. Five channels are gated by current resource viability; defense and
recovery readiness are reported separately rather than silently zeroing present
autonomy. L0 measures only internal-causal autonomy. At L1 and above, directed
information from normative states is separated from independent innovation
noise and external bypass flow, so a pure random register cannot receive a
maximal normative-autonomy reading.

A conditional representation theorem recovers the classical weighted-geometric
characterization under explicit calibration, invariance, and homogeneity
commitments. The main results concern attribution coverage, conditional strict
comparisons, retention margins, and identifiability. R15 repairs the execution
horizon to exactly $n$ actions, withdraws the claimed necessary-and-sufficient
T2 shortcut, and distinguishes structural point readings of goal alignment
from behavior-based IRL set identification. Explicit counterexamples delimit
compatibility, positivity, flow-ratio monotonicity, and attack optimization.
The endorsement factor $\Lambda$ is defined by actual/no-surgery output-law
total variation under abduction--action--prediction, separately from mandatory
surgery-history and source-registration fields. Nondegenerate observationally
equivalent SCMs prove that the specified passive protocol cannot identify it.

A legacy $4{,}620$-run campaign supplies implementation and same-model checks,
not external validation. New R15 negative controls cover the reviewer
counterexamples; earlier R14 X5-c computations remain model-sensitive and a
same-environment C0 witness remains a model construction. Failed criteria,
post hoc replacements, provenance gaps, and unrun work remain visible.
Independent-substrate validation, external SESOI/power analysis, and an
immutable R15 release/version DOI remain outstanding.
\end{abstract}

animals performed by any of the authors. The human-side content
(Section~\ref{sec:triangulation}) is qualitative triangulation over
