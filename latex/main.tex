\begin{abstract}
How much autonomy does an agent retain under influence? We present a
white-box-first audit schema with five channels behind a viability gate. Its
primary output is an instantiation-relative channel profile and evidence
grade, not a context-free autonomy score. A conditional representation
theorem recovers the classical weighted-geometric characterization under
explicit calibration, invariance, and homogeneity commitments; boundedness and
zero semantics are not claimed to prove ratio scales.

The main results concern attribution coverage, conditional strict
comparisons, retention margins, and identifiability. Explicit counterexamples
now delimit positivity, flow-ratio and goal-distance monotonicity, and
log-linear versus linear-fraction attack optimization. The endorsement factor
$\Lambda$ is defined solely by actual/no-surgery output-law total variation
under abduction--action--prediction, and is distinguished from surgery
history. Environment accuracy and direct-bypass resistance are likewise not
identified with source recognition or general origination. Nondegenerate
observationally equivalent SCMs prove that the specified passive protocol
cannot identify $\Lambda$; additional provenance or mechanism evidence is
required.

A legacy $4{,}620$-run campaign supplies definition checks, implementation
replays, and same-model demonstrations, not external validation or a tally of
confirmed theories. New R14 tests cover the counterexamples and the corrected
linear-fraction optimizer. A preregistered pooled X5-c implementation and a post hoc time-stratified
sensitivity supplement execute Gaussian and logistic counterfactual
computations; their point estimates are model-sensitive, so we report
two-model bands. A separate same-environment C0
witness preserves identical full-trajectory $(E^T,O^T,A^T)$ laws while separating epistemic accuracy
and $\Lambda$. Failed criteria, post hoc replacements, and unrun work remain
in the evidence ledger. Independent-substrate validation, external power
analysis, and a frozen public R14 release/version DOI remain outstanding.
\end{abstract}
