\begin{abstract}
How much autonomy does an agent retain under influence? We present a
white-box-first audit schema with two co-primary outputs: a current structural
channel profile and a historical formation profile. Neither is a context-free
score, and the historical profile is evidence rather than a scalar inferred
from current channels. Five current channels are gated by resource viability;
defense readiness is separate. L0 measures only internal-causal autonomy. At
L1+, ordered residual attribution separates normative, innovation, and external
flows; reverse-order and semantic-partition reports expose non-uniqueness.
Multi-step execution is restricted to precommitted open-loop action sequences:
a feedback-copy counterexample shows why ordinary reactive mutual information
cannot serve as execution capacity. Goal alignment distinguishes structural
fixed-point readings from behavior-IRL intervals, and a fixed-point implant
example shows why present self-endorsement does not certify goal origin.

A conditional representation theorem recovers the weighted-geometric form under
explicit calibration, invariance, and homogeneity commitments. It does not
establish ratio scales or causal attribution; log-damage shares are bookkeeping
under the selected aggregator. We provide conditional comparisons, retention
margins, identifiability boundaries, and exact weight-simplex sensitivity
bounds rather than treating a few alternative weights as a robustness proof.
The endorsement factor $\Lambda$ is an actual/no-surgery output-law deviation
with an explicit counterfactual time origin; audit-time removal and
original-implant prevention are different estimands.

A legacy $4{,}620$-run campaign supplies implementation and same-model checks.
A small native multi-step benchmark audits the current estimands from one
joint trajectory-log batch of a single registered SCM, with a separate
precommitted open-loop execution stream as the estimand requires, including
sampled surgery and action-decoupled negative controls. Layer-aware
certificates carry explicit channel states, separate point/interval modes,
coordinatewise interval propagation, and exact/logical-envelope/sampling kinds.
Above-limit composite intervals are \emph{share-conditional}, with other
channels fixed at points. A supplementary matched bootstrap compares
basic/percentile constructions and fixed/joint channel propagation on common
replicates; these are descriptive method contrasts, not a formal decomposition
of under-coverage into conditional-design and plug-in-bias effects. Envelope
containment is separate from sampling coverage. Data-driven near-zero detection
uses a bias-corrected bootstrap interval and below/above/indeterminate states,
never a truth-triggered annotation. The registered NRI verdict is
\emph{indeterminate} at $n=8{,}192$, with a conservative envelope and the
plug-in retained only as a diagnostic. Order and semantic sensitivity use
confirmed/indeterminate/not-evaluable statuses---identical wide intervals are
never read as agreement---under post hoc frozen internal thresholds. The exact RNI, NRI, and order-average composites
($0.4609$, $0.0004$, $0.4012$) are model anchors, not finite-sample
recoverability claims: multi-seed studies show plug-in bias amplified by the
geometric composite prevents recovery of the near-zero endpoint. Evidence
hashes bind immutable raw logs, not non-unique optimizer coordinates. The
contribution is a conditional conceptual and mathematical audit framework with
executable model checks, not a deployment-ready tool or cross-substrate
measurement standard. It establishes neither cross-substrate construct
validity, universal interval coverage, statistical consistency, nor external
validation of real-LLM or human autonomy. X5-c remains model-sensitive, T7
remains inconclusive, and failed or post hoc criteria remain visible.
Independent substrates, a domain-justified SESOI and power analysis,
independent-investigator replication, and external execution remain
outstanding; the engineering equivalence/non-inferiority margins are
design-frozen, and an archival version DOI is still pending.
\end{abstract}
