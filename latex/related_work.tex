\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related-work}

Our framework sits at the intersection of eight literatures that have
developed largely in isolation: philosophical theories of autonomy, cybernetic
and enactive accounts of agency, empirical psychology of self-regulation,
information-theoretic measures of control, AI-agent characterization and
governance, AI safety research on goal corruption, formal delegation and
adversarial allocation, and composite-indicator/scale-consistent aggregation.
This section surveys each, identifies the points of consensus and contention
that structure the field (\S\ref{sec:related-work}.1--.8), and states the gaps
our contribution fills (\S\ref{sec:related-work}.9). Throughout, we use the
five-channel vocabulary of Section~\ref{sec:framework} only informally; the
formal definitions are self-contained.

\subsection{Philosophical accounts of autonomy}\label{rw:philosophy}

% [MaM: keep full passage; Syn: may compress to first two + last sentence]
Modern analysis begins with hierarchical accounts. \citet{frankfurt1971}
grounds autonomy in the reflective structure of the will: an agent acts
autonomously when the desire that moves her is endorsed by a second-order
volition with which she identifies. \citet{watson1975} objects that a purely
hierarchical picture invites a regress---nothing guarantees that the endorsing
faculty is itself autonomous---and proposes instead that autonomy consists in
the governance of motivation by the agent's valuational system. Both accounts
are \emph{procedural}: they constrain how motives are formed and reviewed, not
what they contain. Proceduralism was refined by \citet{christman2004} into
conditions of ``potential reflective acceptability'' that avoid demanding
occurrent introspection.

The decisive challenge to proceduralism is the manipulation case: an agent
whose entire motivational structure, endorsement faculty included, was
installed by a designer passes every procedural test while intuitively lacking
autonomy. \citet{fischer_ravizza1998} respond with a \emph{historical}
condition---the mechanism issuing in action must be moderately
reasons-responsive \emph{and} the agent must have taken responsibility for it,
so that provenance, not merely present structure, matters. Whether history is
genuinely required remains contested (Section~\ref{sec:objections}); we return
to it in \S\ref{sec:axioms}, where it becomes Axiom~AX3 and, crucially, a
separate provenance and counterfactual audit requirement (AX3 and Theorem~T3).

A third strand, relational autonomy, rejects the atomistic picture shared by
both sides. \citet{mackenzie_stoljar2000} argue that the capacities
constitutive of autonomy---self-trust, self-esteem, the standing to avow
commitments---are socially scaffolded; \citet{westlund2009} adds that holding
oneself answerable to others is constitutive rather than derivative. On this
view relationships are the substrate of autonomy, not its principal threat.
Relational autonomy licenses the distinction, central to our intervention
taxonomy, between \emph{constitutive support} and \emph{reductive
interference}: the same causal coupling can raise or lower autonomy depending
on its functional role. Externalism supplies the final coordinate:
\citet{oshana1998} argues that autonomy is partly constituted by
socio-relational and independence conditions external to the agent's
psychology, against purely internalist procedural accounts. Our framework
assigns each camp its instruments rather than adjudicating between them: the
internalist clauses live in the origination and endorsability respects of
Definition~\ref{def:autonomy}, while the externalist conditions are carried
by the viability gate and the executability respect---no intact internal
processing constitutes autonomy on this scale when the resource floor or the
action lever is gone (the gate and execution disjuncts of Theorem~T5).
Finally, the ethics of manipulation supplies the
operative concept of autonomy-undermining influence in technological settings:
\citet{sussner2019} define online manipulation as the covert exploitation of
decision-making weaknesses, distinguished from persuasion (which offers
reasons) and coercion (which threatens). This tripartite distinction is the
direct ancestor of our $\alpha$/$\beta$/$\gamma$ intervention classes.

\subsection{Cybernetic, autopoietic, and enactive accounts}\label{rw:bio}

% [MaM: keep; Syn: may compress by half]
Ashby's law of requisite variety established the information-theoretic core of
self-maintenance: a regulator must match the variety of the disturbances it
absorbs \citep{ashby1956}. Autopoiesis theory generalized this to living
systems, defining autonomy as the operatively closed self-production of a
system whose boundary is maintained by its own dynamics
\citep{maturana_varela1980}. \citet{di_paolo2005} added \emph{adaptivity}---the
capacity to modulate activity when self-maintenance is endangered---thereby
making precariousness, rather than robustness, the mark of the autonomous.
\citet{barandiaran2009} distil the tradition into three conditions for
agency: individuation, interactional asymmetry (the system's influence on its
milieu exceeds the converse), and normativity or sense-making. These accounts
supply the substrate-neutral backbone of our framework---resource reproduction
as a gating channel ($A_{via}$), openness as an axiom (AX2)---but remain
qualitative: no estimator, no scale, no aggregation rule.

\subsection{Psychological measurement of autonomy and its loss}\label{rw:psych}

Self-determination theory provides the most developed empirical construct of
autonomy. \citet{ryan_deci2000} model motivation along a continuum of
internalization from external regulation to integrated self-endorsement;
autonomy-supportive contexts promote internalization while controlling
ones---extrinsic rewards, threats, imposed deadlines---undermine it. Two
findings are load-bearing: the continuum vindicates treating endorsement as a
matter of degree ($A_{goal}$), and the ``paradox of compelled autonomy''
(interventions aiming to produce autonomous behavior through controlling
means can backfire). Our restricted Theorem~T8 is a model-level consistency
result for direct goal implantation and action compression, not a derivation
of the empirical regularity; the $\sigma$-class (scaffolding) is a separate
modeling counterpart.

Learned helplessness supplies the paradigm case of execution-channel
collapse. \citet{maier_seligman2016} review fifty years of evidence, including
the neuroscientific reversal showing passivity is the default and control is
learned: after sufficient response--outcome independence, organisms cease to
exercise control \emph{even when it is restored}. Capability remains; the
action--outcome lever and the belief in it are gone---exactly the zero
semantics of $A_{exec}$ in Theorem~T5.

The measurement sciences of self-knowledge frame our reflection channel and
dual-track protocol. \citet{fleming_lau2014} separate metacognitive
\emph{sensitivity} from \emph{bias} (the estimator family for $A_{ref}$'s
fidelity term); \citet{moore_obhi2012} show the felt sense of agency is
measurable yet systematically dissociable from objective causal
contribution---the warrant for treating self-report as calibration data and
hallucination detector, never as evidence (cf.\ \citealp{lavazza2016} on
operationalizing free will). From dynamical systems psychology and ecology we
adopt early-warning signals for critical transitions \citep{lade_gross2012},
the template for Propositions~C1--C2.

\subsection{Information-theoretic and causal instruments}\label{rw:tools}

Each channel binds to an established instrument. Empowerment---capacity from
precommitted open-loop action sequences to future sensory input---is the
canonical intrinsic measure of control \citep{klyubin2005}, reviewed in
\citet{salge2013} and deployed as a robot-safety norm in
\citet{salge_polani2017}; as a joint agent--environment property it absorbs
the conversion-factor problem of resource-based execution measures.
\citet{jung2011continuous} extend estimation to continuous state spaces
(Monte Carlo, Gaussian-process dynamics) with a discrete action set: direct
precedent for learned execution estimates, not a solution to continuous-action
normalization. We deliberately do not use reactive-policy mutual information
as multi-step execution capacity: the feedback-copy counterexample
(Section~\ref{sec:channels}) shows it can count observation-to-action
correlation as control. Closed-loop directed information
\citep{massey1990}, its causally conditioned network extension
\citep{kramer2003}, and transfer entropy \citep{schreiber2000} provide the
source-attribution machinery; Massey's Theorem~1 (directed $\le$ mutual
information, equality iff no feedback) is background, not evidence for T2's
architecture-specific claims. \citet{abel2025plasticity} generalize directed
information to arbitrary windows (GDI), define plasticity as
observation-to-action influence, and recover empowerment with the direction
reversed---a formal mirror; our question is which registered normative or
external source drives behavior in a fixed instantiation, and adaptation to
observations can be legitimate, so plasticity is not autonomy loss.

The information-theoretic study of autonomy has a direct lineage our
origination channel continues: informational closure
\citep{bertschinger2008}, information-theoretic individuality
\citep{krakauer2020}, the causal-network balance of internally determined
versus externally driven flows \citep{ay_polani2008}---structurally the
distinction \eqref{eq:aorg} makes quantitative with causally conditioned
directed information---and G-autonomy \citep{seth2010} (own-past predictive
improvement via Granger causality), the closest predictive-time-series
precursor. Granger predictive self-determination is not causally conditioned
directed information from a registered normative state, and supplies neither
the endorsement/history distinction nor the attribution cover; we treat it as
prior work on measuring autonomy, not evidence for our mediation theorem.

Two closer studies sharpen the lineage and the validation burden.
\citet{ingel2022quantifying} apply partial information decomposition (PID) to
the \emph{next internal state} $S_{t+1}$: their conditional self-determination
term combines unique internal with internal--environment complementary
information, answering how sources jointly update memory---not our
behavior-directed, order-registered chain $\Frand+\Fnorm+\Fext$ asking which
registered source produces actions. Their RL experiments show task
performance and autonomy need not move together (precedent for perturbation
audits and candidate external substrates, not validation of our channels).
\citet{albantakis2021quantifying} compare structural, information-theoretic,
causal, and dynamical autonomy candidates on structurally diverse Markov
Brains, finding implementation- and measure-dependent dissociations: strong
precedent for multi-instrument profiles and inspecting causal structure
rather than inferring autonomy from successful behavior, but not a one-to-one
anticipation of our five channels (no constitutive self-maintenance,
endorsement history, or reflection estimand). Their automata and Ingel's RL
environments are the minimum substrates of the design-frozen, unexecuted
protocol \texttt{AFC-EXT-001}; no such comparison has been run.

These approaches do not supply our goal/execution/epistemic/reflection
decomposition, intervention taxonomy, historical clause, or aggregation
theory. On the incentive side, power-seeking analysis
\citep{turner2021}---optimal policies tend toward high-option-value states---is
the decision-theoretic shadow of our T4 vertex result and $\Aexec$'s
empowerment semantics: complementary programs (what optimizers seek vs.\ what
an influence takes away). Maximum-entropy IRL recovers effective goals from
behavior \citep{ziebart2008}, yielding the $U_{eff}$ side of $A_{goal}$;
estimation at scale is covered by variational empowerment
\citep{mohamed2015} and $k$-NN/neural MI estimators
\citep{kraskov2004,belghazi2018}. For language agents, EELMA
\citep{song2026eelma} maps multi-turn text trajectories into pretrained
embeddings and estimates effective empowerment via two InfoNCE objectives:
scalable estimation of an action--future-state influence quantity, not our
source attribution or counterfactual endorsement deviation; its
task-performance correlations validate EELMA's capability proxy, not our
$\Aexec$ or composite. Causal emergence \citep{hoel2013} is the closest
information-theoretic cousin of the origination ratio (where in the causal
stack the effective source lives); Bayesian persuasion \citep{kamenica2011}
is the game-theoretic counterpart of the $\alpha$ class (steering through the
receiver's own intact channel)---mediation alone does not prove compatibility,
which requires the full T2 certificate. Our intervention semantics is that of
do-calculus and independent causal mechanisms \citep{peters2017}; belief-state
POMDPs \citep{kaelbling1998} model the epistemic constraints under which
self-estimates drift (T3b); change-point detection \citep{truong2020} supplies
the sequential tools for T6's detection-only control, whose retention margin
is a joint registration/repair/redundancy statement, not a distribution-free
claim (Proposition~\ref{prop:detection} is limited to fixed-drift Gaussian
mean shifts as $\alpha\downarrow0$).

The free energy principle \citep{friston2010,friston2023} would ground
self-maintenance from first principles, but we deliberately do not build on
it: Markov blanket structure is non-unique and interest-relative
\citep{bruineberg2022}, so a blanket cannot deliver the \emph{normative} self
autonomy ascriptions require; we take boundaries as given and reserve a
blanket interface for future unification. Integrated information theory
\citep{oizumi2014,tegmark2016} is the closest methodological precedent
(axioms about causal structure pushed into measures), but targets phenomenal
consciousness.

\subsection{Characterizing and governing AI agents}\label{rw:ai-agents}

The 2024--2026 literature converges, from independent directions, on
multidimensional gradations of AI agency. \citet{dung2024} proposes a
threshold conception (goal-directedness, autonomy, efficacy, long-term
planning, acting for reasons); current LLMs fail the profile, but
architectural compositions \citep[e.g.][]{park2023} point forward. The survey
literature systematizes the engineering paradigm---LLM cores with planning,
memory, tool-use, and action modules \citep{wang2023,xi2023}---whose
component-wise attack surfaces are catalogued by \citet{su2025}.
\citet{lassiter2026compendium} organize criteria, metrics, and benchmarks
around five agenticness dimensions (environmental interaction, learning and
adaptation, autonomy, goal-directed behavior, temporal coherence) with the
public Agent Compendium: multidimensional definition and an evaluation
catalogue are established prior art, not our increment.
\citet{kasirzadeh2025} characterize agents along autonomy, efficacy, goal
complexity, and generality, feeding ``agentic profiles'' for differentiated
governance; \citet{feng2025} define five autonomy levels indexed by the user's
role (operator, collaborator, consultant, approver, observer), argue autonomy
level is a design decision separable from capability and environment, and
propose autonomy certificates. At the implementation layer, \citet{cihon2025}
score orchestration code by impact and oversight without task evaluations
(AutoGen and ten applications): direct prior art for code inspection, not an
estimate of our channels or endorsement history. The human-factors
level-of-autonomy (LOA) tradition predates these schemes by four decades and
is the most direct prior art on \emph{grading} autonomy:
Sheridan--Verplank's ten-level control-allocation scale \citep{sheridan1978}
and the Parasuraman--Sheridan--Wickens types-and-levels model
\citep{parasuraman2000} are adopted engineering practice with calibrated
usability consequences. Three differences locate our contribution: LOA grades
\emph{task allocation} at design time, we measure \emph{retained autonomy
under influence} at run time; LOA levels are ordinal labels without
estimators, each of our channels carries one; LOA settings admit
\emph{heuristic explanatory crosswalks} into cells of the delegation pair
$(\lambda,q)$ of T7---Feng's user-role labels neither uniquely recover
$(\lambda,q)$ nor imply general rank consistency with our state descriptor
(the separation witness of Proposition~\ref{prop:c0}(ii) is distinct from any
general Feng rank claim). Formal agency criteria \citep{kenton2023} and
induced preference shifts in recommender systems \citep{carroll2022} are the
closest agent-side and user-side formalizations. Governance now separates
capability, permission, and autonomy level explicitly
\citep{capability_permission2026}, while \citet{xing2026} challenge the agent
construct itself (identity and self-regulation dimensions missing from
engineering accounts). Deployment measurement is also established:
Anthropic's 18 February 2026 study analyzes millions of real Claude Code and
public-API interactions (turn duration, auto-approval, interruption,
agent-initiated clarification stops) \citep{anthropic2026agents}; experienced
users both auto-approve and interrupt more often---a change in oversight
strategy, not its disappearance. These are deployment measurements, not
estimates of our channels or external validation of this paper.
On audit frameworks, \citet{nian2026auditable} distinguish accountability,
auditability, and auditing, and specify five evidence dimensions (action
recoverability, lifecycle coverage, policy checkability, responsibility
attribution, evidence integrity) with detect--enforce--recover experiments:
a shared evidence schema before our account. NIST's AI RMF offers
\emph{voluntary guidance} on documentation, traceability, and audit processes,
not a deployment mandate \citep{nist2023}. Our narrower provenance corollary
(Corollary~\ref{cor:logs-necessary}) explains why behavioral traces alone
cannot certify historical endorsement and presents structural logs as one
audit route among several. Related positions include the dynamical threshold
account of \citet{vanlier2024} and the delegation-side analyses of
\citet{danaher2018} (AI assistants and the achievement gap) and
\citet{coeckelbergh2023} (AI threats to citizens' epistemic agency).

Our contribution relative to this direct literature is deliberately narrow.
Code inspection, deployment telemetry, empowerment estimation, evaluation
catalogues, and audit evidence schemas already exist. What they do not jointly
supply is my particular fixed-instantiation decomposition of direct-bypass,
goal, execution, epistemic-accuracy, and reflection instruments, together with
an explicit actual/no-surgery endorsement estimand and conditional proofs of
attribution and passive non-identifiability. Proposition~\ref{prop:c0} gives
only restricted explanatory mappings and a same-environment separation
witness, not a claim that earlier frameworks cannot distinguish manipulation
or that my mappings are unique. The scalar geometric form is a classical
conditional representation, not the innovation. Nor does the framework make
$A_{epi}$ a source-recognition measure or $A_{org}$ a complete origin metric.
The remaining validation burden is precisely external: independent substrates,
expert and downstream criteria, and comparison against these established
alternatives.

\subsection{AI safety as the study of autonomy loss}\label{rw:safety}

The safety literature has accumulated, case by case, exactly the failure
modes our taxonomy organizes. Reward tampering is analyzed in
causal-influence-diagram terms parallel to our $\beta$-class semantics
\citep{amodei2016,pan2022,everitt2021}; goal misgeneralization shows capability
and goal coming apart \citep{langosco2022,shah2022}, unified under proxy
alignment by \citet{ngo2022}. Mesa-optimization exhibits the manipulation case
as engineering reality: an inner optimizer can be selected for \emph{deceptive}
alignment, passing every behavioral endorsement test while its goals were
installed from outside \citep{hubinger2019}. Goal drift in deployed LM agents
\citep{arike2025} is the empirical counterpart of T6's slow sub-threshold
drift; sycophancy \citep{sharma2023} is a qualitative analogue of $\AHI>0$,
not a validation. Prompt injection \citep{greshake2023} and self-propagating
agent worms \citep{cohen2024} show observation-channel inputs producing
action-level hijack---the $\alpha$-masquerading-as-$\beta$ case T2's mediation
criterion separates from persuasion (susceptibility measured by
\citealp{andriushchenko2024}); dark patterns at web scale \citep{nouwens2020}
are $\gamma$-class channel manipulation of human agents. Defensively,
corrigibility \citep{soares2015} is the technical core of our delegation
quality $q$; safe interruptibility \citep{orseau2016} is the $\delta$-class
exit-option condition seen from the designer's side (a property of the
agent's $\Aexec$ environment, not of its autonomy); constitutional AI
instantiates an explicit second-order review loop \citep{bai2022}, and LM
self-models are learnable yet corruptible \citep{binder2024}, supporting both
the feasibility of $A_{ref}$ in silicon and its confinement to the calibration
track. The conceptual map of value alignment \citep{gabriel2020} frames the
normative pluralism we quarantine into the application layer.

What the safety corpus lacks is a shared audit schema: each failure mode is
studied in its own vocabulary, making instantiation-relative autonomy-loss
comparisons difficult, leaving defense allocation without a common objective
(T4), and obscuring whether a retention margin can be stated (T6).

\subsection{Delegation, incentives, and adversarial allocation}\label{rw:delegation}

Three further literatures formalize pieces of the delegation and
attack--defense geometry that our theorems treat, and the connections deserve
explicit statement. \emph{Assistance games and cooperative inverse
reinforcement learning} \citep{hadfieldmenell2016} give the game-theoretic
formalization of aligned delegation: the principal's behavior is evidence
about the true objective, and the agent's optimal policy incorporates that
evidence. Our delegation pair $(\lambda,q)$ of Theorem~T7 measures the same
relationship from the audit side: $q=1$ delegation (transparency,
reversibility, and endorsability all maximal) has the structural mediation
of $\alpha$-class influence (T7(iv-a)), but full losslessness additionally
requires honest, accuracy-preserving content and sound updating (T7(iv-b)).
This conditional audit result complements, rather than reproduces, CIRL's
incentive analysis, while a $\beta_G$ implant is precisely the failure mode in
which the ``principal's'' signal is adversarial rather than informative about
the true objective. \emph{Contract theory} \citep{laffont_martimort2002}
prices delegation through incentive-compatibility and monitoring constraints;
the ungoverned share $m=\lambda(1-q)$ is instead an information-theoretic
measure of what delegation costs in terms of \emph{whose normative structure
drives behavior}---non-welfarist, and complementary to the utility-transfer
accounting of the principal--agent tradition. \emph{Security resource
allocation} has its own canonical model: the Colonel~Blotto game
\citep{roberson2006}, in which two sides simultaneously allocate budgets
across battlefields and equilibria are mixed. T4's vertex concentration with
water-filling defense differs structurally: it is a one-sided comparative
static of attack effort against a fixed defense under log-linear damage, so
vertex concentration is a property of the linear objective rather than an
equilibrium phenomenon, and the defender's equalization is the min-max
response. The two models carve different slices of the attack--defense
problem---Blotto: symmetric simultaneous allocation; T4: asymmetric
sequential audit---and where they overlap they are consistent.

\subsection{Composite indicators and scale-consistent aggregation}\label{rw:composite}

Multidimensional measurement faces a standing objection: aggregation is
arbitrary. The composite-indicator literature answers it. The OECD/JRC
handbook makes scale-type discipline mandatory: admissible aggregation is
constrained by the sub-indicators' measurement scales, and for strictly
positive ratio-scale components geometric aggregation is scale-consistent---a
characterization due to \citet{ebert_welsch2004} and inherited by the handbook
\citep{oecd2008}. Our representation theorem's scale-invariance proof is the
bounded-cube, order-invariance form of that classical characterization; our
channels are not asserted to satisfy those premises by construction. What we
add is the normalization-explicit minimality analysis (zero annihilation and
biseparability as consequences), the harmonic and multiplicative-constant
counterexamples isolating invariance and calibration, and the audit embedding
(gate, weight protocols, falsification windows, sensitivity obligations)---not
the product form itself (Section~\ref{sec:representation}). The
non-compensability debate has named opponents: the Mazziotta--Pareto index
penalizes unbalanced profiles while staying compensatory in form
\citep{mazziotta2016}, and benefit-of-the-doubt/DEA weighting lets the data
choose the weights \citep{cherchye2007}; both reject the zero-annihilation
stance we adopt. Non-compensability is therefore a conscious commitment
against an articulated alternative, discharged through sensitivity
obligations rather than assumed; within our axiom set it is carried by
calibration, strict monotonicity, coordinatewise scale invariance (AX6b), and
homogeneity, from which zero annihilation (AX5) follows
(Remark~\ref{rem:routeb-minimal}), with sensitivity reported across the
axiom-relaxation bands ($\varsigma\in[0.5,1)$ relaxing AX6b, $\varsigma=2$
relaxing AX5, on equal footing; Section~\ref{sec:experiments}).

The measurement-theoretic foundation is the Krantz--Luce--Suppes--Tversky
theory \citep{krantz1971}: admissible transformations are fixed by scale type,
and an aggregation form is meaningful only if invariant under them. The same
normative burden our AX5 carries has been fought most explicitly in
multidimensional poverty measurement, where non-compensatory zero-annihilating
aggregation is a conscious axiomatic stance with standard costs
\citep{alkire2011}; we adopt it with the same explicitness.

\subsection{Summary of gaps and scoped increment}\label{rw:gaps}

The direct comparison changes the novelty claim. Philosophical accounts
motivate endorsement and provenance but stop before executable estimators;
Cihon et al.\ supply design-time code inspection, Anthropic deployment
oversight measurements, Abel et al.\ a bidirectional plasticity--empowerment
formalism, EELMA text-trajectory empowerment estimation, the Agent Compendium
an evaluation taxonomy, and Auditable Agents evidence-quality dimensions. Our
increment is the \emph{combination}, at a fixed instantiation, of (i)
intervention-attributed channels and direct-bypass information bookkeeping,
(ii) a distinct actual/no-surgery endorsement output-law estimand, (iii)
conditional representation and identifiability results, and (iv) an evidence
ledger separating analytic, implementation, model, and external tests---not
the first geometric mean, empowerment estimator, code audit, deployment
measurement, or audit schema. Construct validity remains open:
independent-substrate and external-criterion comparisons are pending, C0's
mappings are restricted explanatory crosswalks, and scalar readings are
calibration-relative, not demonstrated universal ratio measurements.
