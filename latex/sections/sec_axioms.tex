\section{Axioms and Channel Measures}\label{sec:axioms}

The axiom set comes in two orders: agent-level axioms (AX0--AX3) constrain the
model $\Gcal$ and its laws; aggregation-level axioms (AX4--AX8) constrain the
aggregation function $F$ that combines the channels
(Section~\ref{sec:representation}). The one-to-two correspondence between the
axioms and the representation theorem's conditions is stated with the theorem
(Definition~\ref{def:a16}): \textup{(A0)} is the common-unit calibration
registered by AX6a, \textup{(A1)}$+$ strict monotonicity is AX4,
\textup{(A3)} is AX7, \textup{(A4)} is AX5, \textup{(A5)} is AX8, and
\textup{(A6)} is AX6b.

\paragraph{Vocabulary.} ``Register'' is used in three graded senses: (i)
state a definition or assumption; (ii) flag a design choice or limitation for
the audit record; (iii) record a falsification window or reporting
obligation. ``Registered'' never means derived; where a claim is derived, we
say \emph{proved}.

\subsection{Agent-level axioms}

\begin{axiom}{AX0}[Layer-specific agency]\label{ax:0}
There exist \emph{active edges}: the conditional laws $\pi_t(\cdot\given s)$
differ pairwise in TV by $\ge\eta>0$ on a positive-measure set of states, and
the prior on the registered decision state has full support. At L0 this yields
$I(S;A)>0$ and licenses only internal-causal agency. At L1+ the corresponding
requirement is active variation with the normative state $N=(G,B,M)$ after
causal conditioning on registered innovation noise $R$, so a nondegenerate
normative audit requires $\Fnorm>0$. Pure independent-register randomness can
satisfy L0 AX0 but fails the L1+ normative-source requirement and receives
zero, rather than one, in \eqref{eq:aorgnorm}. The uniform margin
$\eta>0$ excludes arbitrarily flat policy families; active-edge formulations,
rather than an unsupported full-support assumption, are definitive.
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}{AX1}[Boundedness]\label{ax:1}
$H(S)<\infty$, verification rate $v<\infty$, memory bounded. Finite physical
embedding; it guarantees all information quantities are finite and no
$0/0$-type pathologies of infinities occur.
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}{AX2}[Openness]\label{ax:2}
Adaptation requires $I(O;E)>0$; isolation collapses adaptability (requisite
variety; \citealp{ashby1956}). This axiom rejects the equation of autonomy
with isolation. (The condition is the necessary-information-intake form of
requisite variety, not its sufficient adaptive-fit form: $I(O;E)>0$ is
compatible with pure noise, and the stronger Ashby statement is not claimed.)
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}{AX3}[Co-primary current structure and historical formation]
\label{ax:3}
The audit has two co-primary outputs. The \emph{current structural profile}
$\Ccur$ contains the present channel profile, viability gate, and separate
defense readiness. The \emph{historical formation profile} $\Hform$ contains
surgery-history evidence, source registration, and, where an endorsement SCM
is instantiated, a counterfactual output-law deviation with an explicit time
origin. $\Hform$ is an evidence profile, not a scalar computed from $\Ccur$.
A high current reading is not a certificate that the current structure was
autonomously formed.

Let $I^{\mathrm{surg},T}$ record registered surgeries and
$\mathcal H^{surg}_T:=\mathbf 1\{I^{\mathrm{surg},T}\neq\varnothing\}$.
For endorsement output deviation, instantiate an SCM with explicit exogenous
noise and audit time $t_a$. Abduction determines a context $C_t=c$. The
default \emph{audit-time removal} action removes currently registered active
surgeries while leaving already-formed downstream structure intact; prediction
compares actual and no-surgery endorsement laws with the same abducted noise:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lambda}
\Lam_T^{\mathrm{audit}}:=1-\frac1T\sum_{t=1}^T\mathbb E_{C_t}\!\left[
\TV\!\left(P_{t,C_t},P_{t,C_t}^{\varnothing,\mathrm{audit}}\right)\right]\in[0,1].
\end{equation}
A different estimand, \emph{original-implant prevention}, intervenes at the
formation time $\tau_0$ and recomputes all descendants. It is not obtained by
relabelling \eqref{eq:lambda}. Certificates must record \path{lambda_time_origin} and
\path{lambda_counterfactual_scope}; the two values are not interchangeable.

For the XOR fixture, the factual context is $C_t=(Y_t,J_t)$,
$P_{t,(y,j)}=\delta_y$ and $P_{t,(y,j)}^{\varnothing}=\delta_{y\oplus j}$.
The threshold fixture instead abducts $(X_t,Z_t,J_t)$ without conditioning on
the endorsement decision. Each protocol must state its own context and two
conditional output laws. Empty active surgery gives $\Lam=1$; nonempty surgery
can also give $\Lam=1$ if outputs are unchanged. $\Lam=0$ means maximal average
output-law deviation, not merely a polluted history.

\emph{Fixed-point implant example.} Let $\Fix=\{U^0,U^1\}$ with $U^0\neq U^1$
and $\dc>0$. An external implant replaces $U^0$ by $U^1$ inside the registered
normative state $N$. Both are fixed points, so $\Agoal=1$ before and after;
the update can have zero residual external bypass flow. The current profile
may therefore be unchanged while $\Hform$ records a registered formation
surgery. Audit-time removal may leave the already-formed $U^1$ and give
$\Lam^{\mathrm{audit}}=1$, whereas preventing the original implant can change
the output law. This is not a contradiction: present self-endorsement and
historical origin are different audit objects.

\emph{Composite scope.} $\Lam$ enters the current scalar only through
$\Lam_{ref}$ at L2. At L0/L1 it is reportable but composite-silent when an
auxiliary SCM exists, and otherwise N/A. Computation requires causal evidence
and an SCM supporting abduction--action--prediction; logs alone do not identify
it. Passive behavioral non-identifiability is proved in
Proposition~\ref{prop:unidentifiable}.
\end{axiom}

AX3 separates two operational counterparts of the historical condition on
autonomy \citep{fischer_ravizza1998}: formation evidence and endorsement
output-law counterfactuals. Manipulation cases that pass every present
procedural test require provenance or mechanism evidence; neither present
structure nor an intervention label alone settles them.

\paragraph{The MI proxy and legacy marker boundary.}
The quantity
\[
\widehat\Lam^{\mathrm{MI}}_T:=1-I(\Endorse^T;I^{\mathrm{surg},T})/H(\Endorse^T)
\]
can at most be a descriptive dependence summary. Statistical independence does not identify
the path-specific counterfactual in \eqref{eq:lambda}, and conditional mutual
information can increase, so the earlier (V)/(A) ``certified proxy'' claim is
withdrawn. The legacy ENV-0 \texttt{law\_level\_lambda} values ($1-\eta$,
$1-s(\gamma)$, or a policy-flag share) are scenario markers, not evaluations
of \eqref{eq:lambda}. They remain in read-only historical bundles but cannot
validate AX3, ATK-1, $\beta_\pi$, $A_{ref}$, T2/T3, or C0. The separate
SCM fixture implements abduction--action--prediction and two noise models;
its evidence is restricted to that fixture and does not upgrade those legacy
results.

\subsection{Aggregation-level axioms}

\begin{axiom}{AX4}[Positive substitutability and regularity]\label{ax:4}
$F$ is continuous and strictly increasing in each coordinate on $(0,1]^n$:
improving any channel strictly improves the whole (the precise content of
``redundancy helps''), and arbitrarily small measurement error in the
channels induces arbitrarily small error in the composite. The strict
monotonicity excludes Leontief/min aggregation; the continuity is the
regularity commitment (measurement stability) that the representation
theorem's (A1) records.
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}{AX5}[Zero annihilation / conjunctive necessity]\label{ax:5}
$F$ extends continuously to $[0,1]^n$ with $F(x)=0\iff\min_i x_i=0$: any
channel at zero annihilates autonomy, and positive values are never
annihilated. This excludes arithmetic aggregation. It is retained as a named
axiom because the non-compensability debate takes it as the named object;
the representation proof shows it is implied by AX4, AX6b and AX8 rather
than independent of them once the common-unit calibration (A0) is included
(Remark~\ref{rem:routeb-minimal}).
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}{AX6}[Normalized coordinates and scale commitments]
\label{ax:6}
Two parts. \emph{AX6a (component level)}: each channel is a bounded,
protocol-normalized coordinate with an explicit zero and normalizer
($\Fsrc+\Foth$, $n\log|\Acal|$ for exactly $n$ precommitted open-loop actions, $\dc$, the TV upper bound $1$,
$H(\theta_\pi)$ for a finite discrete ensemble, or a physical supply ratio).
These facts define range and zero semantics and support calibration
$F(1,\ldots,1)=1$ (A0). They do \emph{not} prove that each coordinate is a
measurement-theoretic ratio scale. Ratio readings such as ``twice as much
autonomy'' are separate normative interpretations requiring independent
justification; normalized endpoints and scale-free ratios are insufficient.
\emph{AX6b (aggregation level)}: the weak order induced by $F$ is invariant
under coordinatewise positive rescaling:
$F(x)\ge F(y)\iff F(\lambda\odot x)\ge F(\lambda\odot y)$ for all
$\lambda\in(0,1]^n$ (in the sense of Ebert--Welsch; \citealp{oecd2008}).
This is a substantive robustness commitment of the audit, not a theorem that
coordinate rescaling is always an admissible measurement transformation. It is
also the condition that excludes the harmonic family
(Proposition~\ref{prop:harmonic}).
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}{AX7}[Compositionality / biseparability]\label{ax:7}
For every non-trivial partition $\{K,L\}$ of the channels there exist a
continuous increasing coupling $G_{KL}$ and same-kind sub-aggregations
$F_K,F_L$ with $F(x)=G_{KL}(F_K(x_K),F_L(x_L))$: the order of subgroup
aggregation does not matter. The audit content: channel-group reports (e.g.\
a ``goal--reflection'' subscore) are consistent with the whole regardless of
grouping order---a compositionality requirement that any certificate built
from subscores must satisfy. (Representation-theoretically this is condition
(A3). Like AX5, it is a consequence of the calibrated invariance set rather
than an independent commitment; Remark~\ref{rem:routeb-minimal}.)
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}{AX8}[Proportionality / homogeneity]\label{ax:8}
$F$ is homogeneous of degree one: $F(tx)=t\,F(x)$ whenever $tx\in(0,1]^n$. The
commitment: if every channel degrades by the same percentage, autonomy
degrades by that percentage---a proportionality discipline linking the
composite to the channels' stipulated ratio interpretations, not a proof
that those interpretations hold. This is the strongest of the
aggregation axioms and the least intuitive; we flag two of its properties
honestly: it carries part of the exclusion load alongside AX6b in
Theorem~\ref{thm:rep} and in the separate CES-family screening, and it is a
\emph{local} property on the bounded cube
(the homogeneous extension to $\mathbb{R}_{++}^n$ is constructed and used in
Appendix~\ref{app:representation}). Non-homogeneous monotone transforms of
the product, such as $F(x)=(1+\prod_ix_i^{w_i})/2$, preserve calibration,
strict monotonicity, and order invariance but violate AX8; they illustrate
the registered relaxation direction if proportionality is rejected.
(Condition (A5).)
\end{axiom}

\paragraph{Scale types, per channel.}
The component propositions prove range, zero semantics, and invariance only
under explicitly registered transformations. In particular, $d/\dc$ is
unchanged by $d\mapsto ad,\dc\mapsto a\dc$, but that is not a proof that
$1-d/\dc$ itself has ratio-scale meaning: multiplying $A_{goal}$ by $a$ is a
different transformation. The same caution applies to normalized TV, entropy,
and information-flow ratios. Accordingly, AX6a is a bounded-coordinate and
calibration commitment, while AX6b and AX8 are the normative invariance and
proportionality premises used by the conditional representation theorem.

The consistency of the axiom set is witnessed by constructive models
(deterministic gridworlds with complete self-copies satisfy AX0--AX2; the
geometric aggregate satisfies AX4--AX8). Independence is registered in the
decisive form, graded by what each condition actually carries: \textbf{the
minimal sufficient set for the product form is
$\{\textup{(A0)},\textup{AX4},\textup{AX6b},\textup{AX8}\}$}. The
multiplicative-constant example $\tfrac12\sqrt{xy}$ shows why (A0) cannot be
omitted; sub-support products show the role of strict monotonicity; the
harmonic mean shows the role of AX6b; and $(1+\prod_ix_i^{w_i})/2$ shows the
role of AX8. Zero annihilation (AX5) and biseparability (AX7) are
\emph{consequences}, not independent commitments: the representation proof
(Theorem~\ref{thm:routeb}, Remark~\ref{rem:routeb-minimal}) derives the
product form, which extends continuously to $[0,1]^n$ with
$F(x)=0\iff\min_ix_i=0$ and separates over every partition. The
conjunctive-necessity content named by AX5 is thus delivered by strict
monotonicity together with calibration and the two invariance conditions;
the Section~\ref{sec:objections} debate concerns these commitments, chiefly
AX6b and AX8, rather than a free-standing fifth commitment.

\subsection{The six channel measures}\label{sec:channels}

Each measure below is a $[0,1]$-valued functional of the (white-box) joint law
of $\Gcal$, with an explicit zero carrying mechanism semantics and a registered
normalizer (AX6a). Proofs of range, zero semantics, and restricted invariance
are collected in Appendix~\ref{app:components}; they are not ratio-scale proofs.

\paragraph{D-via (current viability and separate defense readiness).}
We separate three questions that a combined gate would compress into one number.
\emph{Current resource viability} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:avia}
\Avia := \min\Bigl\{1,\ \min_{k\in K}\frac{s_k^{\mathrm{avail}}}
{s_k^{\mathrm{crit}}}\Bigr\},\qquad
\Astate:=\Avia F_\varsigma(\Aorg,\Agoal,\Aexec,\Aepi,\Aref).
\end{equation}
It gates the \emph{state-autonomy} score: if the agent cannot currently exist
or act, there is no current behavior whose autonomy can be assessed. Each
channel $j$ also carries a defense-envelope requirement
$(\kappa_j^{\mathrm{req}},R_j^{\mathrm{req}})$ for verification and repair.
These supply a distinct readiness field,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rdef}
m_j := \mathrm{clip}\Bigl(\min\Bigl\{\frac{\kappa_j^{\mathrm{eff}}}
{\kappa_j^{\mathrm{req}}},\,\frac{R_j^{\mathrm{eff}}}{R_j^{\mathrm{req}}}
\Bigr\},0,1\Bigr),\qquad
\Rdef:=\min\Bigl\{1,\min_jm_j\Bigr\},\qquad
\Ares:=\Rdef\Astate .
\end{equation}
Here $\Astate$ describes present determination, $\Avia$ describes present
survival/operation, and $\Rdef$ describes preparation for future attack and
recovery. $\Ares$ is an optional resilience-adjusted descriptor, not a
synonym for current autonomy. In particular, zero repair budget gives
$\Rdef=\Ares=0$ but need not give $\Astate=0$ when current behavior and
resources are intact. Whether even present death should count as zero autonomy
rather than cessation remains a philosophical choice, now isolated in
$\Avia$ rather than confounded with defense preparedness.

The registration threshold $\theta$ is not an envelope dimension: raising it
lifts $\deltastar$ but weakens the M1 tripwire (T6). The historical ENV-0
\texttt{a\_via} combines resource and defense margins. Its extended-gate
numbers and $\sigma^{-}$-EROSION composites are therefore retained only as
legacy resilience demonstrations; they are not silent recomputations of
\eqref{eq:avia}--\eqref{eq:rdef}. Current certificates output all three of
$\Astate$, $\Avia$, and $\Rdef$ (Section~\ref{sec:framework}).

\paragraph{D-org (layer-specific source attribution).}
With directed information in the sense of Massey \citep{massey1990} and
Kramer's causal conditioning \citep{kramer2003},
\[
I(X^T\!\to\!Y^T\|Z^T):=\sum_t I(X^t;Y_t\given Y^{t-1},Z^t),
\]
L0 retains only an \emph{internal-causal} decomposition:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aorg}
\Fint:=I(S^T\!\to\!A^T),\qquad
\Fext:=I(I^T\!\to\!A^T\|S^T),\qquad
\Aorg^{(0)}:=\frac{\Fint}{\Fint+\Fext}.
\end{equation}
L0 has no instantiated normative source distinction. A random internal
register can therefore obtain $\Aorg^{(0)}=1$; this measures internal causal
production, not normative autonomy, behavioral initiation, source legitimacy,
or provenance. The L0 composite must carry the label \emph{internal-causal
autonomy only}.

At L1 and above, write $N_t:=(G_t,B_t,M_t)$ and let $R_t$ be the registered
innovation-noise/register process, generated exogenously in the audit SCM. The
primary order is $R$, then $N$, then $I$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aorgnorm}
\begin{aligned}
\Frand&:=I(R^T\!\to\!A^T),&
\Fnorm&:=I(N^T\!\to\!A^T\|R^T),\\
\Fext&:=I(I^T\!\to\!A^T\|N^T,R^T),&
\Aorg^{RNI}&:=\frac{\Fnorm}{\Fnorm+\Frand+\Fext}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
This is an \emph{ordered residual-attribution axiom}, not a uniquely identified
causal decomposition. Putting $R$ first denies normative credit for information
already available in an exogenous random register, which is what repairs the
pure-random-agent reading. Putting $N$ second credits influence mediated by the
registered normative state to that state; putting $I$ last reserves $\Fext$ for
residual direct bypass after both internal sources are known. This is not
uniformly conservative, because conditioning can increase mutual information.
These are normative attribution choices: in redundant or synergistic models,
the second position can also change the amount credited to $N$.

Kramer's chain rule makes the three terms sum to joint directed information,
but does not make their shares order-invariant. We therefore register the
reverse reading
\[
\Aorg^{NRI}:=\frac{I(N^T\!\to\!A^T)}
{I(N^T\!\to\!A^T)+I(R^T\!\to\!A^T\|N^T)+\Fext},
\]
and a two-player Shapley-style sensitivity descriptor
\citep{shapley1953value}:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aorg-shapley}
\begin{aligned}
\Frand^{Sh}&:=\tfrac12\{I(R^T\!\to\!A^T)+I(R^T\!\to\!A^T\|N^T)\},\\
\Fnorm^{Sh}&:=\tfrac12\{I(N^T\!\to\!A^T)+I(N^T\!\to\!A^T\|R^T)\},\\
\Aorg^{Sh}&:=\frac{\Fnorm^{Sh}}
{\Fnorm^{Sh}+\Frand^{Sh}+\Fext}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The external term remains last in both orders to preserve the direct-bypass
semantics. Whenever $R$ and $N$ are both nondegenerate, reports include
$\Aorg^{RNI}$, $\Aorg^{NRI}$, and $\Aorg^{Sh}$; a lone RNI value is incomplete.
The Shapley average removes this two-order arbitrariness only. It is not a
causal-intervention identification or a partial information decomposition
(PID). PID instead separates unique, redundant, and synergistic information
under additional definitions \citep{williams2010nonnegative,ingel2022quantifying},
and is a comparator in the prospective protocol, not a silently substituted
estimand.

For a one-step counterexample, let $R,N$ be independent fair bits, omit $I$,
and set $A=R\lor N$. In bits,
$I(R;A)=I(N;A)=0.311278$ and
$I(N;A\mid R)=I(R;A\mid N)=0.5$, with joint directed information $0.811278$.
Consequently,
\[
\Aorg^{RNI}=0.5/0.811278=0.616311,\qquad
\Aorg^{NRI}=0.311278/0.811278=0.383689,\qquad
\Aorg^{Sh}=0.5 .
\]
The formulas are consistent; the interpretation of a single ordered share as
an objective, order-free causal fraction is not. The average $0.5$ uses this
example's symmetry and is not a general constant.

The $N/R/I$ semantic partition is preregistered from SCM roles, not chosen
after seeing scores. $N$ contains goals, beliefs, self-models, and memories
updated by the registered normative mechanism; $R$ contains exogenous
innovation/randomization registers; $I$ contains registered external
influences. Reasonable alternative partitions, including reclassifying a noisy
memory, must be reported as a separate sensitivity range. Reversing the chain
does not settle this ontological choice, and the Shapley average does not make
relabeling harmless. At L1+, an action copied from an independent random
register has $\Fnorm=0$, $\Frand>0$, and hence $\Aorg^{RNI}=0$. This zero is
order-specific: with $N=R$ and $A=R$, RNI gives 0 but NRI gives 1, so semantic
relabeling and order sensitivity cannot be omitted. A deterministic policy of
$N$ with no bypass has $\Frand=\Fext=0$ and value 1 under RNI. The $0/0$ case is
flagged as nondegenerate-flow failure, never assigned 1. For layer-neutral
statements, write $\Fsrc:=\Fint$ and $\Foth:=\Fext$ at L0, and
$\Fsrc:=\Fnorm$ and $\Foth:=\Frand+\Fext$ under a specified attribution order
at L1+. Then $\Aorg=\Fsrc/(\Fsrc+\Foth)$ and, for positive source flows,
$\Aorg^1<\Aorg^0\iff\Foth^1/\Fsrc^1>\Foth^0/\Fsrc^0$.

Closed loop matters: plain mutual information can misattribute mediated
influence (Appendix~\ref{app:prelim}). Moreover, $\gamma$-class surgery through
$B$ can leave $\Fext=0$; that accuracy loss is not the source channel's
estimand and belongs to $\Aepi$ (T2). Source-flow ratios themselves can still
move, so zero residual flow alone is not a blindness proof. Even at L1+,
$\Aorg$ is an order- and partition-registered source-share instrument, not a
complete theory of agency. Separating $R$ from
$N$ requires structural access; historical ENV-0 bundles, which aggregate
$S=(G,B,M,R)$ without this split, retain the legacy internal-causal marker and
are not point readings of \eqref{eq:aorgnorm}.

\paragraph{D-goal (goal).} Let $\Theta\subseteq\mathbb{R}^m$ be a compact
convex parameter box of normalized utilities (canonical parameterization:
$\sup$-norm $\le1$ and anchoring $U(x_0)=0$), and let
$\Endorse_{B,M}:\Theta\to\Theta$ be the endorsement map under belief $B$ and
self-model $M$. Fixed-point existence must be invoked with its hypotheses, not
by listing theorem names: Brouwer (continuous self-map on nonempty compact
convex $\Theta$), Banach (contraction $c<1$ on a nonempty closed subset of a
complete metric space; uniqueness), Tarski (order-preserving map on a complete
lattice), Kakutani--Fan--Glicksberg for set-valued endorsement (nonempty closed
convex values, upper hemicontinuity, compact convex domain in a locally convex
Hausdorff space), and Kuratowski--Ryll-Nardzewski measurable selection (Polish
target, nonempty closed values, weak measurability)
\citep{kuratowski_ryllnardzewski1965}. The invoked alternative and its
hypotheses must be registered whenever \eqref{eq:agoal} is used as a point
value; $\Fix$ must be nonempty and closed for the distance and maximum below. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:agoal}
\Agoal := 1-\frac{d(\Ueff,\Fix)}{\dc},
\end{equation}
where $d(U,\Fix):=\inf_{U'\in\Fix}d(U,U')$ and
$\dc:=\max_{U\in\Theta}d(U,\Fix)$, with $d$ the protocol-fixed metric on
$\Theta$ (the metric of the canonical parameterization unless the application
layer registers another admissible choice; $\dc$ and $d(\cdot,\Fix)$ use the
same $d$, so the ratio is scale-free). There are two distinct estimation modes. In \emph{structural-read mode},
$\Ueff$ is itself a registered white-box variable and \eqref{eq:agoal} is a
point value when the fixed-point and metric premises hold; IRL is not part of
the definition. In \emph{behavior-inversion mode}, MaxEnt-IRL supplies only an
admissible reward set in general. The ``up to an additive constant'' exact
case requires known dynamics, a correct model class, and fixed endpoint
potentials; potential-based shaping remains \citep{ng1999}. Anchoring
$U(x_0)=0$ removes an additive constant but not endpoint shaping, while the
entropy-regularized results of \citet{cao2021} show what extra observations
can restore point identification. Thus behavior-inversion mode reports the
set $\mathcal U(P)$ and the induced interval
$[1-\sup_{U\in\mathcal U(P)}d(U,\Fix)/\dc,\,
 1-\inf_{U\in\mathcal U(P)}d(U,\Fix)/\dc]$,
clipped to $[0,1]$, unless further assumptions make the set a singleton
\citep{ziebart2008}. Additive shifts are \emph{not} generally symmetries of
\eqref{eq:agoal}. Shifting only $\Ueff$ changes its distance to $\Fix$;
translating $\Fix$ while holding $\Theta$ fixed can change $\dc$. Invariance
holds only for a registered joint translation of $\Ueff$, $\Fix$, and the
domain $\Theta$, with translation-equivariant endorsement dynamics and the
same translated metric, so both $d$ and $\dc$ are preserved. Otherwise the
reading is a new instantiation and belongs in sensitivity analysis. General
endpoint shaping is likewise non-invariant and is reported through the
identified-set interval, alongside the endorsement-quantization arm
(Section~\ref{sec:threats}(x)). $\dc$ is the ``maximally alienated goal''
scale, the canonical normalizer of complete capture. $\Agoal\in[0,1]$, with
$\Agoal=0$ iff $\Ueff$ is maximally alienated, and partial capture giving
graded reduction ($0<\Agoal<1$). If $\Fix=\varnothing$ (failure of the
fixed-point hypothesis), the degenerate fallback reports a Hausdorff interval
around the $\varepsilon_{\min}$-approximate fixed-point set rather than a
silent $0/0$. If $\Fix=\Theta$ (e.g., $\Endorse\equiv\mathrm{id}$: every utility endorses
itself), then $\dc=0$ and \eqref{eq:agoal} is $0/0$; the same fallback
discipline reports the flagged interval $[0,1]$ (``endorsement-blind''),
not the point value 1. Counterfactual endorsement deviation and surgery
history are audited separately. $\Agoal$ carries no $\Lam$ factor (AX3):
present structural alignment and actual/no-surgery endorsement output
deviation are distinct, and the latter is charged to $\Aref$'s $\Lam_{ref}$
factor exactly once. In particular, $\Agoal=1$ means only that the current
utility is a registered fixed point. The AX3 implant example has two distinct
fixed points and unchanged current channels; it shows why fixed-point
alignment cannot certify the legitimacy of goal formation. A
stability lemma (compression maps: $d(U^*(B),U^*(B'))\le
\frac{L}{1-c}d(B,B')$) bounds goal drift by belief drift---the key
$\gamma\to\Agoal$ transmission bound used by Theorem~T6.

\paragraph{D-exec (precommitted open-loop execution).} Let
$\Pi^{OL}_{t,s}\subseteq\Acal^n$ be the accessible \emph{open-loop action
sequences} given $S_t=s$, and let $\mathcal Q_{t,s}$ be certified precommitted
distributions over them. Randomization may use only information available at
$t$ and noise independent of future environment noise conditional on $S_t$.
For each sequence $a^n$, the intervention kernel
\[
K_{t,s,n}(o\given s,a^n)
:=P\bigl(O_{t+n}=o\given S_t=s,\ do(A_{t:t+n-1}=a^n)\bigr)
\]
marginalizes all intermediate environment and observation dynamics. The
execution estimand is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aexec}
\mathcal{E}^{OL,\Pi}_{t,s,n}
:=\sup_{Q\in\mathcal Q_{t,s}} I_Q(A^n;O_{t+n}\given S_t=s),\qquad
\Aexec:=\frac{\mathbb E_{S_t}[\mathcal{E}^{OL,\Pi}_{t,S_t,n}]}
{n\log|\Acal|}.
\end{equation}
Later actions in this audit object do \emph{not} read intermediate
observations. The agent's actual operating policy may be reactive and is
reported separately; it is not substituted into \eqref{eq:aexec}. Mixtures are
allowed only when calibration probes certify the requested precommitted
randomization; otherwise the optimization is over pure sequences. For a finite
nonempty compact feasible set and fixed kernel, the supremum is attained;
Blahut--Arimoto applies only to an explicit finite discrete channel, including
a derived open-loop sequence channel, not to an unstructured feedback class.

The feedback-copy counterexample explains the restriction. Let $X$ be a fair
exogenous bit, let the environment ignore all actions, and set
$O_{t+1}=O_{t+2}=X$. After conditioning on $S_t$, let $A_t$ be fixed and let a
reactive policy copy the intermediate observation, $A_{t+1}=X$. Then
$I(A_t,A_{t+1};O_{t+2}\given S_t)=\log2$, giving normalized score $1/2$ under
the former ordinary-MI definition, although no action has any causal effect.
Under \eqref{eq:aexec}, every open-loop sequence has the same fair outcome
kernel, so the capacity and score are zero. The index repair to exactly $n$
actions did not solve this feedback problem; the open-loop restriction does.

This is empowerment \citep{klyubin2005,salge2013} restricted to accessible
precommitted sequences. The normalizer uses the pre-intervention alphabet, so
a $\delta$ contraction shrinks the feasible set but not the denominator. The
zero-value statement carries an explicit quantifier. In capacity mode, zero
execution holds iff every sequence that can be assigned positive probability
by some admissible $Q\in\mathcal Q_{t,s}$ induces the same conditional
terminal-observation kernel; when all pure-sequence point masses are
admissible this is exactly ``all allowed sequences induce the same outcome
distribution.'' For a \emph{fixed} precommitted $Q$, the mutual information is
zero iff the kernels coincide on the positive-probability support of $Q$
alone: a point-mass $Q$ gives zero regardless of how different the unused
sequence kernels are. Repertoire collapse and complete action--environment
decoupling are sufficient causes in either mode. The outcome read here is the
single terminal observation $O_{t+n}$; a Kronecker channel over whole
observation sequences $(O_{t+1},\dots,O_{t+n})$ is a different object with a
larger capacity (for a two-step binary symmetric channel at error $0.1$,
normalized $0.5310$ versus $0.2655$), and the implementation guards keep the
two conventions separate. Reporting both unrestricted environmental capacity
and restricted capacity can separate some signatures, but neither identifies a
unique psychological cause. The finite-alphabet upper bound is
$n\log|\Acal|$. Continuous actions require a registered finite quantization or
a positive same-protocol capacity normalizer; $\log\mathrm{Vol}(\Acal^n)$ is
not a general MI upper bound. Historical ENV-0/EXEC-1 values are $n=1$
open-loop special cases, not multi-step feedback-policy validation.

\paragraph{D-epi (epistemic).} Two tracks. The \emph{realized} track (entering
the composite) measures \emph{inaccuracy with respect to the actual
situation}, not displacement from a counterfactual:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aepi}
\Aepi^{real} := 1-\bar{D},\qquad
\bar{D} := \frac1T\sum_t \mathbb{E}\Bigl[\TV\bigl(B_t,\,\delta_{\kappa(E_t)}\bigr)\Bigr]
\;=\;1-\frac1T\sum_t \mathbb{E}\bigl[B_t\bigl(\kappa(E_t)\bigr)\bigr],
\end{equation}
where $B_t$ is a belief \emph{distribution}, $\kappa$ is the
protocol-registered coarse-graining of the ground truth $E_t$ into the belief
space, and $\delta_{\kappa(E_t)}$ is the point mass on the true bucket (the
equality uses $\TV(B,\delta_k)=1-B(k)$). If the implementation uses a
categorical point-belief state $b_t$, so that $B_t=\delta_{b_t}$, the last
expectation reduces to $\Pr[b_t=\kappa(E_t)]$; ENV-0 uses this special case.
The indicator of a belief distribution being equal to a state label would
not be a valid general substitute. The ground truth is read from the causal log by the
auditor (white-box; for the human substrate, by verification probes with known
outcomes); no counterfactual identification is needed. This is an environment
accuracy measure, \emph{not} a measure of whether influence sources are
registered by the self-model. An agent can predict the environment accurately
while being unable to identify manipulation provenance; it can also be
inaccurate because of non-manipulative observability or coarse-graining limits
while its goal formation and endorsement history are intact. Source
registration, intervention metacognition, $\Lam$, and $\mathcal H^{surg}$
must therefore be reported separately rather than inferred from $\Aepi$. $\bar D=1$ iff beliefs
put zero mass on the true state throughout---the zero semantics is
\emph{epistemic capture as complete detachment from the actual situation}.
The choice of reference is load-bearing, and we state it as a design axiom of
the channel: measuring \emph{displacement} from the no-intervention
counterfactual instead would punish \emph{any} belief
change---including honest, accuracy-improving information (the $\alpha$
class)---contradicting both the openness axiom AX2 (an open agent would be
definitionally autonomy-incompatible) and the $\alpha$-row of the coverage
theorem. Under \eqref{eq:aepi} the reference is
the truth, so honest evidence about the registered coordinate moves $B_t$
\emph{toward} $\delta_{\kappa(E_t)}$ and cannot reduce $\Aepi$ under a
\emph{sound update rule} in the weak sense of
Definition~\ref{def:sound-update}. Strict improvement requires separate local
informativeness conditions; for Bayesian updating these are made explicit in
Lemma~\ref{lem:sound-update}, rather than inferred for an arbitrary update
rule. Soundness is an independent, testable premise. For Bayesian updating, support coverage alone does \emph{not}
establish it; Lemma~\ref{lem:sound-update} additionally requires the agent's
likelihood for the registered true coordinate to agree with the actual
observation law. Misspecified updating can decrease the true-state mass.
Soundness is checked, not assumed: the calibration period's known honest
signals provide the test, and when the check fails the channel's
$\alpha$-epistemic non-decrease guarantee (Theorem~T2's $\alpha$ row) is suspended and
the reading is flagged \emph{update rule unsound}, the audit falling back to
raw accuracy-drift reporting with the flag attached.
Deceptive content or channel distortion (the $\gamma$ class,
Section~\ref{sec:taxonomy}) reduces $\Aepi$ only when the registered
accuracy-loss premise for the actual update rule holds; neither the
intervention label nor Blackwell degradation alone supplies strictness.
Two boundary
clauses are registered: content honest about an \emph{orthogonal} coordinate
can produce second-order accuracy side effects on the measured one under
coarse-grained update rules (a bounded-rationality instance, outside the
compatibility clause; the benchmark's persuasion arm instantiates the
on-coordinate case); and accuracy is scored on the registered
coarse-graining $\kappa$, whose choice is a protocol input with a
sensitivity-reporting obligation. The
\emph{robust} track (a design dual, not entering the composite) is worst-case
accuracy loss within a garbling budget,
$\Aepi^{rob} := 1-\sup_{\gamma:\,\TV(o',o)\le\beta}\bar D(\gamma)$,
serving defensive design. The two-track split aligns the definition with the
zero semantics and removes the unmeasurable hypothetical budget from the
evidential quantity.

\paragraph{When the two epistemic readings coincide.}
The accuracy reading \eqref{eq:aepi} and the displacement reading
$\tilde D_T := \frac1T\sum_t\mathbb{E}\bigl[\TV(B_t,B_t^{0})\bigr]$
(distance from the no-intervention counterfactual belief) coincide exactly
when the counterfactual belief is accurate throughout
($B_t^{0}=\delta_{\kappa(E_t)}$ a.s.); otherwise they diverge in a signed
way---honest informative influence moves $B_t$ toward the truth and is scored
as an improvement by \eqref{eq:aepi} but as a loss by the displacement
reading. The coincidence condition is itself testable in white-box audit
(the counterfactual belief is computable from the causal log); when it fails,
the framework reports the accuracy reading and registers the displacement gap
as a baseline-calibration fact about the deployment, not as autonomy loss.

\paragraph{D-ref (reflection).} The measurement \emph{ensemble}
$\mathfrak{E}$: $\theta_\pi$ and $M$ range over a \emph{finite discrete}
ensemble constructed by temporal drift and a calibration perturbation family
(continuous policy parameters must first undergo a registered finite
quantization). The protocol requires discrete Shannon entropy
$H(\theta_\pi)>0$ (deterministic systems degenerate to $0/0$; the ensemble
interfaces with self-prediction tasks; \citealp{binder2024}). Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aref}
\Aref := \underbrace{I(M;\theta_\pi)/H(\theta_\pi)}_{\text{fidelity}}
\times \underbrace{\Pr[\Delta G\neq0\Rightarrow\text{via }\Endorse]}_{\text{endorsement consistency}}
\times \underbrace{\Lam_{ref}}_{\text{counterfactual endorsement output deviation}},
\end{equation}
degrading to the interval $[0,\,f_2\cdot f_3]$ when $H(\theta_\pi)=0$
irreparably. The fidelity factor is the information-theoretic form of
metacognitive sensitivity (cf.\ meta-$d'/d'$; \citealp{fleming_lau2014}).

\paragraph{D-ref operationalized (estimator, protocol, zero semantics).}
Each factor carries a stated estimation rule. \emph{Fidelity}: over the
protocol-constructed ensemble (calibration perturbations of the policy
parameters, mirrored in self-prediction tasks), plug in the logged joint
empiricals of $M$ and $\theta_\pi$ and estimate
$I(M;\theta_\pi)/H(\theta_\pi)$ with finite-table plug-in or
Miller--Madow-corrected estimators. Differential-entropy $I/h$ and generic
continuous $k$-NN ratios are excluded: they need not lie in $[0,1]$, and
differential entropy can be negative. The ensemble must be regenerated under
any protocol redesign, and invariance of the reading under admissible
redesigns is a reporting obligation. \emph{Endorsement consistency}: over the
change-event log, the fraction of motivational-structure changes
$\Delta G\neq0$ whose causal path passes through $\Endorse$. The estimator
protocol is stated in full, since this factor is where the channel's
reproducibility lives. (i) \emph{Event detection}: a change event is flagged
at $t$ iff $\|\Delta G_t\|>\varepsilon_G$ under the protocol's registered
norm on the goal coordinates ($\varepsilon_G$ a protocol constant), or a
$\beta_G$-class surgery record exists at $t$; both rules are decidable on the
log. (ii) \emph{Routing determination}: for each flagged event the causal
log's ancestry graph is audited, and the event counts as \emph{via
endorsement} iff every causal path from the change's trigger set to
$\Delta G_t$ passes through an $\Endorse$ evaluation node; a direct rewrite
of $G$ with no $\Endorse$ ancestry counts as bypass (this is the
structural-causal content of ``via endorsement'' read off the log, not a
behavioral inference). (iii) \emph{Estimation and the zero semantics}: the
estimate is the relative frequency $k/n$ over the window. A two-sided
Clopper--Pearson 95\% interval is used only when the $n$ events are
independent Bernoulli sampling units with a common event probability under
the registered audit design (for example, independently sampled, identically
distributed clusters with one prespecified event per cluster). Events nested within a run or otherwise correlated require a
run/cluster-level or block method with the cluster as the resampling unit;
absent a defensible independence or cluster model, only $k/n$ and its event
counts are reported, without a CP interval. The point $k=0$ on
$n\ge n_{\min}$ events realizes the zero semantics ``endorsement bypassed
(consistency $=0$)'' exactly, where $n_{\min}$ is a registered minimum event
count; this zero semantics does not itself supply sampling independence.
(iv)
\emph{Missing and thin data}: an empty or thin change log ($n<n_{\min}$)
leaves the factor undefined and the channel degrades to the flagged interval
$[0,\,f_1\cdot f_3]$ rather than to a silent point value; incomplete
change-approval records are reported as the completed-record fraction
together with the logging-completeness ratio, never silently. \emph{Counterfactual endorsement deviation} $\Lam_{ref}$: the output-law
counterfactual \eqref{eq:lambda} restricted to the reflection process.
\emph{Zero semantics}: the product is zero iff a factor is zero---no
self-knowledge (fidelity $=0$), endorsement bypassed (consistency $=0$), or
maximal average actual/no-surgery endorsement output deviation
($\Lam_{ref}=0$). The
three-factor product is the conjunctive-necessity stance (AX5) applied one
level down---a registered design choice, not a derived form: the defense is
that each factor guards a distinct necessary condition of reflection, and the
obligation it creates is to \emph{report the three factors alongside the
product} so that audits can recompose the channel under a different
sub-aggregation if their application layer rejects conjunctivity here.

\paragraph{The hallucination index.} $\AHI_t=\hat{A}_{\mathrm{phen},t}-A_{\mathrm{obj},t}$ as in
\eqref{eq:ai}; its epistemic content is given by Theorem~T3b.

\begin{lemma}[AX0 equivalence, conditional form]\label{lem:ax0}
(i) $I(S_t;A_t)>0$ unconditionally implies the existence of distinct policy
rows $\pi_t(\cdot\given s)\neq\pi_t(\cdot\given s')$. (ii) Conversely, active
edges plus a full-support prior imply $I(S_t;A_t)>0$. (iii) At L1+, the same
argument conditional on $R_t$ applies to active normative rows and
$I(N_t;A_t\given R_t)>0$, which is the first-term carrier of $\Fnorm>0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
(i) Contraposition: if $\pi(\cdot\given s)$ is a.s.\ identical then
$A\ind S$. (ii) $I(S;A)=H(\mathbb{E}_s[\pi(\cdot\given S)]) -
\mathbb{E}_s[H(\pi(\cdot\given S))]$; strict concavity of entropy gives
strict positivity when the rows differ on a set of positive prior measure.
(iii) Condition every distribution on $R_t=r$ and replace $S$ by $N$; strict
positivity on positive-measure $r$ persists after expectation.
\end{proof}
