\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We have proposed a substrate-neutral, instantiation-relative procedural audit
schema for autonomy: five channels behind a viability gate, with channel
profiles and evidence grades as the primary output. Each channel has an
estimator, a protocol normalizer, and a mechanism-labeled zero; these facts do
not prove ratio scales. The weighted geometric aggregator is characterized by
the stated calibration, monotonicity, scale-invariance, and homogeneity
commitments, and the multiplicative viability gate remains an independent
design choice rather than a consequence of conditional independence.

The theorem suite is deliberately conditional. Strict origination loss
requires an increased external/internal flow ratio; goal loss requires an
increased off-$\Fix$ distance; strict composite loss requires a positive
baseline. T4 separates log-linear highest-leverage concentration from the
correct extreme-point analysis of linear-fraction damage, and the script
corollary requires a complete sufficient state and noise accounting. T6
retains its registered margin and certificate-failure band. T7 remains
inconclusive on the registered DEL-1 arms.

The estimand distinction is equally important. $\Lambda$ measures
actual/no-surgery endorsement output-law deviation under an explicit SCM; it
does not measure whether tampering history exists. $A_{epi}$ measures
environment accuracy, not influence-source recognition, and $A_{org}$ measures
direct-bypass resistance, not general origination. Nondegenerate
observationally equivalent endorsement SCMs show that the specified passive
protocol cannot identify $\Lambda$. Historical certificates therefore require
additional provenance or mechanism evidence; full structural logs are only
one sufficient route under stronger model assumptions.

Evidence has been reported by type rather than by a headline count of
``confirmed theories.'' The historical $4{,}620$-run archive and separate R11
DEL-1 replay supply definition, implementation, and same-model checks, not
external validation. R14 adds counterexample regressions and independent
vertex/grid/symbolic optimization certificates. The time-stratified X5-c
executes abduction--action--prediction under Gaussian and logistic noise:
implementation criteria pass, but the maximum cross-model $\Lambda$ difference
exceeds the registered SESOI, so point estimates are reported as model-sensitive
bands. A new same-environment C0-CF witness preserves identical $(E,O,A)$
laws while separating accuracy and $\Lambda$; it does not rewrite legacy
C0-DEMO or validate the construct externally.

Failed criteria, post hoc protocol clarifications, and unrun programs remain
in the public ledger. Independent-substrate validation, frozen external
SESOI/power analysis, selector-conditional DEL-1 estimation, and a frozen
public R14 release/version DOI remain outstanding. The resulting contribution
is an auditable conceptual and mathematical framework with executable model
checks, not an empirically validated universal measure of autonomy.
