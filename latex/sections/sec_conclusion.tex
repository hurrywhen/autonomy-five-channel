\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We have proposed a substrate-neutral, instantiation-relative audit schema with
two co-primary outputs: current structural autonomy and historical formation
autonomy. The current profile is gated by $\Avia$, with $\Rdef$ and optional
$\Ares$ separate; the historical profile is evidence, not a scalar inferred
from current channels. L0 is limited to internal-causal autonomy. L1+ ordered
residual attribution gives zero normative credit to a pure independent random
register under primary RNI, but NRI and semantic-partition reports expose
non-uniqueness.

The mathematical statements remain conditional. Multi-step execution is
restricted to precommitted open-loop sequences; the feedback-copy counterexample
shows why reactive ordinary MI is not execution capacity. T2 supplies mediation
and componentwise sufficient certificates, and any single channel is
insufficient for the registered classes, but no exactly-five minimality is
claimed. Goal alignment is point-valued under structural reads and generally
set/interval-valued under behavioral IRL; a fixed-point implant can leave
$\Agoal=1$ while changing historical formation. The weighted-geometric
aggregator is a conditional characterization, not a proof of ratio scales or
causal attribution; exact weight-simplex bounds replace a few alternative
weights as the primary weight-sensitivity analysis, and the historical
contrasts are mostly weight-dependent.

$\Lambda$ measures actual/no-surgery output-law deviation under an explicit SCM
and time origin. Audit-time removal and original-implant prevention are
different estimands. Surgery history and source registration are mandatory
co-primary fields, and unknown provenance caps the evidence grade.
Nondegenerate observationally equivalent SCMs prove the specified passive
protocol cannot identify $\Lambda$.

Evidence is reported by type. The historical $4{,}620$-run archive supplies
implementation and same-model checks. The native joint-log benchmark audits
current estimands from one trajectory batch of a single registered SCM, with
the separate open-loop stream and sampled negative controls. Layer-aware
certificates distinguish channel states and interval kinds, disclose
share-conditional sampling intervals, and never read overlapping intervals as
agreement. Matched diagnostics compare constructions and channel propagation
without a claimed causal bias decomposition; envelope containment remains
distinct from sampling coverage. Exact convention endpoints are model anchors,
not recoverability claims: multi-seed studies show that geometric-composite
plug-in bias prevents recovery of the near-zero NRI endpoint, whose honest data
verdict is indeterminate. Evidence hashes bind immutable raw inputs;
numerical-replay details and retained failure history are in the repository.
X5-c remains model-sensitive,
C0-CF is a same-environment model construction, T7 remains inconclusive, and
failed or post hoc criteria remain visible. External substrates, a
domain-justified SESOI and power analysis, external execution,
independent-investigator reimplementation, and selector-conditional DEL-1
estimation remain outstanding; the engineering equivalence/non-inferiority
margins and TOST rule are design-frozen. We contribute a conditional conceptual
and mathematical audit framework with executable model checks, not a
deployment-ready tool, a cross-substrate measurement standard, or an
empirically validated universal measure. No universal interval coverage,
statistical consistency, or real-LLM/human external validation is claimed.
