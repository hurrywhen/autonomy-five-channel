\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We have proposed a substrate-neutral, instantiation-relative procedural audit
schema for autonomy. Its primary output is a channel profile with provenance
and evidence grades, not a universal scalar. Current resource viability
$\Avia$ gates present $\Astate$; defense readiness $\Rdef$ and the optional
resilience descriptor $\Ares$ are separate. L0 is explicitly limited to
internal-causal autonomy, while L1+ uses ordered residual attribution to
separate normative flow from independent innovation and external bypass flow.
Reverse-order and Shapley-style reports expose the non-uniqueness of that share.
Thus a pure random register can illustrate a high L0 internal-causal reading but
cannot receive maximal L1+ normative source attribution.

The mathematical statements remain conditional. Execution empowerment uses
exactly $n$ actions and attains a maximum only under stated compactness/channel
premises. T2 supplies mediation and componentwise sufficient certificates, not
a no-surgery/full-mediation/honesty iff or four-track shortcut. Goal alignment
is point-valued under structural reads but generally set/interval-valued under
behavioral IRL. C2 distinguishes $s>s_c$ after attack removal from
$s>\delta+s_c$ under persistent attack. The weighted-geometric aggregator is a
classical conditional characterization, with quotient-cone pointedness and
covector normalization proved explicitly; neither gate follows from
conditional independence.

The endorsement estimand is equally distinct from history. $\Lambda$ measures
actual/no-surgery output-law deviation under an explicit SCM; surgery history
and source registration are mandatory separate fields, and unknown provenance
caps the evidence grade. $A_{epi}$ measures environment accuracy, not source
recognition. Nondegenerate observationally equivalent SCMs still prove the
specified passive protocol cannot identify $\Lambda$.

Evidence is reported by type rather than a headline count. The historical
$4{,}620$-run archive and separate DEL-1 replay supply implementation and
same-model checks, not external validation or automatic identification of the
current constructs. Deterministic negative controls, exact closed-loop source
enumerations, and a tie-aware $\tau_b$ reporting audit test the implementation.
X5-c remains model-sensitive, C0-CF is
a same-environment model construction, T7 remains inconclusive, and failed or
post hoc criteria remain in the ledger. Ingel's RL environments and
Albantakis's automata are candidate external substrates for comparisons with
their next-state PID quantities and candidate measures, alongside newly labeled
intervention-attribution tests; none has yet been run for this framework.

External validation, external SESOI/power analysis, and selector-conditional
estimation for DEL-1 remain outstanding; release identifiers and replay
requirements are in the data-availability statement. We contribute an auditable
conceptual and mathematical framework, an intervention-attribution protocol,
and executable model checks---not an empirically validated universal measure.
