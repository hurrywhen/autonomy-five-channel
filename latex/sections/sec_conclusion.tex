\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We have proposed a substrate-neutral, instantiation-relative audit schema with
two co-primary outputs: current structural autonomy and historical formation
autonomy. The current profile is gated by $\Avia$, with $\Rdef$ and optional
$\Ares$ separate; the historical profile is evidence, not a scalar inferred
from current channels. L0 is limited to internal-causal autonomy. L1+ ordered
residual attribution gives zero normative credit to a pure independent random
register under primary RNI, but NRI and semantic-partition reports expose
non-uniqueness.

The mathematical statements remain conditional. Multi-step execution is
restricted to precommitted open-loop sequences; the feedback-copy counterexample
shows why reactive ordinary MI is not execution capacity. T2 supplies mediation
and componentwise sufficient certificates, and any single channel is
insufficient for the registered classes, but no exactly-five minimality is
claimed. Goal alignment is point-valued under structural reads and generally
set/interval-valued under behavioral IRL; a fixed-point implant can leave
$\Agoal=1$ while changing historical formation. The weighted-geometric
aggregator is a conditional characterization, not a proof of ratio scales or
causal attribution; exact weight-simplex bounds replace a few alternative
weights as the primary weight-sensitivity analysis, and the historical
contrasts are mostly weight-dependent.

$\Lambda$ measures actual/no-surgery output-law deviation under an explicit SCM
and time origin. Audit-time removal and original-implant prevention are
different estimands. Surgery history and source registration are mandatory
co-primary fields, and unknown provenance caps the evidence grade.
Nondegenerate observationally equivalent SCMs prove the specified passive
protocol cannot identify $\Lambda$.

Evidence is reported by type. The historical $4{,}620$-run archive supplies
implementation and same-model checks. The native joint-log benchmark audits
the current estimands from one trajectory batch of a single registered SCM,
with the separate open-loop execution stream the estimand requires and
sampled negative controls; its layer-aware certificates make every channel,
attribution order, semantic partition, and the composite range mandatory,
support interval/unknown/degraded states without forced point values, carry
a declared data-driven near-zero detection rule per convention (with
interval kinds separating logical envelopes from sampling intervals), disclose
above-limit composite intervals as \emph{share-conditional} (other channels fixed
at points), compare interval constructions and channel propagation through
matched supplementary diagnostics rather than a claimed causal bias decomposition,
keep envelope containment distinct from sampling coverage, and
evaluate order/semantic sensitivity as confirmed/indeterminate/not-evaluable
under post hoc frozen internal thresholds---never as midpoint agreement.
Exact convention endpoints are model anchors, not finite-sample
recoverability claims: multi-seed studies show plug-in bias amplified by the
geometric composite prevents recovery of the near-zero NRI endpoint; the
executable rule honestly reports it as indeterminate at the registered
sample size, with the plug-in composite retained only as a diagnostic.
Evidence hashes bind immutable raw inputs, and non-unique
optimizer coordinates are canonicalized under a declared rule with per-field
replay tolerances; the recorded R18 cross-environment replay failure and its
repair are retained. It is internal concept validation.
X5-c remains model-sensitive, C0-CF is a same-environment
model construction, T7 remains inconclusive, and failed or post hoc criteria
remain visible. External substrates, a domain-justified SESOI and power
analysis, external execution, independent-investigator reimplementation, and
selector-conditional DEL-1 estimation remain outstanding; the engineering
equivalence/non-inferiority margins and the TOST decision rule are
design-frozen. We contribute an auditable conceptual and
mathematical framework and executable model checks---not an empirically
validated universal measure.
