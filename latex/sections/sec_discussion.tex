\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{What the framework buys}

One measure stack runs from concept to audit: a substrate-neutral definition
(Definition~\ref{def:autonomy}); interface-relative intervention-primitive
coverage with composite labels (Proposition~\ref{thm:taxonomy-completeness});
axioms from which the
aggregation form is \emph{derived} (Theorem~\ref{thm:rep}); a theorem suite
in which every claim carries an explicit falsification window
(Section~\ref{sec:theorems}); an identifiability theory stating what is
knowable from what data (Section~\ref{sec:identifiability}); and an evidence
registry separating mathematical status, criterion outcomes, evidence type,
and external validation, with T7 inconclusive under the corrected bracket
(Section~\ref{sec:experiments}).
The primary output is the channel profile and evidence grade; the scalar
$A$ is a secondary joint index under the registered normative axioms. The
discipline matters as much as the results: a \textsc{refuted} verdict cannot
be silently edited away. Any post hoc criterion replacement requires a
versioned record of the original failure, its rationale, and its separate
evidential status, as now documented for E2-X1.

\subsection{The objectivity question: can a designed metric measure
autonomy?}\label{sec:objectivity}

A standing objection deserves a direct answer before the point-by-point
debates: \emph{every indicator carries its designer's limitations---how can
limited indicators objectively measure autonomy?} We concede the premise and
deny that the conclusion follows, in four parts.

\emph{First, the claim is scoped, not denied.} We nowhere claim the five
channels \emph{are} autonomy, or that a mind-independent reading exists and
our instruments recover it. Under an explicit instantiation (self-boundary,
endorsement map, protocol, horizon) and explicit aggregation commitments,
these quantities are an instantiation-relative operationalization of the
registered construct, with every commitment priced
(Section~\ref{sec:representation}). If objectivity requires convention-freedom,
no measurement of anything complex---intelligence, poverty, health,
democracy---is objective; the objection proves too much, and the measurement
sciences relocate objectivity from the instrument to the discipline around it
\citep{krantz1971,oecd2008}.

\emph{Second, we claim procedural and conditional auditability, not
objectivity delivered by theorems.} (i) \emph{Registered invariance}: AX6b
preserves an induced ordering under positive coordinatewise rescaling, and
the representation theorem shows this axiom does real work
(Proposition~\ref{prop:harmonic}); it does not make different teams' protocols
comparable. (ii) \emph{Auditable error structure}: every channel carries an
estimator, an absolute zero with mechanism semantics, a canonical normalizer,
and a registered check; the identifiability analysis
(Section~\ref{sec:identifiability}) states per channel what is knowable from
what data, and where knowledge is impossible (implanted endorsement,
Proposition~\ref{prop:unidentifiable}) the impossibility is \emph{proved}.
These proofs delimit the instrument; they are not evidence that independent
teams reconstruct stable readings. (iii) \emph{Mandatory sensitivity
reporting}: weight and elasticity bands are obligations, so residual
designer-dependence is itself reported.

\emph{Third, explicit registration replaces neutrality.} The product form
has one normalization-explicit proof here and coincides with the classical
scale-consistency characterization of ratio-scale indices
\citep{ebert_welsch2004}; no independent second derivation is claimed. This
does not prove the channels are ratio scales, that percentage losses are
substantively meaningful across protocols, or that log-damage shares are
causal effects---those shares are bookkeeping under the selected weights and
aggregator. The channels bind to instruments built for other purposes
(directed information, empowerment, IRL, metacognitive sensitivity), and
three governance frameworks admit an interpretive correspondence under
registered readings (Proposition~\ref{prop:c0}). These are constraints on
arbitrary design, not evidence of external construct validity.

\emph{Fourth, the residual subjectivity is made falsifiable rather than
denied.} The channel carve-up is a registered design choice, and the
framework's own falsifier is external and stated
(Section~\ref{sec:experiments}): a strictly coarser account must be shown
equivalent or non-inferior on preregistered independent outcomes, with frozen
engineering margins and nested validation, before the five-channel choice is
disconfirmed. Equivalence is declared only by two one-sided tests (TOST) at
$\alpha=0.05$ each---equivalently, a 90\% two-sided interval inside the frozen
margins---and non-inferiority by the corresponding one-sided bound in the
registered direction; non-significant differences are not equivalence. Until
that test is run, the honest status is an \emph{explicitly committed,
internally audited, externally pending} operationalization.

\emph{Conditional auditability, not convergence to true autonomy.} We make no
construct-level or asymptotic convergence claim. Registered functionals are
identified and auditable under: (i) \emph{fixed instantiation}
(re-instantiation---including surgery on the environment's own
dynamics---restarts the audit, Section~\ref{sec:taxonomy}); (ii) \emph{layer
discipline} (cross-layer readings are profiles, not scalar rankings); (iii)
\emph{identification tiers} (each channel's premise met---joint-law access
plus the L1+ $N/R$ partition for $\Aorg$, ground truth for $\Aepi$,
current-resource records for $\Avia$, structural $\Ueff$ or an IRL identified
set for $\Agoal$, certified precommitted open-loop kernels for $\Aexec$, the
constructed ensemble for $\Aref$, abduction-level access for $\Lam$; failed
premises invoke the degradation rules of
Section~\ref{sec:identifiability}, down to proved impossibility); (iv)
\emph{axiom commitments accepted, or relaxations reported} (every verdict at
$\varsigma=1$ with relaxation bands); and (v) \emph{registered estimation
checks} (the two-path exercise reports absolute error $\le0.0096$ on five
fixed configurations; the multi-seed native studies show plug-in bias
amplified by the geometric composite prevents finite-sample recovery of
near-zero convention endpoints---implementation agreement and an honest
recoverability boundary, not statistical consistency or stability under
independent reconstruction). Under (i)--(iv), truth mode computes the
registered joint-law functionals exactly by definition; it does not compute
the legacy $\Lambda$ marker as a counterfactual. No condition here makes the
\emph{construct} converge to a mind-independent ``true autonomy''; that
residual remains a registered design choice exposed to the external falsifier
of clause four.

\subsection{Objections and debates}\label{sec:objections}

The aggregation axioms carry the framework's normative weight; the map from
each rejected commitment to what would be lost is collected in
Section~\ref{sec:representation} (``the normative commitments, and what
rejecting one costs''), and the debates below presuppose it.

\paragraph{(i) The historical condition: is procedural autonomy enough?}
We retain historical provenance as an audit requirement but no longer conflate
it with $\Lam$: the surgery-history flag $\mathcal H^{surg}$ records whether
registered tampering occurred, while \eqref{eq:lambda} measures
actual/no-surgery endorsement output deviation. Nonempty history can coexist
with $\Lam=1$, and $\Lam<1$ requires an output-law difference. The
manipulation cases that motivate historical analysis (reward tampering
\citep{everitt2021}; deceptive alignment \citep{hubinger2019}) need both
provenance and structural counterfactual evidence, not a label-valued
shortcut. Proposition~\ref{prop:unidentifiable} uses nondegenerate
observationally equivalent SCMs to prove the limit of the specified passive
protocol---not that every active probe is non-behavioral or that full logs
are uniquely necessary (Corollary~\ref{cor:logs-necessary}). At L0/L1 the
scalar composite is weaker than a complete history-sensitive certificate;
unknown history/source registration caps the grade at \textsc{limited}, and
no score may be exported as an integrity certificate.

\paragraph{(ii) Phenomenological autonomy.} We neither enthrone nor dismiss:
the phenomenal track is not a component of autonomy but supplies calibration
data and the hallucination detector $\AHI$ \eqref{eq:ai}. T3b's directional
prediction is that the registered lower bound $g(s)$ increases with attack
strength; monotone growth of the realized gap is only an ENV-0 observation
(ATK-1(iv), ATK-2(iii)(iv)), with a qualitative analogue in sycophancy
\citep{sharma2023}, not direct LLM validation. The dissociation of felt agency
from causal contribution \citep{moore_obhi2012} is exactly what the dual-track
discipline encodes. We remain agnostic on machine consciousness
\citep{chalmers2023,butlin2023,long2024}.

\paragraph{(iii) Self-boundary.} We take boundaries as given (engineering
individuation: weights-plus-context, body-plus-metabolism), consistently with
what the AI-agency literature assumes \citep{dung2024,kasirzadeh2025,feng2025},
and explicitly so. The pearl-blanket critique \citep{bruineberg2022} shows
blanket structure cannot by itself ground the \emph{normative} self autonomy
ascriptions require; we therefore do not build on Markov blankets, but reserve
an interface for unification with the free-energy tradition
\citep{friston2010,friston2023}. This is division of labor, not rivalry.

\paragraph{(iv) Procedural versus substantive.} The framework is procedural
with the pluralism risk quarantined, not denied: $\Ustar$ is elicited by the
agent's own endorsement procedure rather than prescribed in content, and the
application layer may anchor norms locally (value pluralism
\citep{gabriel2020} is an application parameter, not a theory defect). The
substantive camp's cases (oppressive internalized preferences) cannot be read
off from $\Lam<1$ or $\Aepi^{real}<1$ alone: endorsement output deviation and
environmental inaccuracy have different causes and do not establish
provenance or normative illegitimacy. The framework supplies auditable
instruments for those distinct questions rather than claiming to settle them.

\paragraph{(v) Coercion versus scaffolding.} Theorem~T8 does not classify an
intervention by its stated purpose. It gives two conditional mechanism
cases: a direct goal implant strictly off $\Fix$ reduces $\Agoal$, and an
action-leverage contraction strictly reducing $\EmpPi$ reduces execution;
either reduces $\Astate$ ceteris paribus (a corresponding $\Ares$ decrease
additionally requires fixed positive $\Rdef$). Scaffolding has no uniform
``all parameters raise the floor'' comparative static: repair-capacity
increases can raise both $\deltastar$ and $\Amin$, whereas lower $\theta$
does not raise $\deltastar$ and higher redundancy need not raise
\eqref{eq:amin} without the separate M4 mechanism. The SDT literature
\citep{ryan_deci2000} is qualitative triangulation for these distinctions,
not direct evidence for the theorem.

\paragraph{(v-a) Present autonomy, survival, and preparedness.}
A combined gate would let zero verification or repair capacity annihilate the
same scalar used for present behavioral determination. We reject that
compression: $\Astate$ answers who determines current behavior under current
resources, $\Avia$ whether the system can presently exist and act, $\Rdef$
whether registered defenses can preserve or recover it under future attacks.
A system with intact current behavior and zero repair budget can have
positive $\Astate$, zero $\Rdef$, and zero optional $\Ares$; exhausted
critical resources still zero $\Avia$ and $\Astate$. This refuses to give
future vulnerability the semantics of present nonexistence. Historical
EROSION numbers support only the old resilience convention.

\paragraph{(vi) The measurement paradigm debate.} Level lists
\citep{feng2025}, dimension profiles \citep{kasirzadeh2025,dung2024}, capability
benchmarks \citep{liu2023,kapoor2024}, and the capability/permission/autonomy
separation \citep{capability_permission2026} are mutually unconnected; none
supplies a cross-substrate definition, an axiomatized composition rule,
per-channel estimators, and loss/retention dynamics at once.
Proposition~\ref{prop:c0} gives registered explanatory mappings from our state
descriptor to the profile frameworks; clause (ii) and Corollary~\ref{cor:c0}
give an additional-audit separation and a passive-behavior non-identifiability
witness, without asserting identical full mapped profiles or that the source
frameworks are special cases of ours. Aggregation arbitrariness is addressed by
the representation theorem and its invariance analysis
(Section~\ref{sec:objectivity}, clause two) plus the mandatory relaxation bands
$\varsigma\in[0.5,1)$ (AX6b) and $\varsigma=2$ (AX5). We register the challenge
of \citet{xing2026} (the agent construct itself): our identity and
self-regulation dimensions are the $M$/$R$ channels and $\Aref$, compatible with
richer self-constructs.

\paragraph{(vii) Black-box knowability.} Rather than principled pessimism or
quiet white-box assumptions, we state per-channel identifiability conditions
(\S\ref{sec:datareq}) and prove the degradation rules with their certified
scope: a one-sided certified bound \eqref{eq:aorg-interval} for $\Aorg$ under
bypass-semantics probes (the reference lower end is a labeled heuristic, not a
bound); an independent-probe direction for $\Aexec$ but \emph{direction
unidentified} under general passive observation; and $[\Lam_{\min},1]$ for
$\Lam$ with a nontrivial lower end only from warranted provenance or mechanism
evidence \citep{capability_permission2026}. The benchmark exercises these rules
(BB-1: the certified origination direction holds $3/3$ on bypass arms, its
passive execution inequality only an ENV-0 observation; BB-2: the certified
scope and its calibrated premise violation under adaptive adversaries). The
practical scope is plain: the framework is white-box-first because its core
quantities (directed informations against internal states; the law-level $\Lam$)
presume causal-log access current LLM-agent deployments do not provide; absent
that access the deployable remainder is the one-sided origination bound,
independent-probe execution comparisons, and the impossibility warnings---a
narrower audit than the title suggests, registered as the honest applicability
boundary (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}(x)).

\paragraph{(viii) Cross-substrate comparability.} The shared audit schema is
a common \emph{definition and reporting schema}, not a common instrument:
measurement
protocols differ by substrate (\S\ref{sec:datareq}), and the continuous-action
normalization requires a registered finite quantization or a same-protocol
capacity choice, not $\log\mathrm{Vol}$ as a generic bound. Cross-substrate
comparisons do not license scalar $A$-rankings. The required report is a
channel \emph{profile} with the instantiation stated, including the
protocol-fixed metric for $\Agoal$, registered quantization/capacity for
$\Aexec$, ensembles for $\Aref$, and elicitation protocol for $\Ustar$.
Within a substrate and layer, at a fixed instantiation, the normalized
coordinates are fully ordered, but ratio interpretations remain normative and
calibration-relative rather than established by the component proofs.

\paragraph{(ix) Logging governance.} When full structural logging is chosen
as the provenance route (Corollary~\ref{cor:logs-necessary}), it has a
dual-use tension the framework registers rather than resolves: the same logs
that make manipulation auditable make comprehensive monitoring feasible.
Alternative provenance mechanisms have different governance costs.
Deployment obligations---log holders and access control, retention limits,
purpose binding, and tension with data-protection law---belong to the
governance layer the framework measures rather than prescribes. The
calibration period's known-intervention probes on human subjects are
manipulations under ethical review; the four-phase protocol is designed for
institutional review, not around it.

\paragraph{The C0 caveat.} Proposition~\ref{prop:c0}(i) uses
\emph{definitional readings} of the target frameworks' dimensions
(Appendix~\ref{app:c0}); their faithfulness to the source texts remains a
hermeneutic question. Clause (ii) is narrower than equality of full external
profiles: it constructs identical specified passive behavior with different
additional epistemic and provenance audit values. Since C0(i)'s own mappings
contain $\Aepi^{real}$ and $\Aref$, no claim is made that all three mapped
profiles are identical or cannot separate the pair. C0 is an
\emph{interpretive correspondence plus a behavioral non-identifiability
witness}, not a mathematical unification or subsumption result.

\subsection{Construct validity}\label{sec:construct}

A composite index stands or falls with its construct validity
\citep{oecd2008}: whether the five channels measure \emph{autonomy} rather than
something adjacent. The summary judgment comes first: this paper does \emph{not}
establish construct validity. What is established is instrument-level
attribution coverage (T2, within the instrument family), the C0 interpretive
correspondence under registered readings, and within-substrate discriminant
patterns; the external half---convergence with independent autonomy assessments
on an independent substrate---is a registered, design-frozen program
(\texttt{AFC-EXT-001}), not a result, with numerical SESOI and power inputs
prerequisites before unblinding. The four standard faces:

\paragraph{Content validity.} The channel--respect mapping
(Section~\ref{sec:framework}) is a registered design choice, not a derived
unique decomposition (many-to-many; the gate is deliberately not a channel;
reflection has no separate respect). Its defense is threefold: the attribution
cover (Theorems~\ref{thm:coverage},~\ref{thm:necessity})---within the instrument
family every surgery class has a channel that sees it, not a proof that the five
respects are the unique correct carve-up; the C0 interpretive correspondence
(coordinate-wise registered explanatory mappings on restricted cells, without a
general Feng rank claim); and the rejection map of
Section~\ref{sec:representation}, which prices every aggregation alternative.

\paragraph{Convergent and discriminant validity.} Within the substrate the
channels respond to distinct surgery loci; the blind-witness pattern of
Theorem~\ref{thm:necessity} is an instrument-specificity/attribution matrix, not
by itself a discriminant-validity matrix. Truth/estimation-path agreement is
implementation agreement (Section~\ref{sec:estimators}), not convergent validity
with independent instruments. Convergence with \emph{independent} assessments
(expert ratings, established scales) and discriminant tests against independent
constructs remain the registered external program.

\paragraph{Predictive validity.} The one risky cross-substrate prediction is
that channel \emph{profiles}---not the scalar---predict independent autonomy
assessments and downstream robustness; the falsifier is the external one
(Section~\ref{sec:objectivity}, clause four). Within the substrate the
endorsed-goal utility rate $\bar u$ tracks $A$ under goal capture, dissociates
under epistemic capture and behavioral injection, and is blind to pure
capability erosion (\S\ref{sec:e2checks}): the registered ENV-0 utility is not a
universal proxy for $A$ in these regimes, a testable dissociation structure on
other substrates, not a claim that no scalar functional could proxy for $A$.

\paragraph{Measurement invariance.} Comparability is claimed within a layer at a
fixed instantiation; cross-substrate invariance (same channels, same zero
semantics, transportable scales) is the open program
(Section~\ref{sec:limitations}). Instantiation-relativity is the honest starting
point, not a defect to hide.

\subsection{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

(i)--(iv) \emph{Mathematical scope conditions}, as registered in
Section~\ref{sec:threats}(i)--(v): the margin theorem is proved under
deterministic damage dynamics (the unified stochastic detection$\times$repair
analysis, $P(A<\Amin)<\beta$-type guarantees, and noise-induced escape versions
of C1/C2 are open; the detection lower bound holds only for fixed nonzero
drift in the registered iid Gaussian mean-shift family as
$\alpha\downarrow0$); continuous-action empowerment requires a registered
finite quantization or same-protocol capacity normalizer (general passive
execution proxies have unidentified sign); copula parameter identification
for channel-cluster dependence is pending (bounds without identification in
Appendix~\ref{app:copula}); infinite-dimensional goal spaces require the
Schauder route with weak-* continuity conditions (our four sufficient
conditions are finite-dimensional). (v) \emph{Estimator coverage and the $\Lam$ proxy}: the
event-level sampled carriers of $\Aref$ and $\Lam$ are implemented in the
native joint-log audit (endorsement-event and surgery-context logging from one
batch), but only on the registered fixture SCMs; \eqref{eq:lambda} requires an
explicit SCM and exogenous-noise abduction, so MI ratios and ENV-0 marker
functions are not certified estimators, and the explicit-SCM fixture computes
the estimand only on that fixture. Deployable provenance middleware remains an
engineering gap (Section~\ref{sec:future}); MaxEnt-IRL black-box recovery of
$\Ueff$ is not implemented (the audit uses the registered white-box read; IRL
underdetermination otherwise reports intervals). (vi) \emph{Simulation scope}: tabular substrate
with deterministic dynamics; defense coupling is implemented for the
gate/drift classes (ATK-1/2), with ATK-3--6 coupling a placeholder; ATK-5
and LH-1 curves are calibrations. (vii) \emph{Human side}: triangulation
over published literature only; no new subject data---the validation path
(limited experiments and large-scale online data under the four-phase
protocol) and a minimal construct-validity study are registered next steps.
(viii) \emph{LLM-substrate} instantiation (system prompts, memory, goal
functions as $S$) is future work. (ix) \emph{Execution-channel dynamic range}: on
default ENV-0 the realized empowerment is small against $\log 5\approx1.61$
nats ($\Aexec^{obs}\approx0.01$, passive), compressing the composite by a
nearly constant $\Aexec^{w_{exec}}$; EXEC-1 re-instantiates real dynamic range
($0.234$ vs $0.017$) and replicates the $\delta$-chain to the exact
zero-empowerment signature, so the compression is an instance property, not
definitional; porting rich coupling into the default matrix is a
benchmark-design next step. (x) \emph{Deployment scope of the white-box
route}: as registered in Section~\ref{sec:objections}(vii), the deployable
remainder absent causal-log access is narrow; closing the gap is an
engineering program, not a done deal. (xi) \emph{Sensitivity arms, status}: the
endorsement-quantization and $\kappa$-coarse-graining arms are
refinement-invariant in verdicts, with point readings carrying the reported
bands (Section~\ref{sec:e2checks}, X5); the $\Lam$ counterfactual-model choice
is executed on the explicit SCM with time-stratified Gaussian and logistic
arms, but cross-model point differences exceed the internal $0.01$ threshold,
so only two-model bands are reported. This does not upgrade legacy marker
scenarios or constitute external validation.

\subsection{Future work}\label{sec:future}

Three directions are directly load-bearing. First, execute
\texttt{AFC-EXT-001} on Ingel's RL environments and Albantakis's automata,
including PID, intervention-attribution accuracy, and the reduced comparators;
an LLM transfer arm should compare against EELMA \citep{song2026eelma} and real
deployment measurements \citep{anthropic2026agents}. Second, extend the
fixture-level event-$\Lam$ instrumentation to deployable provenance
middleware. Third, evaluate
C1/C2's normal-form defense predictions and an autonomy-margin dashboard with
EWS and certificate-failure alarms. These are registered research and
engineering programs, not results already obtained.
