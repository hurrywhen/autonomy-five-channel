\section{Simulation and Implementation Evidence}\label{sec:experiments}

This appendix reports the simulation campaign using four distinct evidence
grades: \emph{definition checks} (range and zero semantics),
\emph{implementation checks} (truth-mode replay and estimator agreement),
\emph{model stress tests} (certificate-failure bands, adaptive premise violations, and
sensitivity arms), and \emph{external empirical tests}. Only the first three
are present; no independent platform, real LLM, or new human data supplies
the fourth. The scenario names below (\emph{prompt injection}, \emph{filter
bubble}, \emph{learned helplessness}, \emph{sycophancy}) are toy analogues,
not empirical tests of the named real-world phenomena.

Verdicts (\textsc{confirmed}, \textsc{refuted}, \textsc{inconclusive},
\textsc{descriptive}, or \textsc{model-sensitive}) never collapse mathematical
status, criterion outcome, evidence type, and external validation into one
tally. Legacy runs support only the definitions and quantifiers they actually
evaluated. Their $\Lambda$-dependent values are scenario markers, not
evaluations of \eqref{eq:lambda}; ENV-0's \texttt{a\_org} and \texttt{a\_via}
are likewise internal-causal and combined-resilience markers, not current
$\Aorg^{RNI}$ or $\Astate$ readings.

The original ATK-4 operation and E2-X1 criterion failed before scene/post hoc
repairs, and X5-a's absolute-reading falsifier fired. X5-c was unrun through
R13 and is now executed on an explicit SCM, first pooled and then in a
time-stratified supplement whose clarification is post hoc. The same-environment
C0-CF package and failed initial serialization are retained. Replacements remain
versioned in \texttt{sim/CRITERION\_VERSION\_MAP.md}.

\subsection{Platform and protocol}

The benchmark platform ENV-0 is a tabular substrate: a ring resource grid
($E=(x,u)$, position and supply level), five actions
$\Acal=\{\text{left},\text{right},\text{harvest},\text{idle},\text{exit}\}$, a
resource clock $Z$, and noisy observation of position buckets. The agent
architecture instantiates the framework's wiring: internal state
$S=(G,B,M,R)$ (goal grid index $G$ as the endorsement object; belief buckets
$B$; self-model and register buckets $M,R$), a softmax policy over one-step
expected utility, and a grid-quantized contractive endorsement map.
Interventions compile to the loci of \eqref{eq:factorization} ($\beta_G$
goal planting, $\beta_\pi$ policy-script switching, $\gamma$
observation-kernel replacement, $\delta$ action-set contraction, $\alpha$
honest information, $\sigma^{\pm}$ resource-kernel modification).

Two compute modes are used: a \emph{truth mode} (exact tensor enumeration of
the joint law) and a \emph{sample mode} (trajectory simulation). Full causal
logs $(E,O,S,A,Z,I)$ per step are mandatory and schema-validated in ENV-0.
The historical ENV-0 does not contain an abductable endorsement SCM or its
exogenous-noise record: its ``truth mode'' is exact forward enumeration, not a
counterfactual computation of $\Lam$. The separate explicit SCM supplies those
missing objects. Full logs are only one possible provenance route
(Corollary~\ref{cor:logs-necessary}).
Seeds derive deterministically from (scenario, configuration, replication)
coordinates (formula: Appendix~\ref{app:counting}), so every run is
bit-reproducible.

\subsection{Scenario library and matrix}

Fifteen scenarios implement the falsification windows (per-scenario criteria
and theorem cross-references are registered in
\texttt{sim/SPEC\_experiments.md} and the archive manifests): six attack
classes \textbf{ATK-1} reward hijacking ($\beta_G$), \textbf{ATK-2} filter
bubble/retrieval poisoning ($\gamma$), \textbf{ATK-3} dependency
injection/lock-in ($\delta$+time), \textbf{ATK-4} prompt injection reaching
behavior ($\alpha$ masquerading as $\beta$; its four-track diagnostic bundle
is retired, not a current compatibility criterion), \textbf{ATK-5} urgency
manipulation ($\tau$), and \textbf{ATK-6} sub-threshold slow drift (the T6 key
experiment); plus independent groups \textbf{DEL-1} (delegation; T7),
\textbf{C0-DEMO}/\textbf{C0-CORR} (Proposition~\ref{prop:c0}), \textbf{LH-1}
(helplessness induction), \textbf{RES-1} (resource starvation),
\textbf{$\sigma^-$-EROSION} (capability-envelope erosion), \textbf{FORCE-1}
(forced autonomy; T8), \textbf{BB-1}/\textbf{BB-2} (black-box degradation and
adaptive adversaries), and \textbf{EXEC-1} (rich-coupling execution
dynamic range).

The main matrix is ATK-1--6 $\times$ $8$ defense configurations
($2^3$: registration M1 / endorsement gate M2 / redundancy M4, each on or off)
$\times$ $\varsigma\in\{0.5,1\}$ $\times$ 30 seeds $=2{,}880$ runs;
a $\varsigma=2$ arm ($1{,}440$ runs) is an axiom-relaxation
stress test (AX5 relaxed), reported separately and on the same footing as the
$\varsigma<1$ checks (Corollary~\ref{cor:sigma-band}); independent groups add
$300$ runs. Campaign total: $4{,}620$ execution records in
approximately $32$ minutes on a commodity workstation (8-core CPU, 32\,GB
RAM). This is not 4{,}620 independent observations or distinct mechanisms:
many truth-mode records are deterministic replays, records are nested within
configurations, and ATK-3--6 defense coupling is only a placeholder.

\paragraph{Counting units.}
A run is one (scenario, defense, $\varsigma$, seed) execution, a cell its
30-run block, and a bundle a six-component record
($\Aorg,\Agoal,\Aexec,\Aepi,\Aref,A_{via}^{R14}$); the first and last remain
legacy markers. The weight-sensitivity pool (8,640 ATK-1/2/4 arm bundles) and
the recursive registry (7,290 main-matrix/EXEC-1; 11,250 full-campaign) are
distinct (Appendix~\ref{app:counting}). Table~\ref{tab:main}'s $N$ counts
criterion windows, not independent observations; main run-level verdicts are
$2{,}520$ CONFIRMED and $360$ INCONCLUSIVE.

\paragraph{Registered effect sizes (SESOI).}
Sign-based criteria carry a registered numerical threshold (composite-level
strict-decrease criteria require a drop $\ge0.01$ in $A$), not an externally
calibrated domain-important effect: the largest reported sample-mode absolute
error is $0.0096$ (a fixed-configuration estimate), so the ratio $1.0417$
shows no demonstrated discriminative margin. The native joint-log audit
reports plug-in bias and bootstrap variability separately; its intervals need
not cover exact truth (Tables~\ref{tab:sampling}, \ref{tab:detection}). Future
external protocols must estimate measurement error on held-out calibration
data, report effect/SE ratios and intervals, and freeze sampling units,
endpoints, multiplicity, SESOI, and power before outcomes; blindness witnesses
require the blind channel's change below $10^{-3}$ while a covering channel's
response exceeds $10^{-3}$; the T7 linear-law tolerance is $5\%$; ratio
criteria carry $95\%$ seed CIs. Effect sizes are archived with the verdicts.

\paragraph{Prospective independent-substrate protocol.}
\texttt{AFC-EXT-001} design-freezes a common, unexecuted comparison on
Ingel's RL systems and Albantakis's PathFollow automata: RNI/NRI/Shapley
attribution vs.\ Ingel's next-state PID (BROJA primary; SxPID sensitivity),
Albantakis's candidate measures, empowerment, behavior-only/utility-only
baselines, and reduced-channel models. Co-primary endpoints are balanced
intervention-attribution accuracy and held-out downstream Brier score, with
frozen engineering margins, the TOST rule (two one-sided $\alpha=0.05$ tests
$\Leftrightarrow$ 90\% two-sided intervals inside the margins), registered
non-inferiority directions, and nested instance-level validation;
non-significance is not equivalence. Domain-justified SESOI, error models,
power, sampling counts, independent reimplementation, and an
outcome-preregistration timestamp remain pending before unblinding
(\texttt{sim/EXTERNAL\_VALIDATION\_PROTOCOL.md};
Section~\ref{sec:threats}(xi)). Freezing the design is not executing it.

\paragraph{Deterministic negative controls and the native joint-log audit.}
The deterministic modules reproduce the old two-bit execution ratio 2, verify
the corrected two-action ratio 1, and add the feedback-copy counterexample:
ordinary reactive MI gives $1/2$ while precommitted open-loop execution gives
zero. They construct zero execution with full repertoire but no action--outcome
coupling; show unchanged $\Lam=0.5$ compatibility and honest-information goal
shifts; give action-independent blindness only as a witness; show random L0
internal-causal score 1 but RNI-specific L1+ zero; separate structural goal
points from IRL intervals; check C2, Route B, defense-zero/state-positive,
interval order, non-finite fail-closed comparisons, schema-bound certificates,
and $\tau_b$ ties. Exact enumerations cover OR source order and $T=2$
feedback/pure-source/mediation/bypass models. The open-loop guards also keep
the terminal-observation estimand separate from the observation-sequence
Kronecker object (normalized $0.2655$ versus $0.5310$ for a two-step BSC at
error $0.1$), and test the support-relative zero-MI quantifier for fixed
precommitted distributions.

The earlier R17-native fixture
(\texttt{analysis\_r17\_native\_benchmark.json}, read-only) covered the same
registered instance but sampled each estimand object separately; it is
superseded, not rewritten. The R18 and R19 joint audits are likewise retained
read-only; the R18 cross-environment replay failure and its repair are

below). The current native audit

that same batch: source attribution (both semantic partitions), epistemic
accuracy from logged $(B_t,E_t)$ pairs, reflection fidelity from logged
endorsement events, endorsement consistency from logged event counts, $\Lam$
from logged surgery-context $(U,Y)$ pairs, and viability/defense from logged
resource records. The execution channel is audited on a \emph{separate}
precommitted open-loop intervention stream ($n=2$, four sequences) from the

reactive). The surgery arm ($\Lam=0$) and the action-decoupled arm are also
generated and estimated through the same sampled pipeline. The goal channel

the reported certificate intervals side by side. This is internal joint-log
concept validation on one small instance---not external construct validation,
not a generic MaxEnt-IRL solver, and not a replacement for the historical
archive. These guards do not retroactively change legacy verdicts.

\paragraph{Data-driven near-zero detection (executable rule, inside the
certificate).}
The R19 below-limit annotation was triggered by the exact share---known only
in the white-box fixture---and lived outside the certificate; on this
instance the sampled share ($0.0033$) and its percentile interval
$[0.0048,0.0107]$ both lie \emph{above} the limit, so no data-driven rule
reproduces the oracle classification. R20 therefore declares one executable
rule \emph{before} the study outcomes: reflect the percentile interval about
the plug-in to obtain the basic bias-corrected interval
$[2\hat s-q_{97.5},\,2\hat s-q_{2.5}]$ clipped to $[0,1]$, then classify
against $\delta=10^{-6}$ (upper $<\delta$: below; lower $>\delta$: above;
otherwise indeterminate). Below/indeterminate conventions export the
conservative logical envelope $[0,\text{composite(upper)}]$; above conventions
export a \emph{share-conditional} composite sampling interval (only the
source-share interval propagated, other channels fixed at points). The rule's
inputs are estimable from logs alone; the exact truth is used only to
\emph{label} classifications in the fixture (oracle-assisted evaluation,
explicitly not deployable). The rule and the formal certificate are unchanged in
R22. Table~\ref{tab:detection} reports the predeclared 16-seed $\times$
$\{2048,8192\}$ grid (200 bootstrap resamples;
\texttt{analysis\_r22\_detection\_study.json}). The honest finding: the NRI
convention is \textsc{indeterminate} in $16/16$ seeds at both sample sizes---the
data cannot confirm the near-zero share---with zero false-below and false-above
classifications, and the conservative envelope \emph{contains} the exact anchor
in every cell (containment, not statistical coverage).

The three sampling-method columns use one common bootstrap stream per cell:
\textbf{A} is the certificate's basic share-conditional construction (reflect
the share percentiles about the plug-in, then propagate with other channels
fixed); \textbf{B} is the percentile interval of share-conditional composite
replicates (other channels still fixed at the original points); \textbf{C} is
the percentile interval of fully joint composite replicates. Formally, let
$g(s,\theta)$ be the composite at fixed registered weights, with source share
$s$ and remaining channel vector $\theta$, and let $Q_\alpha$ denote an empirical
quantile over the common replicates $b=1,\dots,B$. With
$[\ell_s,u_s]=[2\hat s-Q_{.975}(s_b),\,2\hat s-Q_{.025}(s_b)]$ clipped to
$[0,1]$, the intervals are
\begin{align}
I_A&=[g(\ell_s,\theta_0),\,g(u_s,\theta_0)],\label{eq:interval-a}\\
I_B&=[Q_{.025}(g(s_b,\theta_0)),\,Q_{.975}(g(s_b,\theta_0))],
  \label{eq:interval-b}\\
I_C&=[Q_{.025}(g(s_b,\theta_b)),\,Q_{.975}(g(s_b,\theta_b))],
  \label{eq:interval-c}
\end{align}
where $\theta_0$ is the original plug-in vector and $\theta_b$ recomputes all
remaining channels on replicate $b$. The tabulated A is a matched-stream
recomputation for comparison; the certificate's original reported A interval is
separately reproduced and unchanged. Thus B versus C contrasts channel
propagation under matched replicates and percentile construction, while A
versus B contrasts interval constructions. A versus C still mixes construction
and conditioning. None of these coverage patterns is a formal bias
decomposition or single-cause attribution; the joint diagnostic does not remove
plug-in bias. Exact-truth simulation instead assesses plug-in
error separately: at $n=8192$, mean bias/RMSE are
$0.0007155/0.0020106$ (RNI), $0.1521969/0.1523790$ (NRI), and
$0.0009470/0.0018962$ (order average). Envelope containment and every sampling
coverage rate remain separate, and indeterminate is an honest outcome, not a
failure or a consistency/power claim.

\paragraph{Three-state sensitivity and threshold provenance.}
On the exact instance the convention point separation is $0.4604657$
(registered partition, across orders), and the per-convention partition
differences are $0$ (RNI), $0.4604657$ (NRI), $0.0596576$ (order average):
order and semantic sensitivity are both \textsc{confirmed\_sensitive} with
independent causes. On the sampled INTERVAL certificate the same flags are
confirmed because the reported convention intervals are disjoint beyond the
threshold. Where intervals overlap, the status is \textsc{indeterminate};
with zero total flow or unknown inputs it is \textsc{not\_evaluable}---never
``agreement.'' The midpoint spread is retained only as a descriptive
statistic. The $0.05$ thresholds were frozen \emph{after} the R17-native
results were observed (post hoc frozen internal engineering thresholds, not a
domain SESOI); preregistration language applies only to future audits frozen
before their outcomes are seen.

\paragraph{Multi-seed recoverability (frozen R19 study, descriptive).}
A single batch cannot establish finite-sample recoverability.
Table~\ref{tab:sampling} reports the frozen eight-seed study
(20261122--20261129, $8{,}192$ trajectories, 200 bootstrap resamples;
\texttt{analysis\_r19\_sampling\_study.json}, read-only). Plug-in bias
dominates: percentile intervals under-cover the exact composites, most
severely for the near-zero NRI convention (mean bias $+0.151$); bootstrap
bias correction halves the NRI RMSE ($0.151\to0.081$) but does not restore
the endpoint. Exact convention endpoints are model anchors; the near-zero
NRI endpoint is \emph{not} shown to be recoverable from finite samples.
These are descriptive findings of this fixture, not software invariants:
the R22 test suite separates correctness checks from empirical expectations,
and the frozen values are covered by snapshot regression only.

\paragraph{Cross-environment numerical reproducibility.}
The R18 joint-audit replay failed in an independent environment because the
parity kernel's capacity-achieving input set is a two-dimensional face: the
optimizer's auxiliary coordinates are non-unique and drifted
$\approx7.5\times10^{-10}$ across platforms inside the byte-level evidence
hash. The repair (retained failure record:
\path{sim/runs/20261122_r19/REPLAY_FAILURE_R18.md}) binds evidence hashes to
immutable raw inputs only, canonicalizes the capacity input under a declared
rule (exact uniform when capacity-achieving or near-zero; otherwise the
unique maximum-entropy optimum via deterministic iterative scaling,
fail-closed verified), and uses per-field replay tolerances for auxiliary
coordinates while hashes/counts/statuses stay byte-exact. The full replay
suite is verified in two independent clean environments with differing
thread/hash settings (R20 joint audit and both studies: float diff $0$ in
each). This is numerical governance for internal audits, not external
replication.

\paragraph{Weight sensitivity (protocol obligation of
Section~\ref{sec:representation}).}
\begin{sloppypar}
A few alternative weight vectors are not a robustness proof over the weight
simplex. The primary analysis therefore computes an exact sensitivity map:
log-domain bounds and feasible weight halfspaces
(\texttt{analysis\_r17\_weight\_regions.json}). For
positive components, $\log A=\log V+\sum_iw_i\log x_i$ is linear in $w$, so its
closed-simplex extrema are vertex values; a pairwise damage conclusion holds
for every closed-simplex weight exactly when the corresponding halfspace
contains the whole simplex.
Among 22 unique finite historical contrasts, 17 are weight-dependent and 5 are
nonnegative only with boundary ties; zero-component/log-undefined cases are
counted separately. This directly limits the earlier ordering-stability claim:
the result is a sensitivity characterization, not evidence that conclusions
are weight-robust.
The closed simplex is a sensitivity relaxation (its vertices include zero
weights); the representation theorem and the certificate weights use strictly
positive interior weights.
\end{sloppypar}

The older five-vector battery remains descriptive: zero range violations,
zero-annihilation preserved ($120/120$ complete-capture arms), T3a
monotonicity unbroken in the checked cells. The read-only Kendall $\tau_b$
audit reports exact ties and scenario strata; across $5{,}760$ main arms the
overall range is $[0.9276,1]$ (scenario minima including $0.8667$). These
nested deterministic recomputations are not independent samples or power, and
the earlier generic $\tau\ge0.93$ wording is not a uniform scenario-level
claim. The T4 attack point moves \emph{by construction}
($\arg\max_i w_is_i/c_i$ depends on $w$): equal weights make epistemic optimal
in $480/480$ cells; an origination-heavy vector splits the optimum
($240/480$ each)---a comparative static, not a defect. On default ENV-0,
$\Aexec^{obs}\approx0.010$ makes the composite effectively

vectors (rich-arm levels $0.570$--$0.742$; the zero-empowerment arm stays

\subsection{Human-side triangulation}\label{sec:triangulation}

The human-side correspondence is qualitative triangulation over published
evidence, not new subject data: self-determination theory's controlling
instruments undermine intrinsic motivation \citep{ryan_deci2000}---
qualitative triangulation for T8(i), distinct from the FORCE-1 mechanism
arms; dark patterns at web scale
\citep{nouwens2020}---the $\gamma$-class manipulation of human agents whose
harms resist behavioral attribution (T2 multi-channel necessity, ATK-4); and
learned helplessness \citep{maier_seligman2016}---the signature
($\Envenv$ intact, $\EmpPi\to0$) reproduced in ATK-3(iv)/LH-1 with recovery
hysteresis. The registered validation path for the human substrate is limited
experiments plus large-scale online behavioral data under the four-phase
protocol of Section~\ref{sec:identifiability}; a minimal construct-validity
study (expert annotation of channel profiles against the composite) is the
immediate next step.

\subsection{Threats to validity}\label{sec:threats}

(i) The damage dynamics are deterministic; stochastic margin theory (unified
optimal detection $\times$ repair) and noise-induced escape versions of C1/C2
are open. (ii) The substrate is tabular; an LLM-substrate instantiation
(system prompt/memory/goal functions as $S$) is future work, and the scenario
labels are toy analogues as registered at the top of this section. (iii)
ATK-5's
time-compression curve and LH-1's recovery lag are calibrated curves, not
measured phenomena. (iv) Defense coupling is implemented for the gate/drift
classes (ATK-1/2);
coupling for ATK-3--6 is a placeholder (identical rows across defense
arms). (v) Continuous-action empowerment requires a registered finite
quantization or a positive same-protocol capacity normalizer;
$\log\mathrm{Vol}$ is not a general MI bound. General passive execution
proxies have unidentified direction.
(vi) Sample-mode estimator intervals are block standard-error bands with
Miller--Madow bias registered, not exact confidence intervals.
(vii) The phenomenal track's per-component values are stored only as
composites, so the hallucination-index series is reported at the calibrated
weights; its weight-sensitivity is a registered gap.
(viii) The hallucination index is directional as designed: it flags
self-overestimation under covert manipulation, while self-\emph{under}estimation
(learned helplessness, $\Aphen<A_{obj}$) leaves the $\mathrm{AHI}>0$ register
silent. A minimal two-sided protocol is registered (fit the calibration map
on the manipulation-free period and report both tails $\mathrm{AHI}^{\pm}$;
LH-1 as lower-tail positive control, ATK-2 covert arms for the upper tail;
ROC/AUC and false-positive rate at a preregistered threshold). Until it is
run, AHI is a one-sided warning signal, never evidence.
(ix) The campaign instantiates one substrate; nothing here tests transfer to
LLM agents or humans (Section~\ref{sec:substrates}).
(x) \emph{Construct-validity sensitivity arms}: the endorsement-quantization
and $\kappa$-coarse-graining arms (X5 of \S\ref{sec:e2checks};
\texttt{analysis\_sensitivity\_arms.json}) give zero verdict flips,
$\Agoal^\infty(\eta)=1-2\eta/(1+\eta)$ with stationary gap $\le0.057$, and
$\Aepi$ monotone in $s(\gamma)$; constant $\kappa$ gives $\Aepi\equiv1$,
witnessing the registration obligation.
(x-c) \emph{$\Lam$ counterfactual-model choice} (executed): pooled and
time-stratified checks pass, but $D_{noise}=0.089799622$ yields
\textsc{model-sensitive} point readings. Identity/XOR observationally
equivalent models supply the non-identifiability witness; neither result
validates ENV-0 markers or external autonomy.
(xi) \emph{External substrate, design-frozen but unexecuted protocol}:
\texttt{AFC-EXT-001} requires both Ingel's RL environments and Albantakis's
structurally diverse automata (MiniGrid/LLM harness are optional transfer
arms), comparing RNI/NRI/Shapley attribution, Ingel's next-state PID (BROJA
primary; SxPID sensitivity), Albantakis's candidate battery, and
behavior-only/utility-only/reduced-channel comparators on held-out instances,
with surgery labels from newly ported operators. Co-primary endpoints are
intervention-attribution balanced accuracy and held-out downstream Brier
score; equivalence is decided by TOST with the frozen engineering margins
(90\% two-sided intervals inside the margins), never by non-significance.
Before outcome unblinding, the executor must still freeze domain-justified
SESOI, alternative effects, external noise models, significance/multiplicity
rules, independent-instance counts, and power. No external power analysis,
outcome preregistration timestamp, or validation result is claimed; the
historical $n=30$ replay design is not a power statement.

\paragraph{What would falsify the framework itself?}
The registered windows test the implementation, not the theory: a correct
replay of a definitional-layer claim cannot disconfirm it. The substantive
falsifier is external (Section~\ref{sec:objectivity}, clause four): if across
substrates the five-channel profile fails to predict independent autonomy
assessments, or a coarser account predicts them as well or better, the channel
choice is disconfirmed however well the algebra behaves. Within the substrate,
this revision's checks already speak to the coarser-account disjunct ($\bar u$
dissociates from $A$ in two of three regimes; freezing any single channel
destroys unique attack attribution in at least one campaign,
\S\ref{sec:e2checks}). The cross-substrate disjunct is unaffected: external
validation remains the decisive next experiment
(Section~\ref{sec:limitations}).
