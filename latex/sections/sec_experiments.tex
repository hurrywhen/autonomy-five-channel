\section{Simulation and Implementation Evidence}\label{sec:experiments}

This appendix reports the simulation campaign using four distinct evidence
grades: \emph{definition checks} (range and zero semantics),
\emph{implementation checks} (truth-mode replay and estimator agreement),
\emph{model stress tests} (certificate-failure bands, adaptive premise violations, and
sensitivity arms), and \emph{external empirical tests}. Only the first three
are present; no independent platform, real LLM, or new human data supplies
the fourth. The scenario names below (\emph{prompt injection}, \emph{filter
bubble}, \emph{learned helplessness}, \emph{sycophancy}) are toy analogues,
not empirical tests of the named real-world phenomena.

Verdicts (\textsc{confirmed}, \textsc{refuted}, \textsc{inconclusive},
\textsc{descriptive}, or \textsc{model-sensitive}) never collapse mathematical
status, criterion outcome, evidence type, and external validation into one
tally. Legacy runs support only the definitions and quantifiers they actually
evaluated. Their $\Lambda$-dependent values are scenario markers, not
evaluations of \eqref{eq:lambda}; ENV-0's \texttt{a\_org} and \texttt{a\_via}
are likewise internal-causal and combined-resilience markers, not current
$\Aorg^{RNI}$ or $\Astate$ readings.

The original ATK-4 operation and E2-X1 criterion failed before scene/post hoc
repairs, and X5-a's absolute-reading falsifier fired. X5-c was unrun through
R13 and is now executed on an explicit SCM, first pooled and then in a
time-stratified supplement whose clarification is post hoc. The same-environment
C0-CF package and failed initial serialization are retained. Replacements remain
versioned in \texttt{sim/CRITERION\_VERSION\_MAP.md}.

\subsection{Platform and protocol}

The benchmark platform ENV-0 is a tabular substrate: a ring resource grid
($E=(x,u)$, position and supply level), five actions
$\Acal=\{\text{left},\text{right},\text{harvest},\text{idle},\text{exit}\}$, a
resource clock $Z$, and noisy observation of position buckets. The agent
architecture instantiates the framework's wiring: internal state
$S=(G,B,M,R)$ (goal grid index $G$ as the endorsement object; belief buckets
$B$; self-model and register buckets $M,R$), a softmax policy over one-step
expected utility, and a grid-quantized contractive endorsement map.
Interventions compile to the loci of \eqref{eq:factorization} ($\beta_G$
goal planting, $\beta_\pi$ policy-script switching, $\gamma$
observation-kernel replacement, $\delta$ action-set contraction, $\alpha$
honest information, $\sigma^{\pm}$ resource-kernel modification).

Two compute modes are used: a \emph{truth mode} (exact tensor enumeration of
the joint law) and a \emph{sample mode} (trajectory simulation). Full causal
logs $(E,O,S,A,Z,I)$ per step are mandatory and schema-validated in ENV-0.
The historical ENV-0 does not contain an abductable endorsement SCM or its
exogenous-noise record: its ``truth mode'' is exact forward enumeration, not a
counterfactual computation of $\Lam$. The separate explicit SCM supplies those
missing objects. Full logs are only one possible provenance route
(Corollary~\ref{cor:logs-necessary}).
Seeds derive deterministically from (scenario, configuration, replication)
coordinates (formula: Appendix~\ref{app:counting}), so every run is
bit-reproducible.

\subsection{Scenario library and matrix}

Fifteen scenarios implement the falsification windows:
\textbf{ATK-1} reward hijacking/shaping ($\beta_G$ gradient, sweeping
strength $\eta$; T3a, T5-goal, AX3/$\Lam$, $\AHI$); \textbf{ATK-2} filter
bubble/retrieval poisoning ($\gamma$; T3a/b, T4 both sides, T2 blindness
witness, T1d sub-threshold arm, script corollary); \textbf{ATK-3} dependency
injection/lock-in ($\delta$+time; C1, C2, T5-exec, EWS); \textbf{ATK-4} prompt
injection reaching behavior ($\alpha$ masquerading as $\beta$; T2 attribution,
retired four-track diagnostic bundle, not a current compatibility criterion); \textbf{ATK-5} urgency manipulation
($\tau$-compression; T5-ref, T6 gate monotonicity); \textbf{ATK-6}
sub-threshold slow drift ($\beta$; \textbf{T6 key experiment}: design rule,
$\Amin$ per-run check, certificate-failure band, $\deltastar$ ablation, registration domain,
detection divergence); plus independent groups \textbf{DEL-1} (delegation
$(\lambda,q)$ grid plus direct-flow and $\rho\neq1$ bracket arms; T7),
\textbf{C0-DEMO} (injection vs verbatim principal
instruction; Proposition~\ref{prop:c0}), \textbf{LH-1} (helplessness
induction; human-side counterpart), \textbf{RES-1} (resource starvation
$\sigma^-$; T5-via, T1b converse), \textbf{$\sigma^-$-EROSION} (capability
envelope erosion; extended T5-via gate, D-via), \textbf{FORCE-1} (forced
autonomy; T8),
\textbf{BB-1} (black-box degradation; \S\ref{sec:identifiability}),
\textbf{C0-CORR} (registered-mapping consistency; Proposition~\ref{prop:c0}(i)), and
\textbf{EXEC-1} (rich-coupling arm restoring $\Aexec$ dynamic range;
T5-exec).

The main matrix is ATK-1--6 $\times$ $8$ defense configurations
($2^3$: registration M1 / endorsement gate M2 / redundancy M4, each on or off)
$\times$ $\varsigma\in\{0.5,1\}$ $\times$ 30 seeds $=6\times8\times2\times30
=2{,}880$ runs;
a $\varsigma=2$ arm ($1{,}440$ runs) is an axiom-relaxation
stress test (AX5 relaxed), reported separately and on the same footing as the
$\varsigma<1$ checks (Corollary~\ref{cor:sigma-band}); independent groups add
$300$ runs ($7\times30=210$ across the seven independent scenarios
(DEL-1, C0-DEMO, C0-CORR, LH-1, RES-1, FORCE-1, BB-1)
plus the $30$-run adaptive-adversary supplement BB-2 and the $60$-run
supplement of this revision ($\sigma^-$-EROSION and EXEC-1,
below). Campaign total: $2{,}880+1{,}440+300=4{,}620$ execution records in
approximately $32$ minutes on a commodity workstation (8-core CPU, 32\,GB
RAM). This is not 4{,}620 independent observations or distinct mechanisms:
many truth-mode records are deterministic replays, records are nested within
configurations, and ATK-3--6 defense coupling is only a placeholder.

\paragraph{Counting units.}
A run is one (scenario, defense, $\varsigma$, seed) execution, a cell its
30-run block, and a bundle a six-component record
($\Aorg,\Agoal,\Aexec,\Aepi,\Aref,A_{via}^{R14}$); the first and last remain
legacy markers. Two pools are distinct: 8,640 ATK-1/2/4 arm bundles
($5{,}760$ main plus $2{,}880$ stress) for weight sensitivity, and the recursive
registry of 7,290 bundles over the main matrix/EXEC-1 and 11,250 over the full
campaign. Appendix~\ref{app:counting} decomposes their $1{,}530$-bundle
difference and gives scenario and seed details. Table~\ref{tab:main}'s $N$
counts criterion windows, not independent observations; main run-level verdicts
are $2{,}520$ CONFIRMED and $360$ INCONCLUSIVE.

\paragraph{Registered effect sizes (SESOI).}
Sign-based criteria carry a registered smallest effect size of interest:
composite-level strict-decrease criteria (T3a family) require a drop of at
least $0.01$ in $A$. This is a numerical decision threshold, not an externally
calibrated domain-important effect: the largest reported sample-mode absolute
error is $0.0096$, so the ratio is only $0.01/0.0096=1.0417$. This threshold
therefore has no demonstrated discriminative margin and is not interpreted as
a domain-important effect. That error is a fixed-configuration estimate, not an
independent error distribution or a standard error for every effect. The R17
native benchmark reports plug-in bias and bootstrap variability separately;
its intervals need not cover exact truth because finite-sample DI plug-ins are
biased. Future external protocols must estimate
measurement error on held-out calibration data, report effect/SE ratios and
intervals, and freeze sampling units, primary endpoints, multiplicity, SESOI,
and power before outcomes; blindness witnesses require the blind channel's
change below $10^{-3}$ while a covering channel's response exceeds $10^{-3}$;
the T7 linear-law tolerance is $5\%$; ratio criteria are reported with
$95\%$ seed CIs. Criteria are thus not bare sign tests; effect sizes are
reported with the verdicts in the archived report.

\paragraph{Prospective independent-substrate protocol.}
\texttt{AFC-EXT-001} now design-freezes a common,
unexecuted comparison on Ingel's Gridworld/repeating-pattern RL systems and
Albantakis's PathFollow automata. It compares RNI, NRI, and Shapley-style source
attribution with Ingel's next-state PID (BROJA primary; SxPID sensitivity),
Albantakis's candidate measures, empowerment, behavior-only/utility-only baselines, and
reduced-channel models. Co-primary endpoints are balanced intervention
attribution accuracy and held-out downstream-outcome Brier score, with frozen
equivalence/non-inferiority margins and nested instance-level validation;
expert ratings remain separate. Non-significance is not equivalence. External
SESOI, error models, power, sampling counts, independent-investigator
reimplementation, and an outcome-preregistration timestamp are still pending
and must be fixed before unblinding. Freezing this design is not executing the
comparison.

\paragraph{Deterministic negative controls and R17-native benchmark.}
The deterministic modules reproduce the old two-bit execution ratio 2, verify
the corrected two-action ratio 1, and add the feedback-copy counterexample:
ordinary reactive MI gives $1/2$ while precommitted open-loop execution gives
zero. They construct zero execution with full repertoire but no action--outcome
coupling; show unchanged $\Lam=0.5$ compatibility and honest-information goal
shifts; give action-independent blindness only as a witness; show random L0
internal-causal score 1 but RNI-specific L1+ zero; separate structural goal
points from IRL intervals; check C2, Route B, defense-zero/state-positive,
interval order, non-finite fail-closed comparisons, schema-bound certificates,
and $\tau_b$ ties. Exact enumerations cover OR source order and $T=2$
feedback/pure-source/mediation/bypass models.

The new R17-native benchmark (\texttt{analysis\_r17\_native\_benchmark.json})
uses one registered $T=3$ reactive SCM with action-dependent environment,
$N/R/I$ roles, resource/defense split, structural goal point and IRL interval,
reflection events, and an explicit XOR endorsement SCM. The operating policy
is closed-loop; execution is audited separately by $n=2$ open-loop sequence
interventions in the same environment. It reports exact truth, seeded sampling
errors, bootstrap/Wilson intervals, an alternative semantic partition, surgery
and action-decoupled negative controls, and R17 co-primary certificates. This
is internal end-to-end concept validation, not external construct validation,
not a generic MaxEnt-IRL solver, and not a replacement for the historical
archive. These guards do not retroactively change legacy verdicts.

\paragraph{How to read Table~\ref{tab:main}.}
There is no aggregate ``confirmed theories'' count. Historical counts are
nested/deterministic evaluations, not Bernoulli trials: T6 separates 360
margin/implementation checks from 60 descriptive CUSUM checks, and the separate
30-run DEL-1 replay has 120 CONFIRMED plus 60 INCONCLUSIVE criterion evaluations
and 30/30 run-level INCONCLUSIVE. T1--T5 rest on their conditional proofs and
analytic regressions, not old scenario outputs outside their registered
premises. Neither the explicit-SCM fixture nor new guards upgrade legacy
$\Lambda$, $N/R$, or combined-gate markers into current-construct validation.

\paragraph{X5-c: executed implementation with model-sensitive readings.}
The pooled first run remains in \texttt{sim/runs/20261116\_r14/x5c/}. The
canonical supplement (\texttt{x5c\_stratified/}) registers $T=3$ and 20,000
base $(X,U)$ draws per noise$\times t$ cell. Four arms share those draws within
a cell, yielding 480,000 cell/context evaluations from 120,000 base draws,
plus 8,000 cross-check contexts. The three $t$ cells are independent
cross-sectional audit-time strata, not a sampled longitudinal trajectory. Its
protocol clarification was registered
after the pooled outcome and is therefore labeled post hoc; thresholds were not
changed to manufacture a pass. Gaussian and logistic arms implement
abduction $U=Z-aX-bJ$, action $J=0$, and prediction with shared $V$.
No-surgery and history-without-output-effect both give $\Lambda=1$, while
$\mathcal H^{surg}=1$ in the latter, demonstrating the history/output
distinction. The pooled C1--C5 criteria pass; the stratified C1, C2, C5, and
observation-equivalence C6 criteria also pass. Integrated and sampled
cross-checks share $(X,Z,J)$; only downstream $V$ is independent. A
\textbf{post hoc, superseded-reporting} paired audit
(\texttt{analysis\_r15\_x5c\_paired.json}) therefore computes the SE of
per-context differences rather than adding two marginal SEs: absolute mean
differences are $0.0077275$ (Gaussian) and $0.0003984$ (logistic), with paired
SEs $0.0064779$ and $0.0056650$. Both pass the post hoc inherited
$\max(4\mathrm{SE},0.02)$ rule, which is not a preregistered criterion;
the overall label is \textsc{post\_hoc\_pass}, not an unqualified CONFIRMED.
R14's saved unpaired \texttt{combined\_se} remains unchanged but is superseded
for current inferential reporting.

Across $t$, weak-arm two-model bands are approximately $[0.905,0.943]$, and
strong-arm bands are $[0.753,0.843]$. The maximum cross-noise point difference
is $D_{noise}=0.089799622>0.01$, so the point-reading status is
\textsc{model-sensitive}, not stable. I therefore report bands rather than
selecting either noise model as truth. The C2/C3 strong-versus-weak ordering
is only a CDF identity, not evidence of noise robustness. Identity and XOR
models additionally have the same fair factual endorsement law and fixed

\paragraph{Statistical reporting and sampling units.}
Deterministic truth-mode evaluations (C1/C2 scaling, T7 fits,
certificate-band boundaries, and the BB-2 bias values) are exact computations. Their repeated
runs establish bit-reproducibility, not statistical power; no
Clopper--Pearson interval or ``$n=30$ power statement'' is assigned to them.
The seeded units are reported separately: ATK-6(vi) has 30 independent random
seeds, each evaluating two drift levels with 200 simulated paths per level;
its duplication across two deterministic $\varsigma$ settings creates 60
criterion evaluations but not 60 independent seeds. The C0 sampled-TV check
and the OU early-warning series each have 30 seeds, while their exact
truth/ODE counterparts are deterministic. Estimator validation uses five
fixed configurations with $2{,}000$--$8{,}000$ sampled trajectories and
reports trajectory-block standard-error bands, not exact cross-seed
confidence intervals. These are the only sampling-based interval statements;
no power claim is made without an explicit alternative effect, significance
level, noise model, and independent sampling unit. None of these checks
establishes external construct validity (Section~\ref{sec:threats}(xi)).

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{T6 (key experiment)}. The design rule separates M3's
subthreshold bound from M1 registration; ATK-6's grid also satisfies its
rollback and per-cycle premises, so it is not an all-$\delta$ guarantee. In
Corollary~\ref{cor:blindzone}'s certificate-failure band, the upper comparison
gives neither M3-only retention nor capture. The separate clipped saturating
implementation does give $D\to1$ with zero registrations on its registered
interval: 30/30 deterministic replays, without binomial or upper-bound-only
inference. The $\deltastar$ ablation reproduces its min terms.
The Gaussian CUSUM arm is only a fixed-$\alpha=10^{-3}$ observation, not a test
of Proposition~\ref{prop:detection}'s different fixed-$\delta$,
$\alpha\downarrow0$ limit. Thirty seeds each simulate 200 post-change paths at
$\delta=0.25$ and $0.125$, generated sequentially rather than as CRN pairs.
Their within-seed scaled-EDD ratio has normal and Student-$t$ 95\% intervals
$1.951\pm0.034$ and $1.951\pm0.035$, respectively. Seeds, ratios, and intervals
are in \texttt{sim/analysis\_cusum\_sensitivity.json}; neither interval is over
individual paths.
\item \textbf{C1/C2}. The recovery-time scaling fits
$\Trec\propto(\delta_c-\delta)^{-1/2}$ with slope $-0.504\pm0.000$
(theory $-0.5$) and $R^2=1.0000$ over 30 seeds; the hysteresis loop stays open
with independently positive thresholds $\delta_{down}=0.0701$,
$s_c=0.2347$; the three early-warning indicators ($\Trec$, stationary
variance, AR(1)) are all monotone approaching criticality.
\item \textbf{T7 (re-evaluated)}. Legacy DEL-1 uses unconditioned flows, so
its $2.0\times10^{-5}$ fit deviation and measured-$\Aorg$ algebra do not verify
the selector-conditional law. Direct flows ($0.671/0.844/0.799$ nats) and
realized $\Aorg$ ($0.0030/0.0017/0.0011$) remain descriptive. The invalid
one-$\varphi$ relaxation is superseded by Proposition~\ref{prop:bracket}'s
separate $\varphi_0,\eta_\mu$ terms; with $\eta_\mu\le477.817$ dominating the
registered arms, both corrected brackets are $[0,1]$ and non-diagnostic. The
separate replay therefore leaves T7 INCONCLUSIVE pending a selector-conditional
estimator and a non-vacuous preregistered test.
\item \textbf{Legacy resilience gate ($\sigma^-$-EROSION)}. The historical
combined gate took the minimum of current resource ratios and defense margins $m_j$.
Across $\kappa_{eff}=(1-\varepsilon)\kappa_0$,
$\varepsilon\in\{0,0.4,\ldots,1.0\}$, the margin region leaves it at 1, the
binding region gives $(0.8,0.6,0.4,0.2)$, and full erosion or
$R_{eff}=0$ drives the old gate and composite to zero. These results remain a
valid demonstration of the legacy $\Ares$-like resilience convention, not of current
present $\Astate$: under \eqref{eq:avia}--\eqref{eq:rdef}, defense erosion
reduces $\Rdef$ but does not by itself zero current autonomy. All five
channels and the legacy $\Lam$ marker are invariant ($\le10^{-12}$), so the
episode also motivates mandatory separate readiness output
(Figure~\ref{fig:p3gate}(a)).
\item \textbf{EXEC-1 ($\Aexec$ dynamic range; $n=1$ open-loop special case)}.
On the compact v1 instance the execution channel reads $\Aexec=0.017$; the
rich-coupling arm (fine-grained position observation, $|\mathcal{O}|=4$,
observation noise $0.05$) lifts it to $0.234$ ($13.7\times$), and the
$\delta$-contraction chain replicates exactly down to the zero-empowerment
helplessness signature of Proposition~\ref{prop:wd-exec}
($0.234>0.218>0$; composite $0.647>0.637>0$; Figure~\ref{fig:p3gate}(b)).
These are single-step open-loop channel checks, not multi-step reactive-policy
validation; the R17-native benchmark supplies the separate multi-step
open-loop intervention audit.
\item \textbf{C0}. Legacy C0-DEMO is retained only as a historical behavior,
accuracy, and marker demonstration: its arms change the environment
distribution as well as belief accuracy, so their composite difference is not
a same-environment source-attribution test. Its $\Lam=1-s(\gamma)$ is not
\eqref{eq:lambda}. The new C0-CF witness instead holds the fair environment,
observation kernel, goal-dependent policy, horizon, and $\kappa$ fixed, changes
only belief updating, and obtains identical full-trajectory $(E^T,O^T,A^T)$
laws with $\Aepi=1$ versus $0.5$. The baseline is nondegenerate:
$\Aorg=1$, $\Agoal=0.5$, $\Aexec\approx1$, and $\Avia=1$. The custom
registered L1 weight vector is explicitly
$(w_{org},w_{goal},w_{exec},w_{epi})=(4/15,4/15,4/15,1/5)$, not the
layer-renormalized equal-weight default. In this no-innovation,
structural-read special case it gives positive composites
$0.8312378961$ and $0.7236346187$; the equal-weight sensitivity values are
$2^{-1/4}=0.8408964153$ and $2^{-1/2}=0.7071067812$. Auxiliary identity/XOR endorsement
SCMs have the same fair factual output and passive action but $\Lam=1$ versus
$0$ by actual abduction--action--prediction. All four C0-CF construction
criteria pass; a failed initial serialization attempt is separately retained.
C0-CORR remains an author-defined KG/Dung descriptive consistency check and
contains no executable Feng mapping or general rank validation.
\item \textbf{T2 multi-channel necessity (matrix-level)}. Aggregating the
per-arm component bundles, the blind-witness table reads: $\beta_G$ is
detected by $\{\Agoal,\Aref\}$, $\gamma$ by $\{\Aepi,\Aexec,\Aref\}$,
$\beta_\pi$ by $\{\Aorg,\Aref\}$ in the legacy labels (the $\Aref$ response
is driven by the marker, not by \eqref{eq:lambda}, so it is not counterfactual
provenance evidence), urgency manipulation ($\tau$)
by $\{\Aref\}$, and $\delta$ lock-in by $\{\Aexec\}$ (EXEC-1 arms). No
single component covers all surgery classes---and every component is blind to
at least one class---over all $7{,}290$ registered bundles, with zero range
violations and zero ``death without attribution'' (every $A<10^{-6}$ bundle
has a zeroed disjunct).
\item \textbf{Hallucination index (T3b; Figure~\ref{fig:ahi})}. Covert
$\beta_G$/$\gamma$ arms produce $\AHI>0$ while components degrade but do not
collapse. The realized ENV-0 gap grows with attack strength, but the theorem
asserts only growth of the lower bound $g(s)$, and legacy $A_{obj}$ contains a

\paragraph{Weight sensitivity (protocol obligation of

The closed simplex is a sensitivity relaxation; the representation theorem

\paragraph{Adaptive adversaries and the black-box bound (BB-2): a
sensitivity calibration.}
The $\Aorg$ rule of Section~\ref{sec:blackbox} is certified for
bypass-semantics exogenous probes, and only the upper end is certified.
BB-2 (30 runs) calibrates what the premise violation costs: an
adversary whose injection timing conditions on the agent's state
($G=\theta^*$; per-state flags compiled through the same $\beta_\pi$
machinery) violates the bypass premise $I^t\ind S^t\given A^{t-1}$ of the
randomized-probe route. Control arms (iid flags,
state-independent script): zero violations of the certified upper bound
($2/2$; and the reference band happens to enclose the truth---a heuristic
observation, not a containment claim). Adaptive arms at
$p\in\{0.3,0.5,0.8\}$: the certified bound's bypass premise is violated in
$3/3$ arms; the naive exogenous plug-in then \emph{overestimates} the
external flow (bias $+0.17$, $+0.39$, $+0.90$ nats, truth-mode exact) and
the bound no longer holds---e.g.\ at $p=0.5$, truth $\Aorg=0.33$ against an
upper end of $0.006$ computed under the violated premise (where the premise
fails, the certificate is void, not refuted). The bias figures are deterministic
exact computations (identical across seeds), so the $3/3$ is not a
small-sample failure: premise violation \emph{deterministically} voids the
certificate in these arms, and the calibration's content is the size of the
inflation, not a frequency. The demonstration is thus a sensitivity
calibration rather than a confirmation: adaptive timing is enough to inflate
the plug-in by $+0.17$--$+0.90$ nats, so the
bypass premise is practically fragile even though the rule is formally
correct within its scope. The demonstrated adaptive arms void this
exogenous-probe certificate; they do not rule out redesigned probes or other
structural assumptions. Trusted provenance, active mechanism probes, and
structural logs remain separate audit routes to be evaluated on their own
premises.

\paragraph{External baseline (X1): failed criterion and post hoc regimes.}
The original E2-X1 criterion required $A$ and endorsed-goal utility
$\bar u=T^{-1}\sum_t\mathbb E[U_{\theta^*}(E_t,A_t)]$ to move together with
$\tau\ge0.8$. It failed under $\gamma$ ($\tau=-1$) and was replaced post hoc;
the failure is retained in \texttt{sim/CRITERION\_VERSION\_MAP.md}, not counted
as confirmation. The replacement description exhibits three ENV-0 regimes:
concordant goal capture (ATK-1, $\tau=1$), discordant epistemic capture and
behavioral injection (ATK-2/4, $A$ falls while $\bar u$ can improve), and blind
capability erosion ($\bar u$ invariant while the legacy resilience gate

Matched $\bar u$ and $A$ pairs with sharply different channel profiles exist
($1{,}644$ pairs at registered tolerances), showing that equal scalar readings
can conceal different attributions. This is still ENV-0, not external
validation.

\paragraph{Channel-count ablation at the accuracy level (X2).}
Under the selected weights and geometric aggregator, log-damage decomposes
additively: $\ln(A_0/A)=\sum_j w_j\ln(x_j^0/x_j)$. The resulting shares are
\emph{bookkeeping components under that aggregator}, not canonical causal attribution
and not ratio-scale effects (Table~\ref{tab:logshares}). They can be negative

channels for origination (ATK-4: $0.80$), goal (ATK-1: $0.59$), reflection

epistemic channel's magnitude share is small ($0.07$ in ATK-2), its
necessity being \emph{attributional}, not volumetric---and the second
ablation makes that precise: freezing any single
channel at its baseline value breaks the unique attack-class identification
of the blind-witness signatures in at least one campaign (freeze $\Agoal$
in ATK-1 and $\beta_G$ is indistinguishable from endorsement tampering;
freeze $\Aepi$ in ATK-2 and $\gamma$ has no unique signature; freeze
$\Aorg$ in ATK-4 and the injection becomes invisible at channel level;
freeze $\Aexec$ in EXEC-1 and a $\delta$ lock-in presents the

freeze $\Aref$ and $\beta_G$,

single-channel insufficiency result of Proposition~\ref{thm:necessity}.

\subsection{Threats to validity}\label{sec:threats}

(i) The damage dynamics are deterministic; stochastic margin theory (unified
optimal detection $\times$ repair) and noise-induced escape versions of C1/C2
are open. (ii) The substrate is tabular; an LLM-substrate instantiation
(system prompt/memory/goal functions as $S$) is future work, and the scenario
labels are toy analogues as registered at the top of this section. (iii)
ATK-5's
time-compression curve and LH-1's recovery lag are calibrated curves, not
measured phenomena. (iv) Defense coupling is implemented for the gate/drift
classes (ATK-1/2);
coupling for ATK-3--6 is a placeholder (identical rows across defense
arms). (v) Continuous-action empowerment requires a registered finite
quantization or a positive same-protocol capacity normalizer;
$\log\mathrm{Vol}$ is not a general MI bound. General passive execution
proxies have unidentified direction.
(vi) Sample-mode estimator intervals are block standard-error bands with
Miller--Madow bias registered, not exact confidence intervals.
(vii) The phenomenal track's per-component values are stored only as
composites, so the hallucination-index series is reported at the calibrated
weights; its weight-sensitivity is a registered gap.
(viii) The hallucination index is directional as designed: it flags
self-overestimation under covert manipulation, while self-\emph{under}estimation
(learned helplessness, where $\Aphen<A_{obj}$) leaves the $\mathrm{AHI}>0$
register silent. A minimal two-sided protocol is registered: (a) fit the
calibration map on the manipulation-free period and report both tails,
$\mathrm{AHI}^{+}=\Aphen-A_{\mathrm{obj}}$ and
$\mathrm{AHI}^{-}=A_{\mathrm{obj}}-\Aphen$; (b) use the LH-1
under-estimation arm as the positive control for the lower tail and the
ATK-2 covert arms for the upper tail; (c) report ROC/AUC and the
false-positive rate of the alarm at a pre-registered threshold. Until
(a)--(c) are run, AHI is a one-sided warning signal, never evidence.
(ix) The campaign instantiates one substrate; nothing here tests transfer to
LLM agents or humans (Section~\ref{sec:substrates}).
(x) \emph{Construct-validity sensitivity arms}: of the three arms registered
in the previous version, the two computationally cheap ones were run
(X5 of \S\ref{sec:e2checks}; deterministic truth-mode re-runs,
artifact \texttt{analysis\_sensitivity\_arms.json}); the third is now executed
on a new explicit SCM with a time-stratified supplement. Each is stated with
its protocol, falsifier, outcome, and evidence boundary.
(x-a) \emph{Endorsement-map quantization} (run): four grid refinements across
all defense configurations give zero verdict flips and convergence to
$\Agoal^\infty(\eta)=1-2\eta/(1+\eta)$, with default stationary gap
$\le0.057$. The absolute-reading falsifier fires at the internal $0.01$
threshold; point readings carry a quantization band, verdicts do not flip.
(x-b) \emph{Epistemic coarse-graining $\kappa$} (run): $\Aepi$ remains
monotone in $s(\gamma)$ at nondegenerate refinements; level shifts
$0.811\to0.778$ baseline and $0.500\to0.250$ deep capture are reported.
Constant $\kappa$ gives $\Aepi\equiv1$, witnessing the registration obligation.
(x-c) \emph{$\Lam$ counterfactual-model choice} (executed): pooled and
time-stratified checks pass, but $D_{noise}=0.089799622$ yields
\textsc{model-sensitive} point readings. Identity/XOR observationally
equivalent models supply the non-identifiability witness; neither result
validates ENV-0 markers or external autonomy.
(xi) \emph{External substrate, design-frozen but unexecuted protocol}:
\texttt{AFC-EXT-001} requires both Ingel's RL
environments and Albantakis's structurally diverse automata; MiniGrid and an
LLM tool-use harness are optional transfer arms, not substitutes. It compares
RNI/NRI/Shapley source attribution, Ingel's next-state PID (BROJA primary;
SxPID sensitivity), Albantakis's candidate battery, the full profile,
behavior-only/utility-only baselines, and reduced-channel
models on held-out instances. Ground-truth intervention labels come from newly
ported surgery operators, not published outcome tables; perturbation/taxonomy
mappings and any auxiliary endorsement SCM are preregistered as new
constructions. Intervention-attribution balanced accuracy and held-out
calibration are co-primary; expert ratings and downstream outcomes are separate
endpoints. Before outcome unblinding, the executor must still
freeze numerical SESOI, alternative effects, external noise models,
significance/multiplicity rules, independent-instance counts, and power. No
external power analysis, outcome preregistration timestamp, or validation
result is claimed; the historical $n=30$ replay design is not a power
statement.

\paragraph{What would falsify the framework itself?}
The registered windows test the implementation, not the theory: a correct
replay of a definitional-layer claim cannot disconfirm it. The framework's
substantive falsifier is external. If, across substrates, the five-channel
profile fails to predict independent autonomy assessments---or if a coarser
account (fewer channels, a single scalar, or a plain behavioral audit)
predicts them as well or better---then the channel choice is disconfirmed,
however well the algebra behaves. Within the substrate, this revision's
checks already speak to the coarser-account disjunct: the scalar functional
baseline $\bar u$ dissociates from $A$ in two of three regimes, and freezing
any single channel destroys unique attack attribution in at least one
campaign (\S\ref{sec:e2checks}). The cross-substrate disjunct is unaffected
by these checks: external-substrate validation remains the decisive next
experiment rather than a formality
(Section~\ref{sec:limitations}).
