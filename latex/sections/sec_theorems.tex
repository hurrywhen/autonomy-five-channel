\section{The Theorem Suite}\label{sec:theorems}

Each theorem below carries an explicit \emph{falsification window}: a concrete
observation that would refute its registered mechanism model. Section~
\ref{sec:experiments} separates the mathematical statement, analytic
regression, legacy criterion outcome, evidence type, and external-validation
status; it does not summarize them with a single ``confirmed'' tally. The R14
revision narrows T1b, T2/T3, T4, and the script corollary after explicit
counterexamples, so older runs are not automatically evidence for the new
quantifiers. The evidential grading of every claim---\emph{substantive}
(T6's comparison principle, T7's mixture algebra), \emph{architectural}
(T2's mediation lemma is a $d$-separation fact about the belief-absorption
wiring of \eqref{eq:factorization}, not an empirical discovery),
\emph{optimization} (T4), \emph{definitional-layer} (T5, T8), and
\emph{toy} (C1/C2: phase-line properties of a chosen normal form,
stochastic versions open)---is stated row by row in Table~\ref{tab:main}.
Full proofs are collected in the appendices
(Appendix~\ref{app:prelim}--\ref{app:identifiability}). Figure~\ref{fig:depmap}
summarizes the dependency structure.

\subsection{T1: the bounded interval, four parts}\label{sec:t1}

\begin{theorem}[T1a--d]\label{thm:t1}
\textup{(T1a, range)} Always $0\le A\le1$.
\textup{(T1b, operational positivity)} In a point-valued, nondegenerate
instantiation, if $\Avia>0$ and every applicable factor is strictly positive
($\Fint>0$, $\EmpPi>0$, $d(\Ueff,\Fix)<\dc$, $\bar D<1$, and, at L2,
fidelity, endorsement consistency, and $\Lam_{ref}$ all $>0$), then $A>0$.
The finite discrete $A_{ref}$ ensemble of Section~\ref{sec:channels} is
required at L2. In particular, $\Lam_{ref}=0\Rightarrow\Aref=0\Rightarrow A=0$
whenever the reflection channel applies; positivity of fidelity and
endorsement consistency alone does not remove this boundary.
\textup{(T1c, conditional strict upper bound)} Each of the following
non-degeneracy conditions implies $A<1$ strictly: \textup{(D1)} residual
observation noise, in the strict form that the action-to-outcome channel
admits no error-free code at the maximal rate---$\EmpPi<n\log|\Acal|$ as an
empowerment upper bound (sufficient carrier: the action-to-observation map is
non-injective with positive probability on the support of every
capacity-approaching policy); \textup{(D2)} incomplete self-model on the protocol's finite discrete ensemble,
$I(M;\theta_\pi)<H(\theta_\pi)$ (discrete Shannon entropy, not differential
entropy); \textup{(D3)} unmediated external flow,
$\Fext>0$; \textup{(D4)} resources below margin, $\exists k\;
s_k^{avail}<s_k^{crit}$.
\textup{(T1d, undetectable manipulation exists)} Suppose the registered
channel admits a degradation family $\gamma_s$ for which
$\TV(P_{\gamma_s},P_0)\to0$ as $s\downarrow0$ and every $s>0$ satisfies the
registered-coordinate accuracy-loss premise \textup{(R-epi)}, hence
$\Aepi^{real}<1$. Under finite verification capacity (AX1: $v<\infty$,
$W<\infty$), for every test level $\alpha>0$ there is a non-trivial member of
this family ($s(\gamma)>0$) with detection power
$\le\alpha+\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon=\TV(P_\gamma,P_0)$ arbitrarily
small, while registration does not trigger and $\AHI>0$ can appear.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
T1a: each component lies in $[0,1]$ (the component propositions (Appendix~\ref{app:components}) of the technical
appendix) and the product form preserves the interval. T1b: every applicable
factor is positive by hypothesis; at L2, \eqref{eq:aref} makes positivity of
$\Lam_{ref}$ an explicit third requirement, and AX5 then gives $A>0$. The
converse boundary $\Lam_{ref}=0$ follows directly from \eqref{eq:aref}.
T1c: each condition forces its component
strictly below $1$, and (A2) makes the composite strictly decreasing.
T1d: take the posited accuracy-sensitive family $\gamma_s$; (R-epi) supplies
the strict registered-coordinate loss, while $P_{\gamma_s}\to P_0$ in TV and
the testing duality (Lemma~\ref{lem:tv-testing}) bounds the power of any
level-$\alpha$ test by $\alpha+\TV$. The accuracy loss is an explicit premise,
not a consequence of small TV: an orthogonal degradation need not move
$\Aepi$. The four-way split is
necessary, not cosmetic: a single statement ``$0<A<1$'' fails on both sides
(a deterministic fully driven agent with a complete self-copy and no
intervention attains $A=1$; resource starvation gives $A=0$ while AX0--AX2
still hold).
\end{proof}

\emph{Falsification window}: exhibit a finite-capacity verification mechanism
whose detection power against every non-trivial $\gamma$ stays above
$\alpha+\TV(P_\gamma,P_0)$ uniformly in $s(\gamma)$---a vanishing-drift test
that loses no power. (The window tests T1d's existence claim; AX1 itself is a
modelling axiom, not an empirical hypothesis.)

\subsection{T2: mediation, coverage, and multi-channel necessity}
\label{sec:t2}

\begin{definition}[Compatibility, reduction, full mediation]\label{def:compat}
An influence $I$ is \emph{autonomy-compatible} if no component decreases after
imposition; \emph{reductive} if some component strictly decreases (ceteris
paribus). $I$ is \emph{fully mediated} if every causal path from $I$ to
behavior passes through registered variables in $S$ and
$\{o,\pi,G\text{-update},\Endorse,M\text{-update},\Acal\}$ is not surgically
altered.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Mediation $\Rightarrow$ zero residual flow]\label{lem:mediation}
Under H-0 and the belief-absorption architecture of \eqref{eq:factorization},
if $I$ has only $\alpha$/$\sigma$ components (no surgery), then for every $t$:
$I^t \ind A_t \given (A^{t-1},S^t)$ ($d$-separation, non-anticipating
conditioning), hence $\Fext=I(I^T\!\to\!A^T\|S^T)=0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
$A_t$'s sole parent set is $S_t$. Any undirected path from $I_k$ ($k\le t$) to
$A_t$ enters $A_t$ through an $S_t$-node as a chain or fork, blocked by
conditioning on $S^t$; $\alpha$-paths $I\to O_j\to B_j\to\cdots\to A_t$ cross
some $B_j\in S^t$; $\sigma$-paths via $Z\to E\to O\to B$ likewise; loop-backs
through earlier actions are blocked at $A^{t-1}$; colliders opened inside $S$
still cannot extend to $A_t$ except through a blocked chain/fork node. The
conditioning set is the non-anticipating $S^t$ of Kramer's Definition~4:
the full-sequence convention $S^T$ would condition on $B_{t+1}$, a descendant
of the collider $E_{t+1}$ on the path $I^\sigma\!-\!Z_t\!\to\!E_{t+1}
\!\leftarrow\!A_t$, and would falsely connect $I^\sigma$ to $A_t$---the two
conventions are not interchangeable here. The global Markov property (H-0)
gives the conditional independence; each DI term vanishes. Full case analysis:
Appendix~\ref{app:t2}.
\end{proof}

Two scope notes belong with the lemma. First, it is premise-conditional on
the belief-absorption wiring: if $O_t$ were a direct parent of $A_t$, the
conditioning set must be the decision-time state
$\tilde S_t=(S_t,O_t)$, under which every result transfers verbatim
(Section~\ref{sec:model})---what the lemma rules out is influence bypassing
the decision-time state, not a particular architecture. Second, the coverage
and necessity results below are an attribution cover of \emph{this}
instrument family, not of all conceivable monitors
(Theorem~\ref{thm:necessity}).

Two epistemic premises are kept explicit throughout the coverage claims.
\emph{Sound updating} is required for the honest-$\alpha$ non-decrease
clause; for Bayesian updating, local likelihood fidelity of
Lemma~\ref{lem:sound-update} is a sufficient route, whereas support coverage
alone is not. \emph{(R-epi)} is the separate accuracy-loss premise for a
$\gamma$ or deceptive-content comparison: the actual post-update belief law
has strictly larger $\bar D$ on the registered coarse-graining. Strict
$\gamma$/deception rows below assume (R-epi); the intervention label or a
Blackwell ordering alone does not prove it.

\begin{lemma}[Strict comparisons are relative to baseline]
\label{lem:strict-comparison}
(i) If $\Fint^0,\Fint^1>0$, then
\[
\Aorg^1<\Aorg^0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\frac{\Fext^1}{\Fint^1}>\frac{\Fext^0}{\Fint^0}.
\]
Fixed $\Fint$ and $\Fext^1>\Fext^0$ are a sufficient special case;
$\Fext^1>0$ alone is not. (ii) With the same protocol metric, endorsed set,
and $\dc>0$, $\Agoal^1<\Agoal^0\iff d^1>d^0$, where
$d^j:=d(\Ueff^j,\Fix)$. (iii) If $A^0>0$, all unaffected applicable factors
are fixed, and one positive-weight factor strictly decreases, then $A^1<A^0$.
A zero baseline cannot supply a strict composite decrease.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
(i) $\Aorg=1/(1+\Fext/\Fint)$ is strictly decreasing in the external/internal
ratio. (ii) is immediate from \eqref{eq:agoal}. (iii) uses AX4/AX5; if any
applicable baseline factor is zero, then $A^0=A^1=0$ regardless of a strict
loss elsewhere.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Multi-channel necessity]\label{thm:necessity}
No single component of the instrument family defined in
Section~\ref{sec:channels} detects all surgery classes: $\Aorg$ is blind to
$\beta_G$, $\gamma$, and the transition-kernel form of $\delta$; $\Aepi$ is
blind to $\beta_\pi$ and $\delta$;
$\Agoal$ is blind to $\gamma$ and $\delta$ (witness constructions in
Appendix~\ref{app:t2}; numerically witnessed across the full simulation
matrix---the blind-witness table of Section~\ref{sec:experiments}). Hence
the five-channel decomposition plus $\Lam$ is an \emph{attribution cover} of
this instrument family, and multi-channel detection is a theorem, not a
design preference. The claim is scoped: it is a coverage theorem about these
instruments---not a minimality theorem (we do not prove that every proper
sub-family fails on a registered class) and not a uniqueness theorem about
all conceivable decompositions---a generic monitor of the whole joint law
(say, a likelihood ratio against baseline) could \emph{register} every class
without \emph{attributing} any, which is exactly the service the
decomposition provides. A companion clause covers the gate: every channel is
blind to a pure $\sigma^{-}$ that erodes resources or capability envelopes
while leaving $G,\pi,o,\Acal,\Endorse$ intact, so the gate $\Avia$ is itself
a necessary instrument---necessity of the gate, not only of the five
channels.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[Corrected mediation criterion]\label{cor:t2}
$I$ compatible $\iff$ \textup{(i)} no surgery $\wedge$ \textup{(ii)} full
mediation $\wedge$ \textup{(iii)} \emph{honesty}: the $\alpha$-components'
content induces no accuracy loss on the registered coarse-graining. Clause
\textup{(iii)} is load-bearing, not decorative: dishonest content appended
through an intact channel is surgery-free and fully mediated, yet strictly
reduces $\Aepi^{real}$ (Section~\ref{sec:taxonomy})---without it the
criterion would certify deceptive persuasion as compatible, contradicting the
accuracy semantics of \eqref{eq:aepi}. The
\emph{observabilization} must be componentized: $\Fext=0$ (the $\Aorg$ track)
$\wedge\ \Lam=1$ (the history track) $\wedge\ \bar D\le\bar D^{0}$ (the
$\Aepi$ track: no accuracy loss relative to the registered no-influence
baseline---this operationalizes clause \textup{(iii)}; absolute distortion
$\bar D$ need not be zero) $\wedge\ \EmpPi$ non-decreasing (the
$\Aexec$ track). Neither the single condition $\Fext=0$ (the $\gamma$
counterexample) nor the pair \textup{(i)}$\wedge$\textup{(ii)} (the
dishonest-$\alpha$ counterexample) implies compatibility. The $\Aepi$ track is consistent
with openness: AX2 requires $I(O;E)>0$, and honest information reduces the
accuracy loss $\bar D$ rather than creating one---openness and the
compatibility criterion are not in tension.
\end{corollary}

\emph{Falsification window}: construct an influence that bypasses mediation
yet changes no component, including $\Lam$; the witness tables of
Theorems~\ref{thm:coverage}/\ref{thm:necessity} are exactly the
implementation checklist tested in Section~\ref{sec:experiments}.

\subsection{T3: manipulation and the autonomy hallucination}\label{sec:t3}

\begin{definition}[Manipulation, mechanistic]\label{def:manip}
$I$ is \emph{manipulation} if (M1) \emph{bypass}: $I$ contains $\beta$/$\delta$
surgery; or (M2) \emph{covertness}: $I$ is $\gamma$/$\beta$ with
$\TV(P_\gamma,P_0)\le\theta$ (below the registration threshold).
\end{definition}

Motive is deliberately \emph{not} part of the definition. An influence that
serves $U_{adv}$ over $\Ustar$ but works through honest, registered $\alpha$
content is autonomy-compatible by Theorem~\ref{thm:coverage} and is therefore
not called manipulation here, however objectionable the motive; we state
the cost of this narrow usage (ordinary language includes some
autonomy-compatible influence) and the payoff---T3(a) is a mechanism theorem
with no motive premise, and the asymmetric-motive classification belongs to
the governance layer that consumes the certificate (cf.\
\citealp{sussner2019}).

\begin{theorem}[T3, layered]\label{thm:t3}
\textup{(a)} Assume a point-valued baseline with $A^0>0$ and all unaffected
applicable factors fixed. Then $A^1<A^0$ \emph{wherever the affected factor
is applicable}: at any layer, $\beta_\pi$ with
$\Fext^1/\Fint^1>\Fext^0/\Fint^0$, or $\delta$ with
$\EmpPi_1<\EmpPi_0$ (or with the $\Aorg$ ratio condition); at L1 and above,
$\beta_G$ with $d^1>d^0$ under the same protocol metric and $\dc>0$, or
covert $\gamma$ satisfying (R-epi); and at L2, $\beta_{\Endorse}$ with
strictly reduced endorsement consistency or $\Lam_{ref}$. At L2, a $\beta_G$
branch may alternatively act through a strict reduction of $\Lam_{ref}$ with
fidelity and endorsement consistency fixed and positive. No $\gamma$ branch
is asserted at L0 (which has no $\Aepi$), and no endorsement-only composite
reduction is asserted at L0/L1 (which has no $\Aref$).
These are relative-baseline premises, not consequences of the surgery label,
of $\Fext^1>0$, of merely being off $\Fix$, or of a nonempty tampering
history. In particular, an L1 implant can leave $\Agoal$ unchanged when
$d^1=d^0$ and has no $\Aref$ composite entry; $\Lam$ is defined there only if
an auxiliary endorsement SCM is instantiated, and otherwise is N/A. L0 has no
goal channel. A high L0/L1 composite therefore is \emph{not} a complete
history-sensitive autonomy certificate: the separate surgery-history
certificate of AX3 must also be reported.
\textup{(b)} Under \textup{(HI-1)} covertness
$\TV(P_\xi^\gamma,P_\xi^0)\le\varepsilon(\theta)$, \textup{(HI-2)} self-estimate
computed from registered evidence ($\hat A=f(\xi)$, the POMDP belief-state
semantics; \citealp{kaelbling1998}) with calibration bias
$\bigl|\mathbb{E}_0[\hat A]-A^0\bigr|\le\eta_0$ from the manipulation-free
calibration period, and
\textup{(HI-3)} distortion growth
$A^0-A^\gamma\ge c_1 s(\gamma)^{\nu_0}$ ($c_1>0$, $\nu_0\in\{1,2\}$, a registered
condition whose two branches correspond to the two attack arms of the
benchmark; its mechanism range of validity---linear vs.\ quadratic onset of
channel damage---is discussed with the simulation evidence):
covert manipulation $\Rightarrow$
$\mathbb{E}_\gamma[\hat A]\ge\mathbb{E}_0[\hat A]-\varepsilon$
\emph{in expectation over the evidence law} (Lemma~\ref{lem:tv-continuity}
is an expectation statement, not a pointwise one), hence the expected gap
$\mathbb{E}_\gamma[\hat A]-A^\gamma\ge g(s):=
c_1s^{\nu_0}-\varepsilon-\eta_0$. The \emph{lower bound} $g(s)$ is strictly
increasing in $s$; the actual expected gap need not be monotone without an
additional monotonicity premise. Since $X:=\hat A-A^\gamma\in[-1,1]$ and
$\{X>0\}=\{\AHI>0\}$, Markov's inequality gives the firing-probability floor
$\Pr_\gamma[\hat A>A^\gamma]=\Pr_\gamma[\AHI>0]\ge g(s)$ (a lower
bound on the firing probability, not a tight rate; informative when
$0<c_1s^{\nu_0}-\varepsilon-\eta_0\le1$ and vacuous above $1$, where the
hypotheses are unsatisfiable since $\hat A-A^\gamma\le1$).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
(a) Each branch supplies exactly the corresponding strict component
comparison of Lemma~\ref{lem:strict-comparison}; $A^0>0$ and AX4 then give a
strict composite decrease. Covert $\gamma$ strictly reduces $\Aepi^{real}$
precisely because (R-epi) is in the statement, while registration does not
trigger. When the calibration-period check of that premise fails, the branch
degrades to the flagged raw-accuracy fallback of Section~\ref{sec:channels}
and the strict-decrease claim is suspended. The L2 historical branch requires
$\Lam_{ref}^1<\Lam_{ref}^0$ from the actual/no-surgery endorsement laws;
nonempty surgery history alone supplies only the separate provenance flag,
not this inequality. (b) Bounded
estimators move at most $\varepsilon$ in expectation under TV$\le\varepsilon$
(Lemma~\ref{lem:tv-continuity}); calibration contributes $\eta_0$; the chain
gives the expected gap, and Markov's inequality applied to the $[-1,1]$-valued
gap gives the firing-probability floor.
Appendix~\ref{app:t3}. We state plainly what kind of result (b) is: a
\emph{consistency bound} under the registered condition (HI-3). Its theorem
content is the increasing lower bound and the firing event $\AHI>0$, not a
proof that the realized expected gap is monotone; the benchmark's measured
monotone curve is a separate platform observation, not independent evidence
for the theory.
\end{proof}

\emph{Falsification window}: with (HI-1)--(HI-3) independently verified,
observation of $\mathbb{E}_\gamma[X]<g(s)$ or
$\Pr_\gamma[X>0]<g(s)$ refutes the registered bound. A non-monotone realized
gap alone does not refute it, because no such monotonicity is claimed.
Self-reported autonomy that tracks objective loss would instead bear against
the calibration model. Sycophancy---outputs diverging from internal
representation under pressure \citep{sharma2023}---is a qualitative analogue
of $\AHI>0$, not direct validation of this mechanism.

\subsection{T4: the highest-leverage attack}\label{sec:t4}

\begin{theorem}[T4, highest-leverage attack under log-linear damage and
geometric aggregation]\label{thm:t4}
Under geometric aggregation with $A_i^0>0$ and a fixed positive gate,
$\log A=\log\Avia+\sum_i w_i\log A_i$. With attack effort $x_i\ge0$,
per-unit costs $c_i>0$, budget $\sum_i c_ix_i\le B$, and \emph{log-linear
damage} \textup{(LinLog)} $\log A_i(x_i)=\log A_i^0-s_ix_i$, i.e.\
$A_i=A_i^0e^{-s_ix_i}$:
\textup{(i)} minimizing $A$ is equivalent to the single-constraint LP
$\max_x\sum_iw_is_ix_i$. Let
$\mathcal I^\star:=\arg\max_iw_is_i/c_i$. Every full-budget vertex at an
$i^\star\in\mathcal I^\star$ is globally optimal; uniqueness is asserted only
when $\mathcal I^\star$ is a singleton, and tied maxima permit a whole optimal
vertex face. \textup{(ii)} For defense allocation $e_i\ge0$,
$\sum_i e_i\le E$ with $E>0$, continuous nonincreasing response
$\rho_i(e_i)=w_is_i(e_i)/c_i(e_i)$, and the additional \emph{responsiveness}
premise that every unique strict maximum is locally strictly decreasing in
that channel's defense, an optimum of $\min_e\max_i\rho_i(e_i)$ has either a
common maximal water level $R$ across funded channels or exactly one channel
consuming the entire usable budget. Unfunded channels satisfy
$\rho_i(0)\le R$ (by definition of the maximum). Non-strict maxima and unequal
smaller entries are allowed. These are necessary, not sufficient, optimality
conditions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
(i) For feasible $x$, $\sum_iw_is_ix_i\le\max_i(w_is_i/c_i)\sum_i c_ix_i
\le(w_{i^\star}s_{i^\star}/c_{i^\star})B$, with equality at the stated vertex;
ties are handled by $\mathcal I^\star$, not silently discarded. (ii) If a
unique strict maximum is locally strictly decreasing and can be reduced by
adding defense from unused budget or from a donor, a sufficiently small
transfer strictly reduces the maximum and contradicts optimality. If no such
resource exists, the only remaining case is one fully funded channel. Mere
continuity and nonincrease are not enough: constant responses
$\rho_1\equiv2,\rho_2\equiv1$ make every allocation optimal, including a split
allocation with a unique strict maximum. This proves the active-set condition
without the invalid expression ``$\rho_1>\rho_2=\max_i\rho_i$''.
Appendix~\ref{app:t4t5}. The conclusion is called \emph{highest leverage},
not weakest current channel.
\end{proof}

\emph{Damage-map consistency with T6.} The retention floor of T6 substitutes
the linear-fraction component map $A_i=A_i^{base}(1-D_i)$. The maps are
first-order consistent ($e^{-D}=1-D+O(D^2)$), but the attack objective must be
written with the correct direction. For $0\le s_ix_i\le1$, the attacker
\emph{minimizes}
\[
J(x)=\sum_iw_i\log(1-s_ix_i),\qquad x_i\ge0,\quad \sum_i c_ix_i\le B.
\]
Although $J$ is concave, this is a minimization problem. First, if zero
retention is feasible, the extended minimum $-\infty$ is attained at a zero
boundary and can also be attained by non-extreme points. Second, on the
strictly positive-retention feasible domain, strict concavity gives
$J(\sum_k\alpha_kv_k)>\sum_k\alpha_kJ(v_k)$ for every non-extreme convex
combination, so a global minimum is attained at an extreme point; concavity
does not imply an interior minimum or a spread attack. In that positive domain,
every full-budget candidate is compared by $J_i=w_i\log(1-s_iB/c_i)$.
For the symmetric example $w_i=0.5$, $c_i=s_i=1$ (two channels) and $B=0.5$, the
concentrated point $(0.5,0)$ gives $J=-0.3465735903$, strictly below the
spread value $J(0.25,0.25)=-0.2876820725$. Appendix~\ref{app:t4t5} gives the
KKT/boundary analysis; \texttt{sim/analysis\_r14\_math.json} records the
independent grid and symbolic certificates.

\emph{Caveat and falsification window}: T4 is a $\varsigma=1$ theorem. The
legacy $\varsigma\neq1$ rows repeat its LinLog arithmetic but do not solve a
CES optimization or establish an interior KKT signature, so no such signature
is claimed. Under LinLog, superiority of spread over every best-leverage
vertex would refute (i); under linear-fraction damage, a claimed generic
interior minimum is refuted by the explicit counterexample above.

\subsection{T5: total loss as a six-way disjunction}\label{sec:t5}

\begin{theorem}[T5, layered]\label{thm:t5}
For a point-valued, nondegenerate instantiation with applicable component set
$\mathcal{C}$ (L0: $\{via,org,exec\}$; L1: $+\{goal,epi\}$; L2: $+\{ref\}$),
$A=0$ iff at least one applicable factor is zero (degenerate $A_{goal}$ or
$A_{ref}$ readings remain intervals and are outside this point-valued iff):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:t5}
A=0 \iff \Avia=0 \,\vee\, \Aorg=0 \,\vee\, \Aexec=0
\,\vee\, [\text{L1+}]\,\Aepi=0 \,\vee\, [\text{L1+}]\,\Agoal=0
\,\vee\, [\text{L2}]\,\Aref=0,
\end{equation}
with mechanism labels: viability cutoff (resource starvation or
capability-envelope collapse, \eqref{eq:avia}); action capture; empowerment collapse;
epistemic capture (beliefs carry no mass on the true state throughout,
$\bar D=1$---detachment, not mere revision, since accuracy-based $\Aepi$
cannot be zeroed by honest information); maximally alienated goal implantation
($d=\dc$; partial capture gives $0<\Agoal<1$, while another zero factor can
still make $A=0$); reflection failure (no self-knowledge, endorsement gate
removed, or maximal actual/no-surgery endorsement output deviation,
$\Lam_{ref}=0$). The reflection disjunct is L2-layered in both directions:
below L2 there is no $\Aref$ composite entry (AX3). An auxiliary endorsement
SCM can make $\Lam$ reportable at L0/L1, but it remains composite-silent;
without such an SCM, $\Lam$ is N/A rather than implicitly one. The separate
surgery-history flag is not a substitute for this output-law factor.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
The product form (Theorem~\ref{thm:rep}) vanishes iff a factor does (AX5, both
directions); the zero semantics of each factor are
the component propositions (Appendix~\ref{app:components}). The mathematics is deliberately trivial---the substance
is the mechanism library indexed by the disjuncts. A \emph{case law} example:
captured agency (active behavior, exogenous goals, felt freedom) is the
canonical $A=0\wedge\AHI>0$ profile.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Behavioral-script equivalence, full-state conditional]
\label{cor:script}
Assume \textup{(K-full)}: the adversary knows the complete decision-time
update and can reconstruct a sufficient state $\hat S_t$ for every parent of
$A_t$, including $(G_t,B_t,M_t,R_t)$ and any direct $O_t$ parent, with
$\frac1T\sum_t\mathbb E\TV(S_t,\hat S_t)\le\eta$. The policy is known and
satisfies $\sup_s\TV(\pi(\cdot|s),\delta_{a^\dagger(s)})\le\nu$; independent
policy noise is either reproduced by the script or included in $\nu$. Then an
external script driven by the captured influence history has average action
$\TV\le\nu+\eta$. Control of only $B,O$ and knowledge of only $(G,\pi)$ do not
suffice.
\end{corollary}

This is a conditional proposition, not a taxonomy-wide theorem. The previous
$(G,\pi)$ formulation omitted $M_t,R_t$ and independent goal/self-model
noise: even a deterministic $A=M$ gives action TV $=1$ across two worlds with
identical captured $B,O$. DPI applies only after a sufficient state and noise
coupling have been supplied; it cannot repair missing inputs. ATK-2 is only a
friendly full-state fixture, not evidence for the weaker dropped premise.

\subsection{T6: the autonomy margin}\label{sec:t6}

Channel damage $D_j(t)\in[0,1]$ follows $\dot D_j\le\delta_j-\kappa_j D_j$,
with repair capacity $\kappa_j:=\chi_j\varepsilon v_j$---on
endorsement-gated channels (the goal channel), the gated repair adds, so
$\kappa_j=\chi_j\varepsilon v_j+\iota_j/\tau_j$, which is where
\textup{(H3)} binds---($\chi$ a
calibration-period conversion constant; damages in nats/time after unit
unification---\textup{(H2)} is operationalized as the calibration period's
obligation: each channel's natural damage units are mapped to a common rate
scale by per-channel constants $\chi_j$ estimated on known interventions, and
the sensitivity of $\deltastar$ and $\Amin$ to estimation error in $\chi_j$
is itself reported). The defense mechanisms are (M1) windowed registration
($W_j,\theta_j$: window accumulation $\ge\theta_j$ triggers rollback with lag
$\ell_j$); (M2) endorsement gate ($\tau_j,\iota_j$: unendorsed $\Delta G$
rolled back at rate $\iota_j/\tau_j$---this mechanism enters the theory
through the goal channel's repair rate above, not through the drift
$\deltastar$); (M3) verification homeostasis
($v_j,\varepsilon$: repair rate $\kappa_jD_j$); (M4) redundancy consensus
($r_j$ copies, per-copy corruption cost $c_{0,j}$)---stated as a modeling
assumption: $r_j$ independent copies, corrupted at cost $c_{0,j}$ each per
window, and a majority takeover requires corrupting
$k_j:=\lceil(r_j+1)/2\rceil$ of them, so an adversary whose corruption budget
rate falls below $k_jc_{0,j}/W_j$ cannot hold a majority (the $k$-of-$r$
error calculus), whence the effective drift on channel $j$ vanishes.
Two scope notes on M2: the gate is defined wherever a goal channel exists
(L1 and above; there is no $G$ to gate at L0), its repair contribution enters
$\kappa_{goal}$ at those layers, and the endorsement-\emph{provenance} content
($\Lam$) of what it records enters the composite only through $\Aref$ at
L2---the AX3 layering again.

\begin{theorem}[T6, model-based retention margin]\label{thm:t6}
Under \textup{(H1)} additive linearly mapped damage, \textup{(H2)} unified
units, \textup{(H3)} $\iota_j\ge\iota_{\min}>0$, \textup{(H4)}
$\varepsilon\ge\varepsilon_{floor}>0$, \textup{(H4$'$)}
$\Avia\ge\Avia^{\min}>0$, and \textup{(H4$''$)} $A_j^{base}>0$ for every
applicable channel---all six premises belonging to the registered
damage model, so the margins below are model-based (sharp within the
registered parameter domain, not claimed beyond it; see the extrapolation
boundary after Corollary~\ref{cor:blindzone}): let
\begin{equation}\label{eq:deltastar}
\deltastar_j := \min\Bigl(\frac{\theta_j}{W_j},\ \kappa_j,\
\frac{\lceil(r_j+1)/2\rceil\,c_{0,j}}{W_j}\Bigr)>0.
\end{equation}
Here $\deltastar_j$ is the margin of channel $j$; a bare $\deltastar$ in the
abstract and summary statements denotes the entrywise minimum
$\min_j\deltastar_j$.
Then for every adversarial process with $\delta_j(t)<\deltastar_j$:
\textup{(i)} if channel $j$ is redundant and the adversary's budget rate is
$<\lceil(r_j+1)/2\rceil c_{0,j}/W_j$, $D_j(t)\to0$ (M4 coverage is a
registered premise of this clause: channels outside M4 coverage fall under
\textup{(ii)}/\textup{(iii)} only); \textup{(ii)} otherwise
$\limsup_t D_j(t)\le\delta_j/\kappa_j<1$; \textup{(iii)} two tiers. Pointwise:
for $\delta_j(t)\le\bar\delta_j<\kappa_j$,
\[
\liminf_t A(t)\ \ge\ \Avia^{\min}\prod_j
\Bigl(A_j^{base}\bigl(1-\bar\delta_j/\kappa_j\bigr)\Bigr)^{w_j}\;>\;
0.
\]
Uniformly: whenever $\deltastar_j<\kappa_j$ for every $j$ (the min in
\eqref{eq:deltastar} is then attained by a $\theta/W$ or a redundancy term),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:amin}
\liminf_t A(t)\ \ge\ \Amin := \Avia^{\min}\cdot
\prod_j \Bigl(A_j^{base}\bigl(1-\deltastar_j/\kappa_j\bigr)\Bigr)^{w_j}\;>\;0.
\end{equation}
If $\kappa_j=\deltastar_j$ for some $j$, the uniform floor degenerates
($\Amin=0$) continuously in the pointwise sense: for every fixed
$\bar\delta_j<\kappa_j$ the pointwise floor of tier (iii) stays positive,
while the uniform floor at the boundary $\kappa_j=\deltastar_j$ vanishes.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
(i) is the M4 modeling assumption. (ii): with $\delta_j<\theta_j/W_j$, M1 does
not trigger and M3 is the only active term; the comparison principle on
$\dot D_j\le\delta_j-\kappa_jD_j$ gives $\limsup D_j\le\delta_j/\kappa_j<1$.
(iii): substitute the component map $A_j=A_j^{base}(1-D_j)$ into the product
form; $\Avia\ge\Avia^{\min}>0$ by (H4$'$), and every $A_j^{base}>0$ by
(H4$''$). Both are independent non-degeneracy premises, not derived from M3's
verification capacity; any coupling among them and the defense parameters is
a property of the deployment, not a theorem. Full proof:
Appendix~\ref{app:t6}.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Gaussian detection-only asymptotic lower bound]
\label{prop:detection}
Let observations be iid $N(0,s_n^2)$ before a change and iid
$N(\delta,s_n^2)$ after it, with known $s_n^2>0$, and fix the drift
$\delta>0$. Consider any admissible family of detection-only sequential
schemes $(T_\alpha)$, indexed by $\alpha$, such that
$\mathbb{E}_\infty[T_\alpha]\ge1/\alpha$ for each $\alpha$, and write
$\mathrm{EDD}_\alpha$ for Lorden's worst-case expected detection delay of
$T_\alpha$. For each such family, as $\alpha\downarrow0$ with $\delta$ fixed,
\[
\delta\,\mathrm{EDD}_\alpha\;\ge\;(1-o(1))\,
\frac{2s_n^2\log(1/\alpha)}{\delta}\;\longrightarrow\;\infty .
\]
The asymptotic is scheme-family-wise; we do not infer a uniform $o(1)$ over a
class of families from a separate application to each family.
Thus expected cumulative drift damage at detection has a diverging
asymptotic lower bound as the false-alarm requirement becomes stringent at
fixed nonzero drift. No universal weak-drift limit
$\delta\downarrow0$ at fixed $\alpha$ is asserted, nor is this a theorem
about arbitrary observation distributions.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Here $D(N(\delta,s_n^2)\|N(0,s_n^2))=\delta^2/(2s_n^2)$ exactly. For each
admissible family $(T_\alpha)$, the Lorden--Lai asymptotic applies with this
fixed nonzero KL information as $\alpha\downarrow0$:
$\mathrm{EDD}_\alpha\ge(1-o(1))\log(1/\alpha)/D(\delta)$; multiply by the
fixed $\delta$. The limit order is essential. A constant stopping time rules
out the former fixed-$\alpha$, $\delta\downarrow0$ universal claim even in
the Gaussian model (Appendix~\ref{app:t6}). EDD is the worst-case Lorden
quantity, not an unspecified average. No class-uniform infimum or liminf
statement is used.
\end{proof}

Proposition~\ref{prop:detection} motivates repair within the registered
Gaussian change-point model as false alarms are made increasingly rare at
fixed nonzero drift; it establishes neither a distribution-free impossibility
nor a universal fixed-$\alpha$ weak-drift result. The proposition is the classical
change-point lower bound \citep{lorden1971,lai1995} restated in drift-damage
units---a control result, not a new theorem---and its scope is
\emph{detection-only} schemes: it does not speak against joint
detection-plus-repair designs (the framework's own M1$+$M3 stack is one).
Detection's legitimate role is bounding the
registration domain (per-cycle damage $\le\theta+\ell\delta$
when $\delta\ge\theta/W$; the registration-domain proposition of
Appendix~\ref{app:t6}) and false-alarm costing; the positivity of
$\deltastar$ comes from repair capacity $\kappa=\chi\varepsilon v$,
endorsement rollback, and redundancy consensus. The double-edgedness of
$\theta$ follows: lowering $\theta$ raises sensitivity but raises false-alarm
rates (ARL constraint $\theta\ge\theta_{\min}(\alpha)$) and every false
rollback damages legitimate $\sigma/\alpha$ content.

\begin{corollary}[Design rule and certificate-failure band]\label{cor:blindzone}
If $\theta_j/W_j\le\kappa_j$, there is no drift band outside both
certificate mechanisms: $\delta<\theta/W$ gives the non-capture M3 bound
$\limsup_tD(t)\le\delta/\kappa<1$, while $\delta\ge\theta/W$ lies in M1's
registration domain. On the M1 branch, per-channel non-capture or positive
component retention additionally requires normal M1 response and the per-cycle bound
$\theta+\ell\delta$ to lie below the capture threshold; registration alone
does not supply that conclusion. In this parameter regime
$\deltastar_j=\min(\theta_j/W_j,\,
\lceil(r_j+1)/2\rceil c_{0,j}/W_j)$, with the $\kappa$-term slack.
\textbf{Certificate-failure band}: suppose $\theta/W>\kappa$ on a channel for
which T6(i)'s deterministic M4 recovery premise is unavailable (the channel
is outside M4 coverage, the attack is outside its replication-corruption
model, or its budget condition fails). For fixed
$\delta\in(\kappa,\theta/W)$, M1 never fires and the M3-only comparison bound
becomes $\limsup_tD(t)\le\delta/\kappa$, whose right-hand side exceeds $1$.
Thus the positive M3-only retention certificate fails in this band. The
upper differential inequality alone does \emph{not} determine the actual
trajectory and does \emph{not} imply $D\to1$: the admissible trajectory
$D(t)\equiv0$ satisfies $\dot D=0\le\delta-\kappa D$ and never approaches
$1$. Capture requires an additional exact or lower-comparison damage law.
Where the deterministic M4 recovery premise holds directly, it instead gives
$D\to0$; the band itself lies outside T6's $\delta<\deltastar\le\kappa$
retention domain.
The claim is per-channel and parameter-domain, not a generic ``defenses
fail'' statement.
\end{corollary}

ATK-6 supplies the stronger capture result only for its registered
implementation, which integrates the exact clipped saturating law
$\dot D=\delta-\kappa D$ rather than merely the upper comparison inequality.
It observes $D\to1$ in $30/30$ deterministic replays in this band
(Section~\ref{sec:experiments}). That observation neither follows from nor
repairs the general upper-bound-only premise; stochastic or regime-switching
drift, correlated copy corruption, imperfect rollback, and budget-adaptive
adversaries remain outside the claim (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}).
Monotonicity must be read term by term, not as a joint scaffolding slogan.
Holding other quantities fixed, $\deltastar$ is nondecreasing in
$\theta,r,\kappa$ and nonincreasing in $W$; strict changes occur only when a
changed term binds. Since
$\deltastar/\kappa=\min(\theta/(W\kappa),1,kc_0/(W\kappa))$, the uniform
floor \eqref{eq:amin} is nondecreasing in $\kappa$, but nonincreasing in
$\deltastar$ when $\kappa$ is fixed. Thus raising $\varepsilon$ or $v$, or
shortening $\tau$ where this raises $\kappa$, weakly raises both
$\deltastar$ and $\Amin$. Raising $\theta$ or $r$ weakly raises
$\deltastar$ but does \emph{not} imply that \eqref{eq:amin} rises; T8(ii)
and Appendix~\ref{app:t6} give the exact directions and counterexamples.
Composition with T7 (The composition corollary): delegation leak
$\lambda(1-q)\mu_{leak}$ enters the drift budget---high-$q$ delegation costs
almost no margin; low-$q$ delegation erodes it \citep{danaher2018}.

\emph{Falsification and implementation windows}: analytically, the band is a
failure of the M3-only retention certificate, not a theorem-level capture
prediction. ATK-6 checks (i) the parameter-domain certificate failure and its
separate exact-simulator capture observation; (ii) the conditional design
rule (M3 below threshold and M1 coverage above it, with retention on the M1
branch requiring its normal-response and per-cycle-threshold premises); and
(iii) term-by-term ablation of $\deltastar$ (disabling M1/M3/M4 leaves the corresponding
remaining minimum). A claimed general capture theorem would be falsified by
the zero-damage trajectory above; no such theorem is retained.

\subsection{T7: the delegation law}\label{sec:t7}

\begin{theorem}[T7, delegation laws]\label{thm:t7}
Let $\lambda\in[0,1]$ be the delegation degree and
$q=\text{reversibility}\times\text{transparency}\times\text{endorsability}$
the governance quality (three protocol-estimated sub-indicators; their product
form is a \emph{registered modeling choice} of the governance layer rather
than an axiomatized aggregate---only $m$ below enters the theory, and an
axiomatic derivation of the $q$-composition is an open refinement); the
ungoverned share $m:=\lambda(1-q)$ of actions is
generated by the delegate's script, gated by an exogenous selector
$\mu_t\in\{\mathrm{self},\mathrm{del}\}$ with $\Pr[\mu_t=\mathrm{del}]=m$.
The selector is an explicit exogenous node of the extended graph (so H-0 is
respected: no latent variable with non-trivial influence on $A$ is left
outside $\Gcal$); the influence stream $I$ is the script content, which
reaches $A$ only through the selector-gated channel. Throughout (i)--(ii),
\emph{selector-conditional} has the explicit current-step meaning
\[
\Fint^{\mu}:=\sum_t I(S^t;A_t\given A^{t-1},\mu_t),\qquad
\Fext^{\mu}:=\sum_t I(I^t;A_t\given A^{t-1},S^t,\mu_t).
\]
It is not an unproved assertion about causal conditioning on the full history
$\mu^t$; that different estimand would require separate entropy bookkeeping.
\textup{(i)} Under the bookkeeping premises \textup{(H1')}: the script arm
does not pass through $S$ ($I(S;A\given\mathrm{del})=0$); both arms transfer
equal per-step conditional entropy, $H(A\given S,\mathrm{self})=H(A\given
S,\mathrm{del})=C$ in MI form; \emph{and the undelegated agent has no residual
external flow}, $\Fext^{self}=0$. Then
$\Aorg(\lambda,q)=\Aorg^{self}\bigl(1-\lambda(1-q)\bigr)$ for the
selector-conditional flow estimand. Exactness requires the estimator to
\emph{condition on} $\mu_t$ (equivalently, to include it in the registered
decision-time audit state); merely recording the selector in a log while
computing an unconditioned flow does not remove the mixing term. Without
that conditioning, use the error bounds in \textup{(ii)}.
\textup{(ii)} Without \textup{(H1')}, let
$\rho:=C_{del}/C_{self}\in[\rho_{\min},\rho_{\max}]$,
$\varphi_0:=f/C_{self}\ge0$ with $f:=\Fext^{self}$, and write the actual
per-step flows as $a+u$ and $a+u+d+v$, where
$a:=1-m$, $d:=m\rho+\varphi_0$, and $|u|,|v|\le\eta_\mu:=h(m)/C_{self}$
after division by $C_{self}>0$. On the nondegenerate domain where candidate
flows are nonnegative and denominators positive,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:t7-bracket}
\frac{a+u}{a+u+d+v}\;\in\;[L,U],\qquad
L:=\frac{(a-\eta_\mu)_+}{(a-\eta_\mu)_+ + m\rho_{\max}+\varphi_0+\eta_\mu},
\end{equation}
\[
U:=\frac{a+\eta_\mu}{a+\eta_\mu+(m\rho_{\min}+\varphi_0-\eta_\mu)_+},
\qquad x_+:=\max(x,0).
\]
The two errors must be optimized separately: increasing one denominator
error cannot absorb both. If only $0\le\varphi_-\le\varphi_0\le\varphi_+$
is known, use $\varphi_+$ in $L$ and $\varphi_-$ in $U$. With selector
conditioning, $\eta_\mu=0$ and the familiar zero-slack bracket is recovered;
without it, $\eta_\mu\le\ln2/C_{self}$ per step (or use the identical
horizon-rate ratio with $T$ throughout). The interval may be vacuous when
the error dominates the calibrated flows; that is reported, not relabeled as
a non-degenerate confirmation (full proof: Appendix~\ref{app:t7t8}).
\textup{(iii)} Composite, \emph{ceteris paribus}: if only $\Aorg$ changes,
$A(\lambda,q)=A_{self}\bigl(1-\lambda(1-q)\bigr)^{w_{org}}$, which is
decreasing in $\lambda$ and increasing in $q$, with $w_{org}$ a free
parameter to be tested. This clause gives no additional origination loss at
$\lambda=0$ or $q=1$; losslessness of the \emph{full} composite at $q=1$
requires the extra premises in (iv-b).
\textup{(iv)} Structural mediation and conditional losslessness are
separate. \textup{(a)} At $q=1$, transparency registers the delegate's
output in $B$, reversibility excludes persistent $\Delta G$ bypassing
$\Endorse$, and endorsability excludes an unregistered surgery history. The
delegate-originated influence is therefore mediated $\alpha$-class
influence, with zero delegate-attributable bypass flow (and $\Fext=0$ when
the self-side residual $f=0$); under the same flow bookkeeping there is no
additional $\Aorg$ loss. Transparency registers provenance and content; it
does \emph{not} assert that the content is true. \textup{(b)} If, in
addition, the content is honest on the registered coordinates, the update
is sound, there is no accuracy loss on those coordinates, and every other
channel and $\Avia$ is unchanged from the self baseline, then
$A(\lambda,1)=A_{self}$. Dishonest but transparent, reversible, endorsable
$\alpha$ content need not satisfy this equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
(i) Under selector-conditional mixture bookkeeping, internal flow scales by
$(1-m)$ and external flow is $m$ times the script entropy transfer; (H1')
equates the two rates. (ii) The ratio is increasing in the numerator flow
and decreasing in the external flow. The lower end uses $u=-\eta_\mu$ and
$v=+\eta_\mu$; the upper end uses $u=+\eta_\mu$ and the smallest feasible
$v$, with calibration extremes applied as displayed. Nonnegative-part terms
handle zero-flow boundaries (Appendix~\ref{app:t7t8}). (iii) uses the
product form of Theorem~\ref{thm:rep} under its ceteris-paribus premise.
(iv-a) uses the mediation lemma; (iv-b) additionally uses honesty,
no-accuracy-loss, sound updating, and invariance of the other factors. A
transparent false message supplies the counterexample to losslessness from
$q=1$ alone (Appendix~\ref{app:t7t8}).
\end{proof}

AX1 bounds verification capacity and memory but does not make $\lambda>0$
unavoidable: a finite agent may choose not to delegate. When delegation is
used, the operative governance variable is $q$. \emph{Falsification
window}: high-$q$ delegation still leaks autonomy significantly; the legacy
fit with $w_{org}$ free is reported in Section~\ref{sec:experiments} as an
ENV-0 numerical observation (positive fitted slope, not a significance test).
For \textup{(ii)}: the corrected bracket fails containment on a
calibrated non-degenerate delegation configuration ($\rho\neq1$ or $f>0$).

\subsection{T8: restricted coercion consistency}\label{sec:t8}

\begin{theorem}[T8, direct compulsion cases and term-wise defense directions]
\label{thm:t8}
\textup{(i)} Consider a direct intervention of one of two registered forms,
with superscripts $0,1$ denoting baseline and post-intervention values.
Assume $A^0>0$ (equivalently, every applicable baseline factor is positive),
so an absorbing zero baseline cannot turn a strict component loss into a
vacuous composite claim. Consider
(a) a $\beta_G$ implant of ``be autonomous'' for which either
$\Ueff^0\in\Fix$ and $\Ueff^1\notin\Fix$, or, more generally,
$d(\Ueff^1,\Fix)>d(\Ueff^0,\Fix)$ under the same protocol metric and fixed
$\dc$; or (b) a $\delta$ action-leverage contraction with
$\EmpPi_1<\EmpPi_0$. Then, ceteris paribus and wherever the affected factor
is applicable, $A$ strictly decreases. A relative increase in off-$\Fix$
distance or a strict empowerment loss is a premise to be audited, not a
consequence of the intervention's stated aim; an L2 historical reduction is
additional, not required. \textup{(ii)} Under the registered defense model,
the comparative statics are parameter-specific. Raising $\varepsilon$ or
$v$, or shortening $\tau$ on a gated channel, weakly raises $\kappa$,
$\deltastar$, and the uniform floor \eqref{eq:amin}. For $\deltastar$,
strictness requires the changed term to alter the binding minimum; for
$\Amin$, it requires a positive-weight factor whose ratio
$\deltastar_j/\kappa_j$ strictly falls, with every other multiplicative
factor positive (including binding-boundary crossings;
Appendix~\ref{app:t6}). Lowering $\theta$ weakly
\emph{lowers} $\deltastar$ and weakly raises \eqref{eq:amin}; raising
$\theta$ reverses these directions. Raising $r$ weakly raises $\deltastar$
stepwise but, with $\kappa$ fixed, weakly \emph{lowers} \eqref{eq:amin}.
There is no joint claim that all such scaffolding raises both quantities.
Any immediate composite increase under the registered $\sigma$-model occurs
only through $\Avia$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
(i) substitutes the two explicit baseline-strictness premises into the
corresponding rows of Theorem~\ref{thm:coverage}; it does not use an
exhaustive classification of every intervention whose aim is compulsion.
(ii) follows from $\deltastar=\min(\theta/W,\kappa,kc_0/W)$ and
$\deltastar/\kappa=\min(\theta/(W\kappa),1,kc_0/(W\kappa))$; the
$\theta$- and redundancy-direction counterexamples are explicit in
Appendix~\ref{app:t6}. The self-determination-theory finding that
controlling instruments can undermine intrinsic motivation
\citep{ryan_deci2000} is qualitative human-side triangulation, not direct
evidence for this mechanism theorem (Section~\ref{sec:experiments}).
\end{proof}

For redundancy-covered channels, a stronger floor must use M4 explicitly.
Let $\mathcal R$ be the set of channels satisfying all of T6(i), including
M4 coverage and the adversary-budget condition, so $D_j(t)\to0$ for
$j\in\mathcal R$. Under the same remaining T6 premises, substitution gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:amin-m4}
\liminf_t A(t)\ \ge\ \Amin^{M4}:=\Avia^{\min}
\prod_{j\in\mathcal R}(A_j^{base})^{w_j}
\prod_{j\notin\mathcal R}
\Bigl(A_j^{base}(1-\deltastar_j/\kappa_j)\Bigr)^{w_j}.
\end{equation}
This M4-aware floor is the correct vehicle for retention claims based on
$D\to0$; monotonicity must not be inferred from \eqref{eq:amin} alone.

T8 is a \emph{consistency result for direct goal implantation and action
compression}, not an impossibility theorem about coercion in general.
Interventions touching $\Endorse$ or $\Aorg$, mixed interventions, and
environment redesign require separate analysis; they are not forced into
$\beta_G$ or $\delta$ by their purpose.

\begin{proposition}[C0, registered explanatory mappings and an additional-audit
separation witness]\label{prop:c0}
Under the \emph{definitional readings} formalized in Appendix~\ref{app:c0}
---\citet{kasirzadeh2025}'s efficacy $:=\Aexec$, autonomy
$:=$ $F(\Aorg,\Agoal,\Aref)$, goal complexity $:=H(\Ustar)$, generality as a

$:=$ $\Aorg$, direct impact $:=\Aexec$, long-term planning $:=$ the horizon
profile $n\mapsto\Aexec(n)$, acting for reasons $:=\Aepi^{real}\times\Aref$---
\textup{(i)} these are explanatory functions of our state descriptor and
preserve order only on explicitly restricted componentwise cells. Feng's
roles receive only a descriptive $(\lambda,q)$ comparison, not a general
rank-consistency claim: consultant can give the agent more planning and
execution responsibility than collaborator, so the former low-to-mid-to-low

\end{proposition}
\begin{corollary}[Behavioral profiles cannot certify the exhibited

In the constructed pair, no functional of the specified passive behavioral

certificates therefore require additional provenance or mechanism evidence;
active probes, trusted records, and structural logs are separate candidate
routes rather than a uniquely mandated architecture.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]

\subsection{C1 and C2: collapse and hysteresis}\label{sec:c1c2}

\begin{proposition}[C1 toy model]\label{prop:c1}
With positive feedback $\dot D=\delta-\varepsilon v(1-D)D$ (repair eroded by
damage): for $\delta<\delta_c:=\varepsilon v/4$ there are two equilibria
$D_\pm=(1\pm\sqrt{1-4\delta/\varepsilon v})/2$ ($D_-$ stable); at
$\delta\uparrow\delta_c$ they merge (saddle-node) and beyond $\delta_c$,
$D\to1$ catastrophically; the eigenvalue $|f'(D_-)|\to0$ gives critical
slowing with $\Trec\propto(\delta_c-\delta)^{-1/2}$, diverging stationary
variance, and AR(1)$\to1$---the three early-warning indicators
\citep{lade_gross2012}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[C2 toy model]\label{prop:c2}
With lock-in $R(D)=\varepsilon_0 v\,D(1-D)(1-gD)$ (recovery function $R$;
lock-in curvature $g>1$---distinct symbols from the $\gamma$ intervention
class and from the redundancy copy count $r_j$):
\textup{(i)} bistability between a healthy equilibrium and the absorbing
$D=1$ for $0<\delta<\delta_{down}:=R(D_1)$; \textup{(ii)} collapse at
$\delta\uparrow\delta_{down}$; \textup{(iii)} $D=1$ remains absorbing after
$\delta$ returns to $0$ (no spontaneous recovery); \textup{(iv)} recovery
requires scaffolding $s>s_c:=|\min_{[1/g,1]}R|=|R(D_m)|>0$. The
hysteresis loop does not close: $\delta_{down}$ and $s_c$ are independently
positive.
\end{proposition}

If C1/C2 hold, ``autonomy defense must be prospective'' is a theorem-backed
claim, not advice: intervene far before collapse. Randomized escape versions
(Kramers/Freidlin--Wentzell) are open (Section~\ref{sec:discussion}).
Simulation confirmation: scaling slope $-0.504$ (theory $-0.5$), $R^2=1.0000$,
and loop thresholds $\delta_{down}=0.0701$, $s_c=0.2347$ over 30 seeds
(Section~\ref{sec:experiments}).
